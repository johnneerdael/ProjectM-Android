# Third-party content

**ProjectM TV Engine** is this project's maintained, extensively modified fork of projectM, based on unreleased projectM 4.2 master, pinned to commit `6f6480746`. It is built from the pinned upstream sources plus the retained ProjectM TV-specific patches in `tools/projectm-patches/`. This is a development snapshot, not an upstream 4.2 release. The engine name does not replace projectM's attribution or LGPL 2.1 licence. The app and the core AAR consumed by Milkbeat use this engine; their release version and artifact checksums identify the distributed build.

## Presets and textures

The [Aurora Ownership SOL/LUNA diagnostic packs](superpowers/evidence/current-patch-proof/aurora-ownership/README.md)
were created by the user's predictor and supplied for the patch0010 experiment.
Their frozen source and image bytes are retained as test evidence outside the
shipping assets; they are not part of the bundled CC0 collection. The capture
records the supplied forecast and actual GPU results separately.

This project distributes the bundled presets (`core/src/main/assets/presets`) and textures (`core/src/main/assets/textures`) under **CC0 1.0 Universal** ([LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt](../LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt)): free for any use, without conditions.

- CC0 covers what this project holds: the selection and curation of the collection and any changes made to it (presets removed, textures converted from DDS to PNG).
- The individual presets and textures are the work of their authors, who released them freely over the past two decades. Like projectM, whose preset repository treats them as public domain, this project distributes them on that basis and does not claim rights in them.
- Authors who do not want their work included can open an issue in this repository; it will be removed.

## Removed content

`Nivush - Spiking Mandelbrot.milk` was removed: it was the only preset whose texture (`colors3`) could not be found. Every remaining preset now has all the textures it references.

Removed in 1.9 because they show text, logos or people: `Suff5`, `prayerwheel`, `winamp_woofer`, `VITRIOL`, `kaite`, `portal1`, `portal2`. The presets that used them were removed as well. `tools/check-presets.py` (run by CI) keeps them out.

The historical 28-patch audit checkpoint carried0025 legacy traversal,0026 angle seam,0027 local RGB clamp and0028 border fan coverage; the circle closure proposal is deferred for its positive overall emulator cost. The shared nonzero file-boolean proposal is deferred outside shipping patches because its negative-wave witness adds measured render cost. Original MilkDrop2 source provenance and limited execution/capture qualification are recorded in the [audit ledger](superpowers/evidence/milkdrop-audit-repairs/README.md); these source-derived GLES comparisons are not Windows/D3D recordings.

At the historical 28-patch checkpoint, the discrete/single custom-dot proposal was deferred after controlled Native4K mosaic timing. Its source-corrected final-output images and cost remain in the [I22 owner packet](superpowers/evidence/milkdrop-audit-repairs/I22/README.md). Live subsequent patch numbers shift down one; frozen identities are preserved.

Current audit patch0028 restores MilkDrop2 border strip-fan coverage by changing only eight triangle indices, based on original `milkdropfs.cpp` (both inspected MilkDrop2 copies are byte-identical). Current float colour/alpha and Native rendering policies remain retained; this is not Windows/D3D pixel equivalence. [M02 source and Native evidence](superpowers/evidence/milkdrop-audit-repairs/M02/README.md) binds the original source attribution, finite controls, repeated captures and versioned predictor contract.

The user approved necessary authored-work cost for I19, I22 and I20 and production qualification of I24 and I16. Candidate patches0029–0033 implement those five changes; the older zero-added-cost decisions above remain historical evidence. The new series uses a simple captured style per shape instance and actual real-fragment UV publication. It adds no API, dependency, preset or release-version change. [Current qualification](superpowers/evidence/authored-fidelity-followup/README.md) distinguishes source controls, selected Native4K images, lifecycle checks and the still-pending isolated combined-cost/PR gates.
