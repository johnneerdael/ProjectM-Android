LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r82 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a centred, audio-deformed circle (built-in wave mode 0, radius about half the model unit, slowly rotating at roughly 0.2 rad/s) is drawn over a heavily fed-back canvas, together with three groups of 32-sided polygons (so, effectively, near-circles): 33 instances, 7 instances and 33 instances. The shape groups move (their centres follow sin/cos of time plus bass_att and several random terms), but their exact paths, radii and colours are unresolved or random in the recipe, so I cannot say where the discs sit. The wave line has only 0.001 authored alpha; it is max-normalised to white (all channels 1), so it is a faint white ring at most, not a bold line. Shape borders are off (border alpha 0). The picture is mostly a field of soft, smeared, grown pattern rather than a dark background with a few clean outlines.

Feedback and motion: the previous frame is resampled every frame with a radial zoom (zoom = 1 + 0.05*bass + 0.065*bass_att, with zoomexp about 1.47), so the image breathes: it expands up to roughly 41% per step at the extreme (a nominal ceiling, not typical) and sits close to 1.0 when bass is quiet. There is no rotation and no drift (dx, dy, rot all 0). The warp and composite shaders both use image-driven advection (the picture steers its own sampling, a flowing, smeared, liquid look, with small offsets of up to about 0.004 UV in warp and 0.02 UV in the composite), and the warp also adds a finer fed-back level and subtracts a coarser blur (band-pass sharpening). That means small seeds should grow into blobs, cells or splatter that spread over the canvas. Trails: on flat content the warp keeps about 95% per frame (half-life near 13.6 frames, roughly 0.6 s) with a small offset; the blur-difference gain is about 0.96, so coverage is probably larger than the flat decay alone suggests. There is a 1% black outer border blended every frame. Overall I expect a swirly, zooming, organic and slightly psychedelic look.

Colour: the feedback and composite stages pass grey levels through unchanged (the composite is a neutral transfer: 0.25 stays 0.25, and so on), and the steady-state cue is grayscale at a low level (about 0.06, a lower bound). So the settled canvas colour has no inherent hue. The actual colours come from the shapes, whose RGB, opacity and perimeter colours depend on bass, mid and treble (bass, mid and treble all feed shape colour and opacity). Their palette is unresolved and not constant, so colours probably shift with the music rather than being one fixed tint. The mood layer's saturation is about 0.38 with about 7-8 effective hue bins, but it is an assumption, not a measurement. Warm versus cool is near neutral (display estimate about 0.05, interval -0.29 to 0.40). I would say moderately saturated, multi-hue and music-dependent, on a grey-leaning base.

Audio reaction: bass and attenuated bass drive the overall zoom, so the image pulses and breathes with the low end. Attenuated bass moves the shape centres. Bass, mid and treble all modulate shape radius, colour and opacity, so shapes should swell and change colour and strength with the music (bands average about 1; per-band gain strength unresolved). The built-in circle follows the right-channel waveform, but its tiny alpha makes it a weak contributor. Several shape positions use random draws (rand) and some threshold/select branches on bass_att, so their placement is random per load and per frame and not predictable from the recipe.

Brightness and activity: brightness is uncertain. The calibrated display estimate gives mean luminance about 0.34 (interval 0.15 to 0.57), a mean frame difference of about 0.077, a flow of about 0.33 screen widths per second, and around 15 global brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 4 to 60). Its activity is about 0.88 (interval 0.49 to 0.99), bands Normal to Intense, so a fairly busy, energetic picture. This is a calibrated source estimate, not a measurement of the actual frames. The mood layer also suggests a medium-confidence "Psychedelic, Geometric, Organic/Liquid, Reactive" character, with speed, flash and acceleration values partly assumed. Strict mood eligibility is empty and flashing is unverified, so I would not assume it is strobe-free.

Randomness, chaos and unknowns: random per load or frame are the shape-centre random terms (and the per-frame random positions). The wave colour is fixed white, and no random-texture or random-redraw elements are listed. Feedback with self-advection and the blur-difference stage is likely chaotic, so the long-term pattern will vary from run to run. Nothing in the recipe is marked output-undefined, and the composite is neutral. Unresolved: shape colours, radii and exact paths, how much of the screen the shapes cover, the actual hue, and whether the faint ring is visible at all.
