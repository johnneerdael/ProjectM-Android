LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r99 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. A mostly black screen with a small, bright, soft-edged knot of light near the exact centre, smeared into a liquid, flowing haze by feedback. The recipe lists three drawn layers. (1) A built-in mode 7 waveform, two parallel audio traces from the left and right channels, centred at (0.5, 0.5) and offset by 0.25 from each other, but drawn with authored alpha of only 0.001, so it is almost invisible (colour: green 0.4, scaled up to full green by max-normalisation; /render/layers/0). (2) Custom wave 0, 512 points whose source positions all sit in a very small box around the centre, x about 0.4995 to 0.539 and y about 0.490 to 0.528, so roughly 4 percent of the screen width and height (/render/layers/1/point_cloud/position_bounds). Its colour is animated and its alpha is gated by time and audio-history terms I cannot resolve. (3) Custom wave 1, 512 points with constant white colour (1,1,1) and a similar time-gated alpha; its position bounds are not reported. I would expect a tight, slightly wobbling cluster or short curl of points at screen centre, not a large figure. A warp shader that makes the image steer its own sampling (self-advection) then stretches and carries that cluster outward over the following frames. That should give soft, fluid or smoky filaments around the centre rather than crisp geometry. There are no tiles, mirrors (symmetry score 0.0) or tunnel and fractal constructions.

Colour. Background is black (flat-field state 0,0,0; steady display about 0, 0.002, 0). The built-in wave would be pure green if it were visible, but it is nearly transparent. Wave 0 colour is a slowly time-varying mix of red, green and blue (sine of time with different rates and phases per channel). That gives broad hue possibilities and not one fixed colour, and the reached colours are unverified. Wave 1 is white, so some of the haze is likely grey to white. The shader warp and composite colour palettes are unresolved in the recipe. The calibrated estimate says moderately saturated (about 0.56, interval 0.28 to 0.83) and roughly neutral between warm and cool (0.05, interval -0.29 to 0.40). So I would describe it as a coloured wisp, perhaps with shifting hues, over white-ish glow on black, with no confident dominant hue.

Motion. Global feedback transport is stationary. Zoom is 1, rotation 0, translation 0, stretch 1, and only a tiny warp of 0.00052, so there is no overall zoom, spin or drift of the picture. All visible movement comes from the shader self-advection (sampling offsets reach up to about 2 UV units in the recipe's envelope, which is an upper bound and not a typical speed) and from the moving wave points. Trails persist: each frame keeps 0.99 of the previous image (half-life about 69 frames, about 2.3 s at 30 fps). Bright strokes therefore smear and fade slowly over a couple of seconds. The calibrated mean flow is about 0.07 screen widths per second (interval 0.003 to 0.19), mean frame-to-frame change is low (0.021, up to 0.06), so I expect a slow-to-moderate, flowing, liquid motion near the centre, not fast sweeping. At 5 seconds after load the knot should already have built up a visible smeared halo, since persistence is a couple of seconds.

Brightness and contrast. The composite stage strongly brightens: a flat canvas level of 0.25 is shown at about 0.76 and levels of 0.5 and above are shown as pure white (/render/composite/flat_transfer). So any accumulated content is pushed toward saturated white and the centre glow should look hot, with a hard contrast against the black surround. The calibrated mean luminance is only about 0.17 (interval 0 to 0.41), because most of the screen is black. The activity estimate is low (0.11, Chill, with Normal also possible). The approximate mood layer says Normal with intensity about 48; these two disagree and I trust the calibrated display estimate more. Brightness jumps are rare (about 0.2 per 10 s, interval 0 to 3.5), and the recipe finds no flash mechanism.

Audio response. The only explicit audio route is the built-in waveform, which follows the left and right audio traces, and it is nearly invisible. The custom wave point formulas read bass, bass_att and a per-point value (value2), so the central cluster's wobble and size probably follow the music with some bass influence, though gain and visibility are unestablished. The recipe finds no audio route to colour or brightness (audio.bass_peak_rgb_effect 0) and no beat switch. I would expect a gentle, continuous shimmer in the centre and not beat-synchronised pulses or whole-screen flashes.

Uncertainty. Alpha of both custom waves depends on time-parity and audio-history terms that I did not resolve, so the cluster may blink or fade in and out; how often and how strongly is unknown. The wave 0 and wave 1 programs include a very large multiplier (100000 times the sample index) inside sines, so point positions may jitter in a pseudo-random way and not trace a clean curve. The position bounds for wave 1 are missing. Final palette, dominant hue and the look of the shader warp and composite are unresolved, and the recipe says reached colours are unverified. Mean luminance and the speed intervals are wide. Mood outputs are uncalibrated assumptions that disagree (Normal versus Chill). Real frames will also depend on the audio actually playing and on native rounding.
