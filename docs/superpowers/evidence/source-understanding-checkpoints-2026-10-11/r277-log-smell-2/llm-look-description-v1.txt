Look: a dark to medium-dark, vividly coloured, organic and splattery picture that shifts around a centre. The calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.16 (interval 0 to 0.38), so the Dark facet is just admitted, with mean saturation about 0.77. Colour is uncertain and probably changes on each preset load: the composite multiplies the picture by four corner shades that cycle slowly (periods about 15, 16 and 20 s), and the hue placement is random per load. Likely leading hues are green (about 26%), pink (19%) and red (18%), with blue and purple present; the steady feedback colour is yellowish, and in the warp red and green persist much longer than blue, which pushes trails toward yellow and orange.

Structure: the drawn layer is a field of 512 small four-sided shapes (squares or diamonds), white at the centre fading to black at the edge, whose positions and sizes follow the audio, so they scatter and pulse. The shader stages then turn this seed into the main picture: a band-pass feedback adds a fine copy of the image and subtracts a blurred one every frame, so small seeds grow into blobs, cells or splatter over most of the canvas. An emboss-like relief stage and image-driven flow make edges stand out like lit relief, with fluid, smeared motion. A thin black outer border (about 1% of the screen) frames it.

Motion: the whole image is pulled toward the centre, strongly toward the edges (up to about 63% per step at the outermost radius, almost none near the centre), and there is no net sideways drift. Expect an inward flowing, tunnel-like drain with moving organic patterns; estimated flow about 0.08 screen widths per second, so fairly slow and drifting. Red and green trails persist for several seconds while blue fades within a few frames.

Audio: bass, mid and treble move and resize the shapes and modulate the composite's colour channels (thresholded). Strength of the response is unknown.

Flashing: probably flickers. No-strobe probability about 0.10, estimated 9 brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 2.5 to 36). Activity Chill or Normal; Normal and Drifting facets likely. Uncertain: actual brightness distribution and which hue leads.
