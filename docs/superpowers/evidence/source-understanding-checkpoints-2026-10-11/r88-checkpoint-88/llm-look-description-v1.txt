LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r88 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a dark, soft, fluid-looking canvas that is mostly smoky haze rather than a crisp drawing. The visible content is a faint cloud of 512 orange dots from a custom wave (wave_0, mean alpha only about 0.07, so each dot is very translucent), positions that are closed-form in time and spread over roughly x -0.18 to 0.53, y -0.31 to 1.15 (so partly off-screen and lopsided toward one side), forming a looping radial/curve-like trace (not proven to be an exact circle). A built-in stereo left/right waveform is drawn as dots at screen centre in a dim brown-orange (0.5, 0.4, 0.3) but with alpha of about 0.001, so it is nearly invisible. Those thin sources are then smeared by the composite shader, which steers its own sampling with the image and its gradients (self-advection, reaction-diffusion/fluid style, with blur-sampled offsets up to roughly half a screen). Expect organic, jelly or nematode-like flowing blobs and tendrils grown from the faint orange dots. How much of that structure is actually visible is unverified.

Colour: the only established hue is orange (wave_0 is 100% orange family, high saturation about 0.99 per dot; the estimated overall palette is warm, about 0.49 on a 0..1 warm/cool scale, so not strongly warm). Shader warp and composite palettes are unresolved; the composite colour mapping is time-varying and shifts slowly, so tints may drift. The calibrated display estimate gives mean saturation around 0.74 (interval 0.47 to 1.0) and a warm/cool value of about +0.21 (interval -0.13 to +0.56), i.e. leaning slightly warm, but monochrome versus multicolour is not resolved. There is no per-load random colour or random texture in this recipe (random_textures and random_redraws are empty or null), so the look should be the same on each load apart from audio and state.

Feedback and motion: the previous frame is kept with a per-frame gain of 0.99 on all channels (no wrap or channel selectivity), a half-life of roughly 69 frames, about 2.3 s, so trails are medium-long and fade smoothly. A constant sampling shift of about 72 px/s rightward (dx 0.005) plus a vertical oscillation of up to +/-72 px/s (time-driven sine/cosine mix) makes the whole image drift sideways and wobble up and down. Zoom and rotation are neutral (zoom 1, rot 0), so no tunnel or spin. A 1% black outer border is redrawn each frame. The composite adds fluid-like self-advection that further smears and swirls content. Motion is nominal and speed is only estimated (p95 around 0.26 viewport widths/s, interval 0.17 to 0.45, partly assumed); the display estimate puts mean flow at about 0.06 screen widths per second (0 to 0.17) and frame-to-frame change as low, so overall slow to moderate and smooth.

Audio reaction: the recipe lists no audio bindings for bass, mid or treble and no audio brightness route. The only audio link is the built-in stereo waveform (left/right samples drive the x/y positions of the nearly invisible centre dots), so reaction is continuous and shape-based, not beat-pulsed. Any audio coupling through shaders or the sample-driven composite offsets is unresolved, so expect weak, diffuse response to the music rather than visible beat flashes.

Brightness: dark overall. Flat canvas levels map through the composite to very dim values at low levels (about 0.005 for black, 0.0005 to 0.001 near mid-grey) with a very bright output (about 0.93 to 1.0) at high canvas levels, so a nonlinear, high-contrast transfer; the steady-state canvas is near black (about 0.01). The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.08 (interval 0 to 0.32), brightness jumps are rare (about 0.02 per 10 s, upper 2.8), and activity is low, probably Chill, possibly Normal. The mood estimate has low confidence: intensity about 13 (very uncertain), smoothness about 91, tags Smooth, Warm, Hypnotic, Organic/Liquid. Flashing is unknown but no strobe mechanism was found.

Uncertain, undefined or chaotic: the exact output colours (warp and composite palettes unresolved), true brightness (a lower-bound steady state versus a calibrated estimate with wide intervals), the speed (assumed rates), how visible the fluid structure is and whether the sampling is chaotic, and how much of the dot cloud is clipped off-screen. The recipe is approximate and not a pixel-exact description. Nothing here is a rendered measurement of this preset.
