The Monochrome facet probability is 0.85, above its 0.69 threshold.

Main structure: two full-screen square shapes (radius about 2.0, centred, stationary) each redraw the previous frame back into the picture at 40% alpha, one at texture zoom 0.45 and one at 0.55. Because every frame copies the last one, the copies nest into a droste or tunnel-like picture of repeated, smaller versions of the image, centred on the screen middle. A faint white border (alpha 0.1) is declared on both squares. The mesh warp adds only a very slight contraction toward the centre (0.049% per step), with no rotation, translation or distortion. The feedback decay is 1, so nothing fades through the warp and the picture can build up and persist. A darkening diamond at the centre removes up to 3/32 per frame, so the middle is probably darker or hollowed.

What actually appears depends on what the recursion amplifies, so I cannot say what shapes are visible or where.
