Approximate look: a bright, busy, warm and swirling picture. Fine patterns spread across much of the screen, twist around the centre and keep changing. Estimated mean luminance is about 0.45 (interval 0.28 to 0.67) and saturation about 0.64, with around 80 percent of the screen coloured. The warm facet is likely.

Seed and patterns: the only drawn element is the built-in stereo rotating trace, rendered as dots, not a line. It circles the screen centre at about 2.3 rad/s with a base radius around half the screen, and its shape follows the left and right audio waveforms. Its colour cycles through blue, green, orange, yellow, purple and a little red (roughly 0.5 hue cycles per second at most), always with the brightest channel at full, and it is fully opaque. The warp shader turns these dots into the main picture. A band-pass step adds fine detail and subtracts a coarse blur, so seeds grow into splatter, cells and edge patterns. Relief lighting (two directions), gradient-driven flow and image-driven self-advection are also present, so I expect an organic, liquid, reaction-diffusion-like texture with embossed edges.

Colour: no single dominant hue. The pooled shares lead with green (about 0.27), then yellow, orange and red, with a little pink and blue. The feedback keeps red and green nearly whole each frame and fades blue quickly (halving in about 3 frames), so the stored canvas tends toward yellow and orange. The source estimate for the steady canvas is yellow, with the dots passing through other hues. The composite is an almost neutral brightness curve that only brightens mid and upper levels and does not add colour of its own. Whole-screen brightness may be high where the pattern fills in. The exact balance is uncertain.

Motion: strong and fast. The feedback map zooms differently with radius (about 0.90 to 1.10 per step, so some parts expand and others contract), and it applies a radius-dependent twist of about -0.08 to +0.12 rad per step. Bass and treble add shifts in zoom, rotation and translation through smoothed routes. Estimated flow is about 0.19 screen widths per second, and frame-to-frame change is high (about 0.085). I expect a spiralling, shifting swirl that lurches with the music.

Audio: bass shifts the zoom and the sideways and vertical translation, treble drives rotation and vertical shift, and both change the composite brightness. All are smoothed (attenuated) bands, and the strength is not resolved.

Flashing: probably frequent flicker, possibly strobe-like. The no-strobe probability is only about 0.02 and estimated brightness jumps are about 30 per 10 seconds (range about 9 to 110). It is not gentle, and the energy is likely Intense or the upper Normal band.
