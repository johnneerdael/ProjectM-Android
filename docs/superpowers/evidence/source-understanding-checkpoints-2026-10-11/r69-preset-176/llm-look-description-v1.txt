LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r69 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a feedback-driven scene of dots and thin traces. The image is built from several point/line layers drawn on top of a continuously recycled previous frame: a built-in stereo waveform drawn as dots (an X-Y trace of the left and right channels, centred), four custom 512-point waves (wave_0..wave_3, two of them with procedurally moving points), and one large five-sided polygon (radius about 1.07 in normalised units, so it is clipped by the screen edges and may read as a big angular wedge or fill more than as a small shape). The composite also lets the image steer its own sampling through a blurred copy of itself (self-advection), so the dots and strokes likely smear and flow into fluid, swirling structure rather than staying crisp. Nothing in the recipe says these are independent tracked particles; they are authored point positions recomputed each frame. There is no mirroring or kaleidoscope symmetry (symmetry score 0).

Colour: the drawing colours are mostly dynamic (the custom waves and the shape have no fixed source colour, and the recipe lists only broad hue possibilities), so which hues actually appear is unresolved. The built-in wave has a possible orange source. The only firm palette cue is the composite: flat canvas levels are displayed through a pale, warm-to-white ramp (mid-grey maps to near white, dim content tends to a pale cream or yellow, bright content to white or a slightly bluish white), and that ramp drifts slowly with time (time_varying is true, with the largest spread at the dim level, mostly in blue). The steady-state hue family is yellow, so I expect a washed, light, pastel-leaning look with cream and yellow highlights rather than deep saturated colour. The calibrated estimate says moderate saturation (about 0.52, interval 0.25 to 0.79) and a warm lean (0.25, interval from slightly cool to clearly warm). Bass also modulates the warp's red, green and blue, so the tint likely shifts with the beat, with strength unknown.

Feedback and motion: each frame the previous image is multiplied by about 0.83, so plain trails fade quickly (half-life about 3.6 frames, roughly 0.13 s) and flat areas decay to black. The warp expands the image from the centre by 0.2% per frame and applies a rotation whose range is given as -0.11 to 0.11 rad per step. That rotation is a range, not a known value, and the actual rotation is not resolved (the recipe lists no audio gain for it). Drift is zero. So content slowly zooms outward with a twist; short fading trails and advection smearing are likelier than long persistent tunnels. The polygon's centre and rotation are driven by bass, mid and treble (smoothed and raw), so it should jump or swing around with the music, with a path the recipe calls unknown. Estimated overall motion is moderate: frame-to-frame difference about 0.04 (interval 0.008 to 0.084) and flow about 0.13 screen widths per second (interval 0.04 to 0.28).

Audio reaction: the built-in dot trace follows the waveform continuously, so it moves with the sound at all times. The polygon's position and rotation respond to bass, mid and attenuated bass, mid and treble, but all routes are threshold or clamped and no linear gains are given, so visible strength is unknown. Bass affects the warp's colour channels. There is no per-frame random redraw and no per-load random branch listed, so I do not expect the look to be random per load; the recipe lists no such element.

Brightness and chaos: mean luminance is estimated at about 0.19 with a very wide interval (0.002 to 0.43), so this could look anywhere from nearly dark to moderately bright. Because the composite lifts dim content toward cream or white, content that does appear may look bright against black. Brightness jumps are estimated at about 1.4 per 10 s, interval 0 to 7.8, so occasional pulses are possible but unconfirmed and I cannot rule out strobing. Estimated activity is Normal (0.37), but Chill and Intense are both possible. The self-advection and the point waves could make the picture somewhat chaotic and fluid, but the recipe gives no measured amount. This is an approximate description from source only, not a rendered measurement.
