Approximate look (source-only, uncertain): a fairly dim to medium-bright, blue-and-pink tunnel or kaleidoscope pattern that keeps turning and rising. A single thick waveform line seeds it, and the composite folds that line into a mirrored, tiled, log-polar picture.

Structure: the only drawn element is a built-in angled waveform trace (mode 6, thick, not additive) anchored at x 0.9 and y 0.5, on the right side of the screen. It follows the left audio channel. Its colour changes over time (red 0.2 to 0.8, green 0 to 1, blue 0.2 to 0.8). Its alpha is above 1, so it is fully opaque. Thin dark borders, 1% of the screen each, inner and outer, at half alpha, frame the picture. The composite shader does most of the work: a mirror fold, a rotation, a polar mapping with log radius (a tunnel candidate), mirrored tiling, and image-driven self-advection. So the single line is probably repeated into symmetric, ring-like or kaleidoscopic arms around the centre. How strong the tunnel and symmetry look on screen is conditional.

Colour and brightness: the canvas is near black when empty, and the composite passes flat levels through unchanged, so the picture is not tinted by a background colour. Colours come from the line and the shaders. The calibrated hue shares lead with blue (about 26 to 30%), then pink (about 19%), with green, red and orange each around 10 to 13%. Blue is likely the leading hue, ahead of pink. Calibrated luminance is about 0.21 (0.04 to 0.43), saturation about 0.36, and about 60% of the screen is coloured. The Dark and Bright facets are both not admitted, so it should look mid-dim and moderately coloured, not pastel and not neon. The Balanced facet is admitted (0.74).

Motion: continuous. The feedback map rotates the image 0.02 rad per step and shifts it upward by 0.008 UV per step (about 115 px/s), with a faint contraction toward the centre (about 0.02% per step) and a slight horizontal stretch of 1%. Retention is about 1 or a little above it, so content hardly fades. Trails are very long and accumulate, which suggests a dense, built-up, slowly spiralling pattern. The estimated flow is fast, about 0.3 screen widths per s.

Audio and flashing: bass, mid and treble drive the colour of both shaders, mostly through thresholded or clamped paths, so colours shift with the music. The estimate is about 1 global brightness jump per 10 s (range 0 to 6), and the no-strobe probability is 0.77, so occasional pulses are likely but there is no strobing. The Normal facet is admitted. The activity estimate is Intense (0.77) with a very wide uncertainty interval (0.17 to 0.95). Uncertain: how clear the tunnel is, and the exact colour balance.
