Likely a dim, organic, liquid-looking picture in which coloured blobs and cells grow and flow across a dark ground. Calibrated mean luminance is low (about 0.13, interval 0 to 0.34) and the dark facet is admitted (0.65). Brightness comes in patches, and the middle is not a dull wash. Saturation is about 0.5 and roughly 43% of the screen is coloured, both with wide intervals.

Colours: a mixed palette with no dominant hue. Green leads (about 0.35), then blue (0.15) and pink (0.14), with small shares of orange, red and yellow. Treat it as green, blue and pink. The built-in dot trace cycles slowly through many hues (raw cycle about 0.27 per second), but its alpha is about 0.001, so it should contribute little. The two custom waves have green-leaning authored colours (a dark green and a bright mint green); their exact visible colours are unresolved. The filled shapes are white tint multiplying a copy of the previous frame, so they add no colour of their own.

Structure: no mirror or kaleidoscope. The warp does band-pass sharpening (a finer fed-back level added, a coarser blur subtracted), so small seeds grow into blobs, cells and splatter that spread over the canvas. Image-driven advection lets the picture steer its own sampling, which should look smeared and fluid. Seeds come from three custom point waves whose positions follow three randomly redrawn "ripple" states, so their placement is a random draw and changes over time. There are also three frame-copy shapes. One is a nearly screen-filling, 100-sided circle that turns at 0.25 rad/s and copies the previous frame at 1.15x zoom. The other two are five-sided, mostly screen-filling shapes with moving centres, copying the frame at 0.78x zoom. These add nested, spiralling echoes and a faint swirl. A very thin dark red-tinted edge blend (alpha 0.06) darkens the screen border slightly.

Motion: a slow outward zoom (0.05% per frame) with a small diagonal drift (about 22 px/s towards the upper left), but the main movement is the sharpening and advection feedback plus the rotating copies. Trails are short, a half-life of about 14 frames (roughly 0.5 s). Calibrated flow is moderate (about 0.18 screen widths per second). Still is rejected and drifting is uncertain.

The stereo dot trace follows the waveform, but it is nearly invisible.

Expect noticeable flicker or pulsing with the music, and do not call it strobe-free. Activity: Normal is most likely. Uncertain: where the ripples land and how bright the growing blobs get.
