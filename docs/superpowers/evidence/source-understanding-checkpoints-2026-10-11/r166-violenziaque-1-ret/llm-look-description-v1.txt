Predicted look: a bright, pale, mostly grey-white screen filled with soft, smeared, liquid-looking texture, with very little crisp drawn geometry.

Brightness and colour: The recipe lists no drawn shapes, waves or points, only a warp shader and a composite shader. The feedback steady state is greyscale (hue_family "grayscale"). Canvas level is about 0.40 and the composite pushes it to roughly 0.90 display grey (display_local_rgb about 0.91/0.90/0.90). Flat areas therefore read as near-white or light grey, not dark. The calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.54 (0.35 to 0.77), so overall brightness is medium to bright. The composite's detail response is pure grey (saturation 0), so I do not expect the composite itself to add colour. The calibrated saturation estimate (0.43, interval 0.15 to 0.70) disagrees with that. Colour could come from the sampled textures or the blur, but the recipe names no palette ("palette unresolved"). My best guess is a pale, mostly neutral picture with possibly faint tints. Warm/cool is unknown.

Structure and motion: Both shaders use the blurred copy of the image to steer their own sampling coordinates (self-advection). This should give flowing, smeared, fluid-like patterns that drift and smudge. The warp offsets are modest, up to about 0.08 to 0.28 in UV for one tap. The mesh controls are neutral: zoom 1, rotation 0, no translation, centre at (0.5, 0.5). So there is no global spin, zoom or tunnel. Motion is local swirling and smearing. Estimated flow is about 0.09 screen widths per second, which is gentle to moderate. Trails are very short, with a half-life of about 1.3 frames, so old content is replaced almost at once.

Audio: No audio bindings are found, so no established beat or band reaction. Any response would be indirect.

Flashing: No flash mechanism is found, but this is unproven. The calibrated estimate predicts about 10 global brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 2.5 to 39), which is very uncertain. Treat strobing as unknown, probably low to moderate.

Main uncertainties: what actually seeds the texture, since nothing is drawn; the real colour; and the visible contrast.
