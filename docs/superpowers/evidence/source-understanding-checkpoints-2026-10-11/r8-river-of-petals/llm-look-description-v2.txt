LLM look description from the SDK recipe (before capture, r8 v2):
A reaction-diffusion feedback field. The green channel sharpens itself every frame
(g + 0.1*(g - blur1(g)) - 0.015), so thin seeds grow into dense, worm-like Turing strands that
fill the screen within a few seconds. The red channel is pushed along blur-gradient flow with a
moving per-pixel dither, which turns into soft red glows. Blue decays and stays faint. The seeds
are a 3D star field of orange points (custom dot waves whose depth advances with bass_att² and
wraps every 2 units), drawn in perspective as a petal-like cloud, plus thin blue line waves.
The composite takes the max of the picture and its left-right mirror and adds blur glows, giving
a left-right symmetric, bright, saturated green/yellow/red pattern (illustrated mean RGB about
0.65/0.68/0.12). Motion: strands churn and drift continuously rather than flashing.
