LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r72 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This looks like a centred, softly glowing swirl of coloured light on a mostly dark background. A thin audio-reactive closed ring (the built-in "audio_deformed_circle" wave) sits at the screen centre (0.5, 0.5). Its radius is about half a model unit and it wobbles with the right-channel waveform, rotating slowly (about 0.2 rad/s). It is drawn additively but with a nearly invisible alpha of 0.001, so the ring itself is probably faint or barely visible, and I am unsure how much of it shows. The more visible seed is a custom wave, "wave_2": 512 fully opaque points per frame arranged on a small parametric radial curve (spiral or looping rose shape; the recipe does not prove an exact circle). All of its points stay in a small box near the middle (x 0.47-0.53, y 0.46-0.54), so the seed is a compact, bright, coloured knot or rosette, not something that fills the screen. Positions are closed-form in time and sample index, so the knot drifts and morphs smoothly rather than jittering.

Feedback and motion. The picture is mostly made by feedback. Each frame the previous image is pulled outward from the centre, with an effective zoom of about 0.17% to 4.4% per step. The zoom exponent is 2.19, so the outward rate depends on radius, and it pulses over time with a slow cosine-like drift. Content also rotates by up to plus or minus 0.01 rad per step, switching direction slowly. The warp centre wanders by about plus or minus 0.11 around the middle. There is no net sideways or vertical drift (dx = dy = 0). The coloured knot should therefore stream outward and swirl into a tunnel-like, spiralling bloom with the trails twisting around the centre. A periodic ripple or wobble in the composite shader (UV advection) adds a gentle waviness. The warp-only gain is 0.92 per frame on flat content, with the same decay on all three channels, so trails have a half-life of roughly 8 frames, about 0.28 s at 30 fps. Trails are medium-short and fade to black without colour cycling. The feedback is not channel-selective, so trails keep their hue as they fade. The recipe lists no mirrors or tiling and a symmetry score of 0, so I would not expect kaleidoscope symmetry.

Colour. The wave_2 points are spread over many hues. By share they are blue 35%, orange 22%, red 11%, pink 9%, green 8%, cyan 7%, yellow 6% and purple 3%, with mean saturation about 0.82. The impression is a multicolour but blue-and-orange-leaning knot. The built-in ring cycles slowly through RGB values between 0.3 and 1.0, at most about 0.3 per second per channel. Over a long time it covers green, pink, blue, orange and other hues, but these are raw source ranges, not observed displayed colours. The shader stages' own palettes are unresolved, and the composite is a plain pass-through on flat canvas (flat canvas level 0.5 shows as 0.5 grey), so I do not expect a global tint. The recipe's steady-state estimate gives no hue family for the background, which settles near black (about 0.0004). Because the trails inherit the knot's colours, the screen should show swirling streaks of blue, orange, red and pink against a dark ground. The final hue mix is conditional on the sampled content and is not verified. The calibrated display estimate (an estimate, not a rendering) puts mean saturation low to moderate, about 0.25 with a wide 0 to 0.52 interval. This suggests the bloom may look more washed or pastel than the wave's 0.82 saturation alone implies. The warm/cool balance is near neutral (-0.09, interval -0.43 to +0.26).

Audio reaction. The only route in the recipe is smoothed (attenuated) treble feeding the red, green and blue colour terms of the warp shader through a clamp or threshold mix, with unknown gain. Treble should therefore modulate colour or brightness of the fed-back image, probably gradually because the input is attenuated. The ring's shape follows the right-channel waveform continuously, so it jitters and bends with the sound. No bass or mid routes are listed and no audio switch sites were found. The zoom and rotation motion are driven by time and not by the audio. Motion therefore continues in silence, and the music mostly changes colour intensity and ring shape. Visible strength is unresolved. The hedged flashing assumption is only a few coherent brightness transitions per second of small size (about 0.1 delta), not strobing, and not a certified absence of flashing.

Brightness and activity. The calibrated estimate for mean luminance is about 0.48, with a 10th to 90th percentile interval of 0.29 to 0.71. That is mid-bright overall and more than the near-black steady-state lower bound suggests, since dense feedback texture raises the average. Brightness jumps are low (about 0.26 per 10 s, interval 0 to 3.7). Mean frame-to-frame difference is about 0.09, with flow of about 0.18 screen widths per second (interval 0.07 to 0.38). Activity is a pack-relative 0.68, labelled Normal, but with a very wide interval of 0.13 to 0.92 that could span Chill to Intense. The recipe's own energy tags are "Smooth" and "Hypnotic" (intensity about 29, smoothness about 88). My overall read is a smooth, hypnotic, medium-speed outward spiral bloom with a moderate energy level.

Random or chaotic parts. The recipe lists no per-load random branches and no random redraws, so I do not expect the look to change between loads. The composite does read a low-quality noise texture, a repeating noise sample that perturbs the warp or wobble coordinates, plus a per-frame random value. That likely adds some fine, irregular shimmer or jitter to the ripple, but its strength is unresolved. The treble-driven colour terms depend on the music. Motion is deterministic in time, with no extra chaos beyond that noise and the feedback itself. Fine details of the streaks will differ between runs.

Main uncertainties. The ring may be nearly invisible because of its alpha of 0.001. The knot's coverage and brightness, the shader palettes, the real treble gain, and how the noise texture shapes the wobble are all unverified. Colours are source possibilities, not confirmed screen colours.
