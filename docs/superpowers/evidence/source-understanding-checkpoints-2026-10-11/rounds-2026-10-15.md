  calibrated saturation is 0.37, and its monochrome probability of 0.65 sits just under the threshold. The
  composite colour matrix has a zero blue column and near-parallel red/green columns, which collapse colour to
  one direction. That fact is not yet a calibration input; it is being tested as recipe colour-probe columns.
- **Brightness:** r225 (an exotic jump-flooding warp that zeroes the canvas) is the only large miss. Its frame
  prediction image matches the screenshot exactly, while the calibrated luminance misses (0.34 vs 0.03). The
  recipe's steady-state and composite probes are being tested as calibration inputs for this reason.
