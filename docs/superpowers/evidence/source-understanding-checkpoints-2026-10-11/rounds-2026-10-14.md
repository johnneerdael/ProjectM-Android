takes milliseconds. Only big misses get a deep dive.

## Deep dive: r181 (partly explained)

- The custom composite multiplies by `pow(hue_shader, 4)` and `dot(ret, hue_shader)`, so the random tint of
  each load sets brightness and hue. New recipe field `composite.load_spread`: at canvas levels 0.05, 0.1
  and 0.2 under 8 tint draws, r181 spans brightness ×1.45 and hues green/pink/purple/red. It also shows a
  steep composite (output ≈ level^2.5).
- The wave is dots + thick + additive with `bModWaveAlphaByVolume` (start 0.91, end 1.30). A variant with
  full alpha shrinks the gap from 6× to 1.5× (ours 0.505, engine 0.744).
- A band probe preset encodes bass/mid/treb as shape colour. Engine bands match the reference sequence to
  about 3 decimals per frame, so the bands are not the cause. projectM's vol and alpha ramp match ours.
- Open: the residual canvas-level gap under volume-modulated alpha. Noted along the way: projectM's
  `ModulateOpacityByVolume` replaces the mode-adjusted alpha with raw `wave_a × ramp` (relevant to modes
  2, 3 and 5).

## Triage lessons

- A single predicted frame against the ruler band is too noisy. It missed r166 and r171 and raised a false
  alarm on r169. The window statistics of frames the prediction already computes (`--frame-stats`) fix this
  at no extra cost.
- The ruler window is frames 60–299 and ours 60–149, so mean luma is judged against the ruler's p10–p90 band.
- Black frames are real outcomes: colour is not judged on them.
- Spatial-response features (A/B, `spatial-response-ab-2026-10-14`) add nothing to the calibrated model.
