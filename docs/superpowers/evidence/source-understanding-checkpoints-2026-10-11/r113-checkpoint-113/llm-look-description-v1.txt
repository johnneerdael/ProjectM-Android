LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r113 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. About five seconds in, I expect a full-screen, densely patterned feedback image rather than a dark background with a few drawn objects. The warp stage is a blur-difference feedback (pattern_growth, channels 0-2, fine level 0 minus coarse level 1, flat gain about 0.94 per frame): small seeds grow into blobs, cells and splatter that spread over the whole canvas. The composite then samples the canvas in polar log-radius coordinates with repeat tiling (not mirrored), so the splatter should read as a tunnel-like or radially repeating swirl around the screen centre. This is only a candidate. The recipe states that a tunnel appearance is not guaranteed. The mesh warp adds a radius- and x-dependent swirl (rotation up to about 1.15 rad per step, near 0 at the centre or edges depending on the term) and a zoom of 1 to 2 driven by smoothed treble. The warp centre wanders slowly over roughly 0.1 to 0.9 of the screen (sinusoids with periods of a few seconds), so the swirl centre drifts. A built-in circular waveform sits at the centre (0.5, 0.5), with a base radius of about half of the model unit. It is deformed by the right-channel waveform with radial gain 0.4 and rotates at about 0.2 rad/s. Its authored alpha is only 0.001, so it is probably almost invisible as a line and acts mainly as a faint seed for the feedback. A border about 3% of the screen thick, in pale yellow-tan (0.7, 0.7, 0.5), is blended in at alpha 1 every frame, so a thin warm frame should show around the edge. The recipe says it is also fed back, so it may smear inward.

Colour. The recipe cannot name the palette. Warp and composite colour paths are all unresolved (null biases, amplitudes, phases), and the palette depends on the sampled feedback. The one source cue is the settled feedback colour. Its hue family is blue, with display-local RGB about (0.09, 0.44, 0.56), a teal-blue, and a large channel spread (red up to about 0.82, green and blue up to 1.0). So I expect predominantly blue to cyan/teal content, with a lighter, more saturated look in the brighter parts. The display estimate gives mean saturation about 0.61 (interval 0.33 to 0.88) and warm/cool about -0.19 (interval -0.52 to +0.16), i.e. slightly cool with a wide range. The tan border and the waveform tints (red from bass, green from treble, blue from mid, each about 0.5 at average audio) add a possible warm accent, but they are weak. The composite is a strong tone curve: flat canvas level 0.25 shows as about 0.60 grey and levels 0.5 and above show as pure white. Mid-bright regions therefore clip toward white or light pastel, while the dark parts stay black. Treble also modulates the composite colour in all three channels, with an unresolved strength.

Motion. The recipe supports continuous, fairly fast movement. The trail half-life is about 0.4 s (feedback gain about 0.93 per frame in each channel, half-life around 9 to 10 frames, steady level 0.10 to 0.19 of full), so there are short trails, not long smears. Flow is mostly rotational and expanding/contracting about a wandering centre. There is no constant translation (dx = dy = 0). The calibrated display estimate gives mean flow about 0.44 screen widths per second (interval 0.23 to 0.81) and mean frame difference about 0.12 (interval 0.07 to 0.19). I expect obvious swirling, with the whole image churning continuously. The zoom range reaches 2x per step in the extreme, but that is a ceiling, not a typical speed. The waveform ring spins slowly at about 0.2 rad/s.

Brightness and contrast. Estimated mean luminance is about 0.38 (interval 0.19 to 0.62): mid-brightness overall, with high contrast from the clipping composite curve and the band-pass sharpening. The estimate gives about 13 global brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 3.6 to 51), so the brightness pulses noticeably. Activity percentile is about 0.95 (interval 0.73 to 0.997), band Intense (possible Normal or Intense). I would call this busy and high energy. The source-based mood list has no strictly eligible group, so any mood label here is an approximation.

Audio response. Smoothed treble drives the uniform zoom (gain 0.5 on treb_att, so zoom is 1 + 0.5*treb_att) and the composite colour in all three channels, so treble should visibly breathe the tunnel and shift colour or brightness. Smoothed bass raises the waveform ring's red, mid raises its blue and treble its green, but with alpha 0.001 this is probably a faint effect. The waveform shape follows the right-channel audio. The responses are continuous, not thresholded, and I found no audio-triggered switch sites, so I do not expect hard cuts or discrete flashes from the source, though the estimate still shows frequent brightness jumps.

Uncertainty. The recipe is unsure about the actual hue mix (palette entries null), whether the composite reads as a clear tunnel or just radially tiled blobs, how visible the waveform ring is given alpha 0.001, how strongly the border frame persists, and the real speed (the recipe mentions only ceilings and calibrated averages). Typical zoom, rotation magnitude and audio gains are not resolved. The mood-feature motion and flash values are marked as assumed, not derived. Colour and brightness estimates carry wide intervals. This preset's drawn content and the audio level at the five-second mark will change the look substantially. The description is approximate, not a pixel-exact specification.
