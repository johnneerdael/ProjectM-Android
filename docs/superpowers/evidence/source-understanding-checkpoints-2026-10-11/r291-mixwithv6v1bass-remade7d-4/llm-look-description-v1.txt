Approximate look (source-only, uncertain): a busy, mid-bright, fairly colourful and restless picture built around a large audio-reactive round shape near the middle of the screen, rather than a calm dark background with a few lines.

Brightness and colour: the warp and composite shaders are non-linear. A flat black canvas is lifted to roughly 0.6 grey by the warp, while the composite maps canvas levels non-monotonically (black shows white, about half level shows black, full level shows white). The settled canvas is about 0.77 and the settled display about 0.43 grey, so the background is probably a mid-grey field with strong contrast swings, not black. The calibrated estimate says mean luminance about 0.40 (0.22 to 0.61), mean saturation about 0.42, and about 62% of the screen coloured. Likely leading hues are green and blue, with pink and cyan behind them. The gap between the leading hues is small, so call them roughly equal rather than naming one. The Bright facet is admitted (0.71 against a 0.44 threshold). Pastel is not admitted (0.44 against 0.61), though it may still read soft. Colours come from the circular shape (its fill and rim shift with smoothed bass, mid and treble) and from the shaders, whose colour is driven by all bands. The reached palette is unverified.

Structures: a 100-sided polygon, so effectively a circle, whose centre, radius, opacity and colour all react to bass, mid and treble. A built-in circular waveform ring sits at the centre with a base radius of about 0.5 and turns slowly (about 0.2 rad/s). Its alpha is only 0.001 and its colour is near black, so it is probably barely visible. A custom waveform line also exists but its layout is unresolved.

Motion: the warp shows no drift (dx and dy are 0), no rotation, and an inward stretch of about 0.99 per frame with a warp amplitude of up to 0.4. Zoom is audio-driven but unresolved. Trails are short, with a halving time of about 4 frames. Because of the non-linear transfer, dim content is amplified toward the grey level. Expect a swirling, morphing field rather than steady spinning. The Drifting facet is admitted (0.72).

Audio and flashing: bass, mid and treble all move the shape and change opacity and zoom, mostly through thresholded or clamped routes, which should read as pulsing and jumping on beats. The estimate is about 45 global brightness jumps per 10 s (range 15 to 165), and the no-strobe probability is only 0.004. It should flicker strongly and is not strobe-free. Activity is Intense (0.78, interval 0.31 to 0.97). Uncertain: exact shapes and positions, the custom wave, and the real palette.
