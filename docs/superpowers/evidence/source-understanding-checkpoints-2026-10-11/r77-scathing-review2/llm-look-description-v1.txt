LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r77 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a busy, fast, psychedelic field built from several stacked layers rather than one clear object. A large audio-deformed circle (built-in wave mode 0, base radius about 0.5, slowly rotating at roughly 0.2 rad/s) is drawn from the right-channel waveform, but at an authored alpha of only 0.009, so it is a very faint ring that cycles through colours. On top of that sit four polygon-shape families with many instances: a 20-sided shape (1024 instances), a 12-sided one (92), a 23-sided one (1024) and a small black triangle layer (311 instances, radius 0.01), plus four 512-point custom waveforms drawn as dots. Only one of those dot clouds (wave_1, pink, alpha about 0.1) has known extent: a small cluster near the centre (x 0.49-0.54, y 0.49-0.53). The others have unresolved positions and colours. The recipe does not establish independent particles, so read these as dense dotted or polygon lattices, not tracked sparks. A 46x47 motion-vector grid (2162 vectors) is also drawn into the feedback, adding fine streaky texture. The warp shader mirrors the image, tiles it with mirrored repeats and lets the image steer its own sampling coordinates (self-advection). Expect a kaleidoscopic, tiled, smeared or liquid-looking pattern with symmetry that is only approximate. The recipe calls it a kaleidoscope/symmetry candidate and gives a symmetry score of 0.

Colour: the colour is mixed and uncertain. The built-in circle's RGB each drift between about 0.001 and 0.999 (at most about 0.5 per second), so over time it passes through green (24%), pink (19%), blue (17%), orange (10%), cyan (9%), red, purple and yellow. These are raw source curves, not displayed colours. The shape borders are white, but their alpha is tiny (0.006). Individual layers are authored as grayscale/cyan, grayscale/pink and grayscale. The recipe's own estimate is moderate saturation (about 0.31 from source, 0.38 in the calibrated display estimate, with a wide interval of 0.11-0.65) and a roughly neutral to slightly cool balance (about -0.22 from source; the display estimate is +0.09 with a wide interval that includes both warm and cool). The flat-colour tests show the composite maps flat canvas to black, and the steady-state display colour is black with no hue family. A uniform tint of roughly (0.39, 0.32, 0.21), a brownish-orange, is added each frame in the warp. Whether it shows depends on later shading, so I would not predict a single dominant hue. The final palette is conditional.

Feedback and motion: the trails are very short. The warp multiplies flat content by gain 0, so uniform content does not persist (half-life about 1 frame). Anything that lingers must come from re-drawn layers, the motion-vector grid and the advection and mirroring, not from a long fade. The "bright objects leave long ghosts" picture is therefore unlikely; the look is more of a constantly re-synthesised pattern. The zoom is a constant 1.0 and zoomexp is 1.50. The rotation, centre and translation controls depend on music and state, so their size and direction are unknown, but they are moving. Shape and wave positions are also driven by bass, mid and treble. The estimated flow is about 0.27 screen widths per second (interval 0.12-0.53), and the mean frame-to-frame difference is high (0.14), so it should look restless.

Audio reaction: bass, attenuated bass and attenuated treble drive the warp rotation. Bass, mid and the attenuated bands drive the warp translation, the circle's position, and the shapes' positions (all clamped or thresholded, so the response is not linear). Bass also grows the shape radius slightly (gain 0.01) and changes shape colour and opacity. Expect the whole pattern to lurch, swirl and shift with bass hits, with treble-driven drift on top. The recipe gives no reliable linear strengths, so I cannot say how strong the visible response is.

Brightness and flashing: overall brightness is probably dim to medium. The calibrated mean luminance is 0.18 with a very wide interval of 0.0-0.42, and the source-only drawing contribution is tiny, because most alphas are very low. The same estimate gives activity 0.97 (Intense) and about 100 global brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 33-377), and the mood block assumes coherent transitions of 2.4-4 Hz. That makes it a busy, possibly flickery preset. This is an estimate, not a measured flash rate, and the strict source model assigns no eligible mood group. Read it as Psychedelic, Geometric and Organic/Liquid; that is approximate and tentative.

Random or chaotic: the recipe lists no random-per-load branches or random redraws, so nothing here is flagged as a per-load random choice. The behaviour is chaotic mostly because of audio-driven state, self-advection and the unresolved position, rotation and translation programs, not because of explicit randomness. Expect it to differ run to run with the music. Overall uncertainty is high. This is an approximate recipe, not a pixel specification.
