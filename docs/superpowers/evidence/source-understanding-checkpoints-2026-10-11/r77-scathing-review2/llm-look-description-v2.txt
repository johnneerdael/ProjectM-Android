LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (r77 v2; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. The recipe describes a busy, layered scene rather than one clean figure. A circular, audio-deformed waveform ring (built-in wave mode 0, driven by the right-channel waveform) is drawn over several large groups of small polygons: a 20-sided shape repeated 1024 times, a 12-sided shape repeated 92 times, a 23-sided shape repeated 1024 times and a small triangle repeated 311 times. Four custom waveforms of 512 points each add dotted point clouds. A motion-vector grid of 46 x 47 (2162 vectors) is also drawn into the feedback image. These are configured counts and nominal geometry, not tracked particles (the recipe says independent particles are unestablished), and how much of the screen they cover is unresolved. The only point-cloud position bounds given are for wave_1, a small pink cluster near the screen centre (x 0.49-0.54, y 0.49-0.53, mean alpha about 0.1). The warp stage mirrors the image across an axis, tiles it with mirrored repeats, and lets the image steer its own sampling coordinates (self-advection). I would expect a symmetric, kaleidoscope-like, smeared and fluid-looking texture. The recipe's own mirror symmetry score is 0.0 with the note that echo and later drawing make symmetry inexact, so the symmetry is only a candidate. There is also a radial zoom/expansion feedback transform.

Colour. The palette is unresolved. The built-in wave colour cycles across nearly the full range (r, g and b each between about 0.001 and 0.999, at most about 0.5 per second). Over 120 s it is estimated to visit green 24%, pink 19%, blue 17%, orange 10%, cyan 9%, red 7%, purple 6% and yellow 5%. That is a raw source curve, not an observed on-screen colour, and its alpha is only 0.009. The custom waves are described as possible grayscale, cyan or pink, and wave_1 is pink. The warp's flat-colour response is a constant tint of roughly (0.39, 0.32, 0.21), a muted warm brown, but the recipe gives no steady hue family. The calibrated saturation estimate is about 0.38 (interval 0.11-0.65) and the warm/cool value is about +0.09 (interval -0.25 to +0.44), so it is neither clearly warm nor clearly cool. The source palette estimate leans slightly cool (about -0.22). I would call it moderately saturated, with several hues present, and I would not commit to one dominant colour.

Feedback and motion. The warp transfer on flat content has gain 0 for all channels. Previous-frame content is not kept as a long trail, and trails are essentially one frame long (half-life about 0.03 s). However, the image-driven advection, the mirror and the tiling still reshuffle whatever is drawn, so the picture is smeared and mirrored each frame instead of fading slowly. The composite stage is flagged output_undefined with nonfinite_share 1.0, meaning its output is NaN or infinite in GLSL ES. What reaches the display therefore depends on the GPU driver. It could come out black, flat or garbage, so I do not claim one fixed look. The settled display colour in the recipe is black, and the canvas lower bound is only about (0.05, 0.08, 0.13), a dim bluish level. Taken together, this is a real risk that the preset looks dark or broken on some drivers. The calibrated estimates suggest a lively image when it does render: mean frame-to-frame difference about 0.14 (interval 0.09-0.22), flow about 0.27 screen widths per second (interval 0.12-0.53) and a predicted activity of 0.97, which falls in the Intense band. Motion comes from shape positions that move, the mirrored/tiled warp and the audio-driven translation and rotation of the warp. These are nominal source facts and ceilings, and typical visible speed is unverified.

Audio reaction. Bass, mid and treble, including their attenuated versions, drive the warp rotation and x/y translation, so the whole feedback image shoves, swirls and drifts with the music. Bass and mid also move the circular wave's centre, and treble moves its y position. The shapes' positions respond to bass, mid and treble, many through thresholds or clamps. Shape opacity responds to bass, mid and treble. Shape_0 colour responds to bass, mid and treble. Shape_0 radius grows with bass at gain 0.01, and shape_2 radius responds to bass and treble at about 0.013. Shape_0's perimeter blue channel falls with bass (gain -0.5) and rises with treble (+0.7). The waveform ring follows the right-channel audio shape continuously. Most of these routes have no stated linear gain, so I know the targets but not the strength. They show where it can react, not how visible the reaction is or whether it syncs to the beat.

Brightness and flashing. The mean luminance estimate is about 0.18, but the interval runs from 0.0 to 0.42. That means anywhere from nearly black to moderately bright, and it overlaps the undefined-composite caveat above. The global brightness jump estimate is about 101 per 10 s (interval 33-377), which is high and uncertain. This is a calibrated prediction, not a rendered measurement, and the recipe does not establish a visible strobe. I would expect busy, flickery, intensity-varying output, not a calm picture. The tags are estimated only: Psychedelic, Geometric, Organic/Liquid. The strict model gives no eligible mood group.

Random, chaotic or undefined. The recipe lists no per-load random branches and no random redraw events. The composite output is undefined and driver-dependent. Final palette, dominance and screen coverage are conditional. Wave colour and positions for waves 0, 2 and 3 are unresolved. Visible speed, trail look and beat synchronisation are unverified. Two of the mood features (mean encoded luma and feedback half-life) are listed as unknown. The recipe itself says real frames will differ from this engine-neutral approximation.
