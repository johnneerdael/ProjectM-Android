LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r63 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This looks like a swirling, tunnel-like feedback field with a thin audio waveform drawn on top. The built-in wave is mode 7, two parallel waveform traces (left and right channel) running as unfilled, non-additive, non-dotted lines. It is centred horizontally (wave_x 0.5) and sits at the very top of the frame (wave_y 1.0), so the drawn lines are slim and spread across the upper edge. Most of the picture is not the lines themselves but what the feedback does to them. Each frame the previous image is pulled toward the screen centre (zoom factors of about 0.87 to 0.98, a 2 to 13 percent contraction per step, stronger toward the edge because the zoom depends on radius) and twisted by a radius-dependent rotation of up to about +/-0.3 rad per step. The result should be traces smearing into spiralling, inward-collapsing streaks and swirls. There is no net sideways or vertical drift (dx = dy = 0). Both the warp shader and the composite shader add a periodic ripple or wobble displacement, so edges should look gently wavy rather than sharp. Mirror symmetry is essentially absent (score 0), so expect an organic, asymmetric swirl rather than a kaleidoscope. This is an approximate recipe, not a pixel-exact specification.

Colour. The recipe cannot give a firm palette: the mesh, warp and composite colour sources are all marked unresolved, and the wave colour depends on time and on bass, mid and treble differences, so I treat it as a shifting, colour-cycling line. The only solid hue cue is the settled steady-state display colour, which is in the red family (about R 1.0, G 0.38, B 0.39 locally). The composite's flat-canvas transfer is also red-leaning. Dark canvas areas show as a salmon or red tint, mid levels as a pinkish red, and the brightest levels fall toward dark red or black. So I expect a predominantly red, pink and magenta cast with darker red and black in the highlights, plus whatever the wave colour contributes. The calibrated estimate gives fairly high saturation (about 0.69, interval 0.42 to 0.96) and a warm/cool value near the middle (0.52, interval 0.18 to 0.87), so warm red is my best guess but cooler tones are not ruled out. Several q values (q21 to q29 and q31) are redrawn at random at about 3.1 s and then every 12.6 s (3.1, 15.7, 28.3, 40.8, 53.4 s...). Anything in the colours or shapes that depends on them is a random draw that changes at those moments, so I cannot name one fixed palette or layout, and it will differ from load to load.

Feedback and motion. Trails are moderate: the warp transfer on flat content has gain about 0.971 per frame, a halving time of about 24 frames (roughly 0.8 s). Content is not kept at a constant level, though. Red and blue settle at a floor of roughly 0.45 of full and green decays toward zero, so bright content fades toward a reddish residue and does not fade evenly. The calibrated flow is about 0.32 screen widths per second (interval 0.16 to 0.62), and the mean frame-to-frame change is 0.056 (interval 0.02 to 0.11). That is fairly lively, constantly churning motion, mostly inward and rotational. Both shaders carry a slow nominal brightness cycle of about 0.0796 Hz (a period of about 12.6 s, matching the redraw period). This is a source mechanism only, and the recipe says displayed flashing is unverified.

Audio reaction. Bass, mid, treble and their attenuated versions all feed the rotation and the x/y scaling in the mesh warp. The wave colour channels (wave_r and others) and the shader colours also read the bands. The rotation expression uses differences between raw and smoothed band values, so it is driven by sudden changes (onsets). Expect the swirl direction and strength, and the zoom and stretch, to lurch with beats and transients rather than follow steady loudness. The recipe gives no linear gains, so I cannot say how strong the visible response is. Colour of the wave line probably flickers or shifts with the music. The tags are Reactive and Flashing, but both are estimates.

Brightness and mood. The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.45 (interval 0.26 to 0.69), so mid-bright overall with dark gaps. Saturation is around 0.69. Global brightness jumps are estimated at about 1.2 per 10 s, but the interval runs from 0 to 7.3, so I cannot rule out noticeable flashing. Activity is about 0.72 (interval 0.11 to 0.94), bands Normal and Intense (Chill also possible), and the approximate mood is Intense with high stated confidence. The strict source model lists no eligible mood group. This is a calibrated source-based estimate, not a render. Main uncertainties are the actual palette (red-leaning but unresolved), how prominent the thin top-edge wave is against the feedback, the random per-load q draws, and how strong the flashing really is.
