LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r112 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. Roughly five seconds in, I expect a feedback-built scene with a large, centred, slowly rotating ring and a scattered white waveform trace, both smeared into short glowing trails. The ring is the built-in audio-deformed circle (render.layers builtin_wave, wave_x/wave_y 0.5/0.5, effective radius about 0.58 model units, which is about half the screen height or a little more, so it likely reaches or crosses the screen edges). Its line follows the right-channel waveform, so the ring wobbles with the music, and it turns at about 0.2 rad/s. The recipe's own caveat is that this wave is drawn at authored alpha 0.001, so the ring may be faint or nearly invisible. Whether the mode's alpha boost makes it clearly visible is not resolved. A custom 512-point line wave (wave_0) is drawn in white at authored alpha 0.6. Its closed-form point positions span x -0.27 to 1.30 and y -0.29 to 1.32, so much of it runs past the screen edges, and what shows is probably a wide white loop or scribble crossing the picture. There is also one four-sided polygon (shape_2, a square or diamond, one instance). Its position and radius are unknown ("moving": true, but the path is unresolved) and its fill alpha is 0, so I cannot say whether it is visible at all. The zoom base is about 1.2 plus audio terms, so the feedback has a zoom-in or tunnel character: older content is pulled outward from the screen centre each frame. The recipe has no rotation, no translation and a very small warp (0.01), and sx is 0.9999, so I expect a radial pulse and expansion rather than swirl or drift.

Colour. The only fixed hue in the source is the ring: red 0, green 0.99, blue 1.0, max-normalised, which is cyan to aqua. The white custom wave is unsaturated. The shader warp and composite palettes are marked unresolved, so I cannot rule out other tints. The composite flat transfer maps canvas levels 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 to 0.5, 1.0 and 1.0 in every channel, so it roughly doubles brightness and clips at white. That is grey-preserving and adds no hue. The source palette estimate is cold (warm_cool -0.87, hue bins 1, so a single cyan family). The display estimate is nearly neutral (warm_cool -0.09, interval -0.42 to +0.26) with low saturation (0.15, interval 0 to 0.42). My expectation is mostly white and pale cyan-tinted lines on a dark background, a cool leaning but not strongly coloured picture. A vivid multicolour palette is not supported by the recipe.

Motion. The warp has no drift (dx = dy = 0) and no rotation, so the visible motion is the ring's 0.2 rad/s turn, the waveform's own wiggle and a pulsing zoom. The zoom has bass, mid and treble inputs, but the exact gain is not resolved (a thresholded or clamped response, with a peak-triggered and time-gated term and a modulo on the index). The display estimate gives flow of about 0.44 screen widths per second (interval 0.23 to 0.81) and a mean frame-to-frame difference of 0.13 (0.08 to 0.20). That is brisk, not calm. Trails are short: the warp transfer is a uniform gain of 0.912 per frame, a half-life of about 7.5 frames (0.25 s at 30 fps), so lines leave only a brief, fading echo as they expand outward. The picture does not accumulate into a dense wash.

Brightness and contrast. The recipe's steady-state canvas is near black (about 0.002 to 0.004), so the empty background should be dark, and the composite's 2x gain makes the drawn lines read bright against it. The display estimate gives mean luminance of 0.26, with a wide interval of 0.08 to 0.50. I would say a dark to mid-dark frame with bright thin lines, but not a washed-out one. Global brightness jumps are estimated at about 2 per 10 s (interval 0 to 10), so some pulsing with the music is likely and frequent strobing is not established. Contrast between lines and background should be high.

Audio response. The ring shape follows the waveform continuously. The shape's radius and opacity are driven by bass, mid and treble, and the warp zoom is driven by bass, mid and treble, all through thresholded or clamped responses, so the response probably comes in bursts on stronger hits rather than smoothly. Attenuated treble feeds the warp shader's RGB, which suggests a slow brightness or tint modulation from high-frequency content. The recipe gives no linear gains, so I cannot say how strong any of these are, or whether the zoom pulse is a whole-screen beat or something subtle. The calibrated activity is 0.91 (interval 0.50 to 0.98), which places it in the Intense band, possibly Normal. The strict eligible activity bands are empty and confidence is low.

Uncertainty. Unresolved: whether the nearly transparent ring is visible, the size, position and visibility of the square shape, the real amplitude of the zoom pulse, and any colour from the warp and composite shaders. The 512-point wave extends well beyond the frame, so its visible portion is unknown. The "cold" source palette and the near-neutral display estimate disagree, so the colour reading is low confidence. Mean brightness and saturation have wide intervals, and the activity band is an estimate calibrated on other presets, not a measurement of this one.
