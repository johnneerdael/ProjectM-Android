LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r95 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. The preset is "Dbleja - Horizontology (White Mix)". The recipe shows two drawn things on top of a heavy feedback warp: a single thin additive built-in waveform line (mode 4, "horizontal_momentum_trace", dots false, thick false) anchored near the screen centre (wave_x 0.49, wave_y 0.51), and a 63 x 45 grid of motion vectors (2835 short strokes, length scale 4.5) covering the whole frame. The waveform line is authored with alpha 0.001 (/render/layers/1/colour/channels/wave_a), so on its own it is almost invisible; I expect no bold, clearly readable oscilloscope line. What a viewer mostly sees is the feedback image: a pale, smeared, whitish texture built from faint line and vector-stroke fragments that are repeatedly zoomed, rotated and stretched, with a fine grid-like speckle from the motion vectors (each vector has alpha about 0.10-0.12, /render/layers/2/colour_rgba). No tunnel, symmetry, mirrors (mirror operators empty, symmetry score 0) or fractal construction is recognised, and there are no custom shapes, custom waves or shaders in the recipe.

Colour. Essentially white-grey. The wave and the vectors are drawn with R=G=B between 0.82 and 0.98, drifting together with a 27.3 s period, so the line is a very light grey, never a hue. The steady-state estimate is grayscale (/render/feedback/steady_state, display level about 0.99 with spread about 0.21, hue_family "grayscale"). The composite carries a legacy hue tint of amount 0.3 whose placement is random per preset load, so there may be a faint, soft colour wash in some corners or regions, but the recipe does not say which hues, and I will not name one. The calibrated display estimate gives mean saturation 0.34 (10th-90th percentile 0.07-0.61) and warm/cool 0.04 (-0.30 to +0.39), i.e. mostly neutral with possible mild tint. Overall: a white or silver picture with at most a pastel, randomly placed tint.

Motion. Motion is strong and constant, driven by feedback rather than by the line. Every frame the previous image is zoomed by a factor that oscillates between 0.9065 and 1.1065 (about -9.4% to +10.7% per step, slow envelope; the zoom control changes at most 0.15 per second), so the image alternately contracts and expands. It is also rotated by 0.17 to 0.83 rad per step (offset 0.5 plus 0.33*sin(t), period 6.28 s), which is a very large rotation per frame, so fine detail is twisted into swirling smears rather than traced as clean spirals. The rotation/stretch centre cx moves with bass_att between about 0.33 and 0.73 and cy swings 0.4 to 0.6 with a 6.3 s sine. The warp also has dx=1.0 (a full-unit sideways source offset, 480 px per step in the recipe's units) and a horizontal stretch sx=100; how these combine with wrap or clamping is not resolved by the recipe. My best reading is that content is smeared strongly along the horizontal axis, giving streaky, horizontally brushed texture. Trails are short: decay 0.935 per frame, half-life about 0.37 s (11 steps), with a residue floor at stored level 7/255, so a faint grey haze can linger instead of full black. Flow estimate is about 0.33 screen widths per second (interval 0.16-0.64), pack-relative activity 0.55 (Normal, though Chill to Intense are all possible within the interval).

Brightness and contrast. The recipe pulls in two directions. Composite gamma is 2.63, which can brighten the image up to 2.63x before clamping, and the feedback steady state is stated as a near-white display level of about 0.99 (but flagged as a lower bound with the canvas itself at only 0.0165). Against that, the line alpha is 0.001 and the vectors are faint, so the source content feeding the canvas is thin. The calibrated estimate for mean luminance is 0.17 with a very wide interval (0.00-0.41). So I expect an image that is mid-to-dark overall with a bright, hazy, washed-out texture where strokes accumulate, rather than a full white screen, but I cannot say which. Contrast is probably soft. Frame-to-frame difference is estimated low-to-moderate (0.024, up to 0.065). No flashing mechanism was found (flash transitions 0 Hz, brightness jump 0 in the mood features), though the calibrated estimate allows about 2 global brightness jumps per 10 s (0.05-10.7), so slow pulsing is possible.

Audio response. The only established audio route is that the waveline shape follows the left/right waveform continuously (inputs waveform_left, waveform_right); no band-gated colour, brightness or size route was found (audio_bindings empty). The one band dependence is the rotation/stretch centre cx, which reads bass_att (smoothed bass), so on loud bass passages the swirl centre shifts sideways across roughly 0.33-0.73 of the width; this should look like the whole smear pattern sliding or pivoting with the bass, not a flash. Because the line is almost transparent, the waveform shape itself will barely show; the music mostly shows as modulation of the vector strokes and the shifting pivot. Gain size and beat synchronisation are not established.

Uncertainty. High uncertainty on overall brightness (interval spans nearly black to quite bright) and on how visible the waveform is at all (alpha 0.001). The effect of dx=1 and sx=100 in the warp is not resolved, so the horizontal streak direction and how much texture survives is my inference. The hue tint is random per load, so colour could differ between runs. Speeds are nominal per-step values; real rotation of 0.17-0.83 rad per frame will look like chaotic swirl rather than readable rotation. The recipe has no mesh_field pattern details beyond uniform zoom/rotation/translation, no engine-measured facets, and the mood features for brightness and warm/cool are unknown. Mood scores (intensity 48.5, smoothness 20, psychedelic 14) are uncalibrated assumptions.
