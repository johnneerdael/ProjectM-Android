Approximate look (source-only, very uncertain): a restless, medium-bright field of saturated colour, grown from a few tiny coloured seeds by a blurring, channel-skewing feedback loop. It is probably green-led, with mixed red, blue and pink, and not a clean picture with distinct shapes.

Drawn content: only four custom waveforms of 512 points each are authored. They are pure red, pure blue, pure green and magenta-pink, all at full alpha. Their positions are almost entirely confined to one spot near x and y of 0.9 (bottom right), with an x spread of at most 0.002 to 0.004. They therefore act as small coloured injection points, not as visible lines or rings across the screen. There are no built-in waves and no polygon shapes. Whatever reaches the rest of the screen has to come from the feedback.

Feedback and colour: the warp shader is an 8-tap blur with a fixed radius, so any injected colour spreads into soft haze and smeared streaks. The warp transfer is channel-selective and non-linear. Dim content is amplified strongly, with retention far above 1 at low levels, and the red, green and blue channels settle at different levels (about 0.88, 0.91 and 0.65 from full). So the canvas probably fills up with a yellow-green wash, and faint content brightens quickly instead of fading. The composite maps flat canvas to very dark (at most about 0.09), yet textured detail comes out as brighter neutral grey (about 0.74 luma). So the smooth areas may look dark and the structure bright. The calibrated estimate is medium brightness (about 0.40, range 0.22 to 0.61), saturation about 0.72, and about 78% of the screen coloured. Hue shares lead with green (about 32 to 35%), then yellow, blue, red and pink. The Bright and Pastel facets are not admitted.

Motion: a very slight uniform contraction toward the centre (0.049% per step), with no rotation and no drift. The motion is mainly the blur smearing and the wave seeds being pulled inward, a gentle swirl or bleed inward from the bottom-right seed rather than a big flow. The estimated flow is moderate (about 0.22 screen widths per s, wide range).

Audio and flashing: the only explicit audio route is treble to the green channel of the warp shader, through a clamped path. This could cause treble-driven green pulses. The estimate is about 28 global brightness jumps per 10 s (range 9 to 102), and the no-strobe probability is only 0.08. Expect frequent flicker, and do not call it strobe-free. Activity is Intense (0.91). The Intense and Balanced facets are admitted. Uncertain: whether the seeds produce a visible picture at all, and the actual hue balance.
