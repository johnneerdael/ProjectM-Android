LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (r61 v3; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

The picture is a fairly dark, soft, hazy field with a small bright centrepiece. The core is a small custom line wave, 512 points, packed into a box about 0.4 to 0.6 of the screen in both axes (roughly the middle fifth). Its points sit on closed-form positions that depend on sample index and time, with bass nudging the colour terms. Around the same centre, a built-in circular wave (audio_deformed_circle, mode 0, base radius about 0.5 model units, rotating nominally 0.2 rad/s) is bent by the right waveform channel. It is a thin, non-additive, non-thick line. Its authored colour is a mid-grey 0.5, and its alpha is only about 0.001. The max-normalise rule scales it to a full-strength line, but the near-zero alpha means it is probably barely visible and contributes little directly. The recipe marks its displayed contribution as unresolved. Nothing here is tiled or mirrored: the mirror symmetry score is 0 and there are no mirror operators.

The feedback is what shapes the look. A radial zoom (no rotation, no translation, centre fixed at 0.5,0.5) varies with distance from the centre, from about 0.77 to 1.11. A neighbourhood blur then averages 8 taps at offset 0.01, and the blur radius grows toward the edges and changes with the music. Together these should smear the centre drawing into a soft radial haze or tunnel-like smudge, with an expansion or contraction feel about the screen centre. A composite relief stage (two-direction central differences) then shows the image through its local slopes, like embossing. Flat areas all read as one level and edges stand out. The warp transfer on flat colour has gain 0.453, a halving time of 1 frame and a steady level of 0.25 of full. Trails are therefore very short, and the canvas settles low and does not build up to bright. The recipe lists no persistent drift (dx and dy are 0), and no wrap-around colour cycling.

Colour comes mostly from the composite. Its hue shader multiplies the screen by four corner shades, each cycling with periods of about 14.6 s, 19.6 s and 16.2 s for R, G and B across the range 0.67 to 1. Which hue sits in which corner is random per preset load, so I will not name one fixed tint. The settled colour is a dim purple-leaning tone (about 0.044, 0.030, 0.050), and the recipe says this level is only a lower bound. The flat-transfer palette is time-varying, with blue shifting most. The custom wave's points are blue about 47%, yellow about 46%, purple about 6% and green about 1%. Their mean saturation is about 0.73, and the points are fully opaque. The overall palette is therefore probably saturated blue, yellow and purple against a dark purple-ish ground, though the reached colours are unverified. The warm/cool balance is near neutral. The scalar estimate is 0.04 with a wide interval, and the intent block says 0.15 (slightly warm).

Audio reactivity is mostly on colour. Bass drives red, green and blue in the warp shader, unsmoothed, and attenuated bass and treble drive them in the composite. All of these are threshold or clamped responses with unknown gains, so the strength and any beat sync are unresolved. The blur radius also reads audio, which can make the haze smear erratically, possibly into star or X shapes. The built-in circle follows the waveform continuously. The source model lists a nominal brightness-cycle rate of 14.3 Hz in the warp shader, but a displayed flash is unverified. The "Flashing" tag is an estimate only.

Brightness and activity come from the calibrated display estimate. Mean luminance is about 0.29 (interval 0.11 to 0.53), so it is moderately dim, with large uncertainty. Frame-to-frame change is moderately high at about 0.11, and flow is about 0.24 screen widths per second. Brightness jumps are about 53 per 10 s, with a very wide range of 17 to 200. Mean saturation is about 0.75. The activity percentile is about 0.93, which reads as an Intense but smooth and hypnotic preset. The strict model gives no eligible mood group, so these labels are only an approximation. The Smooth, Hypnotic and Reactive tags are estimates. This is a calibrated estimate from source, not a render. Unknowns include the actual brightness of the centre drawing, how visible the faint circle is, true trail density, and the random hue placement per load. Treat the picture as approximate, not pixel-exact.
