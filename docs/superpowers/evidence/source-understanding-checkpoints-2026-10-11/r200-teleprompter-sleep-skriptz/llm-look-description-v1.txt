Approximate look (source-only, uncertain): a hazy, smeared, flickering glow built from a tiny cluster of coloured points near the lower right of the screen, on an otherwise dark to mid-bright, amplified background.

Brightness and colour: the composite is steep and clips. A dim canvas level of 0.125 displays at about 0.47, 0.25 displays at about 0.94, and anything at 0.5 or above displays as pure white. So small amounts of drawn light are boosted to near-white, and the grey texture response is white (detail response mean 1.0). The calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.39 (range 0.21 to 0.63) and saturation about 0.52, mildly warm. The recipe's settled display colour is only about 0.03, but it is marked as a lower bound. Colour comes from four custom waves whose points carry blue (43%), yellow (29%), green (14%) and orange (14%), highly saturated (about 0.91). These probably show as multicoloured smears. A built-in grey two-trace waveform at alpha 0.001 contributes almost nothing.

Structures: all four custom waves (512 points each, one drawn as points) are collapsed into a nearly single spot at about x 0.9, y 0.9. That is the lower right area, assuming y counts down from the top. So there are no large crisp shapes. Instead there is a bright cluster of dots, spread into haze by an 8-tap blur whose radius grows toward the edges and changes with bass.

Motion: the feedback has a slight contraction toward the centre (0.05% per frame) and a constant sideways shift (0.005 of the screen per frame, content moving right), so the cluster should smear and drift into streaks and soft clouds rather than stay a sharp dot. Trails are fairly short for bright content (about half-life of one frame above mid levels), but dim content below about 0.25 is kept at full strength each frame, so faint haze can build up and persist. The calibrated flow is about 0.2 screen widths per second.

Audio: bass feeds the red, green and blue channels of the warp stage through thresholded routes and also changes the blur radius, so low notes should brighten or tint the haze. The built-in waveform follows the left and right audio but is faint. Gain sizes are unknown.

Flashing: this one flickers. The estimate is about 23 global brightness jumps per 10 s (range 7 to 89), which counts as frequent flicker and possibly strobe-like at the top. The source-estimated coherent transition rate is about 31 Hz, and the warp has a nominal brightness cycle of 14 Hz. It is not suitable for a no-strobe setting. Activity is Intense (low confidence; Normal is possible).
