Approximate look (source-only, uncertain): a smooth, fluid, saturated and colourful smear of light, closer to liquid paint swirling across the whole screen than to crisp geometry.

Colour and brightness: the canvas itself settles near black (about 0.015), but the composite shader adds its own colour. A flat dark canvas is displayed as a magenta-pink (about RGB 0.66, 0.16, 0.57), and that tint changes over time, with a large spread between the sampled times. So the screen background is probably a pink or magenta wash that slowly shifts, not black. The pooled hue estimate leads with green (about 23%), then pink (18%), blue (15%), orange (14%) and yellow (11%). Several hues share the picture and none clearly dominates. Calibrated estimates give mean luminance about 0.35, saturation about 0.72 (vivid) and about 87% of the screen coloured. The Bright facet is not admitted (0.26 against 0.44), so expect medium brightness, not a glaring picture. Pastel is rejected. The warm/cool balance is about neutral to slightly warm.

Structures: no single big shape. The drawn content is a built-in two-channel waveform (parallel left and right traces, additive) with a very low authored alpha of 0.001, so it is faint. Four custom waveforms of 512 points each are authored, with fully coloured points in blue, yellow, green and orange. Their positions sit in a tiny region near x and y of 0.9, bottom right, so they act like a small seed that the feedback then smears across the screen, rather than visible lines. Their displayed coverage is unresolved.

Motion: the picture flows. Both the warp and the composite shaders use the image to steer their own sampling (self-advection with a blur sampler), which gives swirling, smeared, liquid structure. The mesh adds a gentle radial twist, with rotation driven by bass, mid and treble through clamped routes. It also adds a slow global drift of up to about 115 px/s, oscillating with periods of roughly 12 to 24 s. Trails are long: each frame keeps about 99% of the image, a half-life of about 2.3 s, so the colours pile up and blend. Facets: Drifting (0.79) and Normal (0.89) are admitted, and the Chill band is not.

Audio and flashing: bass, mid and treble drive the colour of the waveform and the rotation. The estimate is about 3 global brightness jumps per 10 s (range 0.3 to 13), and the no-strobe probability is only 0.27, so occasional pulses are likely and it is not strobe-free. Activity is Normal (0.50, wide interval). Uncertain: how bright the waveform traces are, whether the bottom-right seeds read at all, and the real hue balance.
