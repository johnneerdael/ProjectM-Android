LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r81 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This looks like a busy, mosaic-like feedback field rather than a single clean object. Three groups of 32-sided polygon shapes (33, 7 and 33 configured instances, so close to circles) are drawn each frame. Their centres and radii come from per-frame equations that use rand and the instance index, so I expect scattered discs or rings placed at random positions, re-scattered over time, not a fixed grid. Their centres are tied to attenuated bass, mid and treble, so they jump or shift with the music. A 512-point custom wave is also drawn, but its alpha comes from a rand-driven expression and the recipe reports mean alpha 0, so I cannot say whether it is visible. A built-in circular wave (audio-deformed ring at screen centre, radius about 0.5, slowly rotating at 0.2 rad/s) is drawn in white with alpha about 0.001, so it is probably almost invisible. A 1% black border is drawn at full alpha every frame and cuts the screen edge.

Feedback and motion. Everything is fed back through a mesh zoom of 1 plus 0.009*bass plus 0.0095*bass_att, with zoomexp 1.47, centred on the screen. That is a gentle zoom or expansion of up to about 6% per step, strongest away from the centre, driven by bass. There is no rotation and no steady drift (dx and dy are 0). On top of that, the warp shader pushes the picture along its own colours and a noise texture, and the composite shader does a blur-driven self-advection. Both give a smeared, flowing, slightly turbulent look, with offsets of a few percent of the screen. In the warp, red and green are kept whole every frame (gain 1.0) and only blue fades (gain 0.985, half-life about 46 frames). Red and green trails therefore do not fade on flat content, so shapes can leave long, smeared streaks that slowly build up and turn warm or green. The recipe's own half-life figure of about 4.6 s is from the mean channel and does not describe red or green. How quickly the canvas fills with red and green is not settled, and I do not think the screen stays a clean dark background.

Colour. The shape colours depend on bass, mid and treble, so the palette is audio-driven and not fixed. The recipe does not resolve final colours for the shapes, the mesh or the shaders. The custom wave has a broad hue spread (blue 25%, green 25%, yellow 13%, pink 13%, plus smaller red, purple, orange and cyan), but that is a possibility, not the colours reached. The feedback steady state points to a green hue family (display about RGB 0.10, 0.20, 0.12), and the composite also changes the palette over time. Flat-canvas colours move between green-teal and a warmer, muted tone as time passes (the spread between sample times is large). I would expect mostly teal or green with changing tints, with some pastel or multicolour accents. The shapes' hues are an uncertain, audio-dependent random mix.

Audio reaction. Bass drives the zoom pulse directly (gain about 0.009 for bass and 0.0095 for attenuated bass), so low-frequency hits make the picture swell outward. Bass, mid and treble all feed shape radius, opacity and fill and perimeter colour, and each shape group's position follows an attenuated band. The shader warp colour also reads all three bands, with thresholds or clamps. Visible strength is unresolved: the recipe gives routes, not how strongly they show. The recipe lists coherent brightness transitions of about 4 Hz as a mechanism, not a proven strobe.

Brightness and activity. The calibrated display estimate gives mean luminance about 0.32 (interval 0.13 to 0.55), so mid-bright but very uncertain, with saturation about 0.44 (0.16 to 0.71). It also gives fairly high frame-to-frame change (about 0.07) and flow of about 0.19 screen widths per second. Brightness jumps are about 28 per 10 s, with a wide interval of 9 to 107. Its activity percentile is about 0.81, in the Intense band, though the interval is wide and spans Chill to Intense. These are calibrated estimates, not a rendering of this preset. Warm versus cool is balanced (about 0, interval from -0.34 to +0.35).

Random, chaotic or undefined. Shape positions and radii, and probably the custom wave's alpha, use rand, so each load and each frame differs and no fixed layout exists. The recipe lists no per-load random branch and no random palette redraw. Composite and warp output are not marked undefined (nonfinite share 0). The image-driven advection with noise makes the fine detail chaotic and hard to predict, so I describe the character, not exact structure. The picture is not symmetric or mirrored (symmetry score 0).
