LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r78 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a busy, glowing, liquid-looking feedback tunnel centred on the screen. The only freshly drawn things are a dotted stereo "XY" oscilloscope trace of the left and right audio channels (a cloud of dots, not a line, centred at 0.5,0.5), four polygon shapes (three pentagons with NDC radii about 0.39, 0.48 and 0.29, and one 15-sided near-circle of radius about 0.18, one instance each) and nothing else. Two of the pentagons have positions that depend on unresolved state, so where they sit is unknown. Their colours are not resolved in the recipe either, and none is guaranteed visible. Those seeds are then smeared and re-fed every frame by a custom warp shader that advects the picture along its own image (it samples a blurred copy of itself for the offsets, with small UV shifts of roughly -0.003 to 0.007 and larger ones of about -0.04 to 0.08 horizontally and -0.12 to 0.28 vertically), plus a radial zoom-exponent warp (zoom 1.0, zoomexp about 1.50). The composite then tiles the image (repeat tiling, not mirrored). I expect swirling, repeated, wobbling liquid ribbons and blobs rather than crisp shapes. The recipe records no mirror symmetry.

Colour: the displayed palette comes from the composite shader's flat-level ramp. Black stays black. Low canvas levels map to a dim blue-violet (about 0.22, 0.22, 0.33 at level 0.25). Mid levels become a clearer blue-teal (about 0.45, 0.52, 0.62). High levels go to near-white with a pink or lilac tint (about 1.0, 0.85, 1.0 at 0.75, and 1.0, 0.96, 1.0 at 1.0). This ramp is time-varying: the red and green channels move noticeably between sample times, with the largest spread at mid levels, so the tint drifts slowly rather than staying fixed. The settled feedback colour is blue-family, which fits a cool, blue/violet overall look with pale pink-white highlights. The dot trace changes colour with time (colour cycling, sinusoids at roughly 0.4 to 1 rad/s), which I read as roughly half a hue cycle per couple of seconds. Intent data gives a mildly cool balance (warm_cool about -0.13) and moderate-to-high saturation (about 0.63). The calibrated estimate (not a render) gives mean luminance about 0.31, with a wide interval of 0.13 to 0.55, so medium brightness with high uncertainty. Saturation is about 0.6 (0.32 to 0.87).

Feedback and motion: the flat-content warp transfer has per-frame gain of about 0.63, 0.59 and 0.63 for red, green and blue, plus a small offset of about 0.10 to 0.15. Content therefore loses roughly 40% per frame with a half-life of 1.4 frames (about 0.05 s), and flat areas settle around 0.3 of full level. This is not wrap or level cycling. Trails should be short and fast to fade, but the offset keeps a faint glow floor so the screen is not pure black. The blue channel is nonlinear, so dim and bright content persist differently: dim content is kept at about 1.06x at level 0.25 and bright content is cut to about 0.74x at level 1.0. Movement comes from the warp: bass, bass_att and treb_att feed a rotation term (a sine-based wobble plus a threshold on bass), and bass, mid, bass_att and treb_att feed horizontal and vertical translation. Several of these are thresholded or clamped. The recipe gives no linear gains, so I cannot say how strongly it moves. The estimate gives mean flow around 0.3 screen-widths per second (0.15 to 0.6), which is lively, and frame-to-frame change is fairly high.

Audio reaction: reactions are mostly threshold-like rather than smooth. Bass, mid and treble each drive the shape opacities (shapes 1 and 3), the warp and composite colour channels, and the warp rotation and translation. Bass and treble attack also move shape 2's position. The XY dot trace follows the actual waveform continuously. Because many routes are thresholded, expect pulses or jumps on beats rather than a smooth swell. The estimate is about 6 global brightness jumps per 10 s, but the interval is very wide (1.4 to 26), so I am unsure whether it flashes often. The source tags it Reactive and Flashing and gives it a high activity score (about 0.85 pack-relative, Intense, possibly Normal). It is not a calm preset, and the recipe's own gentle-TV profile lists it as violating the motion and flash limits.

Random and undefined parts: ten Q variables (q21 to q29 and q31) are redrawn with the random generator at about 3.1 s, 15.7 s, 28.3 s, 40.8 s and 53.4 s, then every 12.57 s. Anything reading them (probably shape or warp parameters, but the recipe does not say which) jumps to a new random value at those moments, so the exact layout and palette character differ per load and change every 12.6 s. No per-load rand_preset branch or hue-shader block is recorded. The initial values of the shapes' persistent state (ypos, yspeed, yamp and similar) are unresolved. The warp's translation expressions depend on mesh x/y and a modulo on the frame count, so there may be a periodic jolt every 15 frames, but I cannot confirm it. No stored-level decay or floor is exported (the recipe shows the warp shader replaces decay). I treat the exact positions, colours, density and flashing as estimates, not facts, and no nonfinite or undefined output is flagged.
