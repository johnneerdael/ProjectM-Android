Approximate look: a bright, pale and fairly pastel field of growing blobs, cells and swirls that spread over much of the screen, with brightly coloured waveform curves drawn over the middle. The calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.49 (interval 0.30 to 0.71). Bright and Pastel facet probabilities are 0.87 and 0.88, both well above their thresholds. Mean saturation is about 0.31 and the coloured share about 0.32 (interval 0 to 0.61), so a lot of the picture is pale or grey with colour in patches.

Pattern: the warp stage adds a finer copy of the fed-back image and subtracts a coarser blur every frame. This band-pass sharpening makes small seeds (the drawn waves) grow into spreading blobs, cells or splatter, so most of the screen should be patterned rather than a dark background with a few lines. This is the same look as a reaction-diffusion texture. The composite adds a periodic ripple or wobble displacement to the picture, and the composite itself leaves flat grey levels unchanged (it acts as a pass-through, with texture appearing grey), so colour comes from the drawn waves and the feedback.

The display palette lists green first, then blue and pink, with smaller red, purple, cyan, orange and yellow. The likely leading hue is green, with blue and pink next.
