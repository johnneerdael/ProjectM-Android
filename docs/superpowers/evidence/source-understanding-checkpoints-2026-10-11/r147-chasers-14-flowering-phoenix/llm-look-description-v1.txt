Approximate look: a warm, mostly dark-to-medium picture (calibrated mean luminance about 0.25, interval 0.06 to 0.49) built from clouds of small glowing dots in orange, red and pale yellow, laid out with strong four-fold mirror symmetry. The calibrated estimate gives fairly saturated colour (about 0.63) leaning warm (0.75). The source steady-state hint is grey, so a neutral or washed grey-white base showing through is possible. I trust the orange/red/yellow dot colours more than the grey.

Structures: four custom waves of 512 points each. They are orange (two layers, one faint), red, and a pale yellow. They are drawn at about 50% alpha, so they read as dotted traces or point clouds, not solid lines. A built-in stereo-trace dot wave sits at screen centre but has alpha 0.001, so it is effectively invisible. Three polygons sit behind or around them. A very large 36-sided textured white disc at 50% alpha covers the whole screen and probably adds a soft texture wash. A mid-sized textured square, radius about 0.8, drifts slowly up and down by about 0.1 over a 63-second cycle. A small dark 30-sided circle rotates at 1 rad/s and moves with bass, mid and treble. The texture contents are unknown.

Motion and trails: every feedback step flips the image top to bottom. The composite adds a left-right mirrored copy at 50%, scaled 1.017x. This gives kaleidoscope-like symmetry, inexact because of zoom and drawing. The zoom varies across the screen, with negative values from -1.35 to -0.68, and its visible effect is uncertain. There is no net drift, and no rotation in the warp. Trails are short. A band covering about 14% of the screen edge is cleared to black every frame. An inner band (about 63%) retains 98% of content per frame, tinted faintly red, with a half-life of about 1.2 s. Expect a moderate, flowing, mostly slow picture, and low activity (Chill likely, Normal possible).

Audio: bass changes the warp zoom and scale. Bass, mid and treble move the small dark circle. Wave point positions depend on bass, mid and treb, so the dot clouds move with the music. Gains are mostly unknown, so reaction strength is unresolved.

Flashing: there is no found source of beat flashes or colour cycling. Calibrated brightness jumps are about 0.3 per 10 s, with an upper interval of 3.8. Strobing is unlikely but unverified.
