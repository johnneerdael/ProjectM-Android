An energetic, organic, liquid-looking picture whose zoom pumps hard with the music, grown from an amber waveform. A single angled waveform trace in additive amber (red 1, green 0.75, blue 0) is drawn at the centre. It is normalised to full brightness at 0.8 alpha. That line seeds a warp shader that sharpens the fed-back image by adding a fine level and subtracting a blurred one. Small seeds grow into blobs, cells and splatter that spread over most of the screen, rather than a dark background with one line. The same shader steers the image along its own gradients (fluid, reaction-diffusion flow), applies an emboss-like relief pass, and the composite adds another relief. Edges and texture stand out as lit ridges.

The mesh zoom is driven by all six bass, mid and treble inputs, with a range of about 0.41 to 1.59. That is a swing of up to +/-58% per frame. The whole picture can lurch inward or outward sharply with the sound. This is the main beat response. Trails are channel-selective: red is kept almost whole, green mostly, and blue is cleared within a few frames. The feedback canvas therefore leans yellow, with a small offset so it never settles fully to black. The composite multiplies by four corner shades that cycle with periods of about 15, 20 and 16 s. The hues drift and their placement is random per load, so the displayed picture may lean blue, green, pink, purple or red from load to load. The steady colour comes out as pale blue-grey.

Calibrated estimate: mean luminance about 0.44 (0.26-0.66), saturation about 0.38, about two thirds of the picture coloured. Green, pink, blue and purple are the leading hues, with warm and cool balanced. Flow is fast (about 0.28 screen widths per second) with strong frame change. The no-strobe probability is only 0.05, so expect flicker, with about 2 brightness jumps per 10 s (range 0-10). It is not strobe-free. Activity is Intense. Uncertain: the hue of the border (2% of the screen) and the exact colour of any single load.
