LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r94 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. The recipe describes a feedback preset with one drawn element: a built-in audio waveform, mode 7, "two_channel_parallel_traces" (left and right channel), thick lines, not dots, not additive, centred at wave_x 0.5 / wave_y 0.5 with line angle 0 and a separation of 0.25 between the two traces. So I expect two roughly horizontal, audio-shaped wavy lines sitting above and below the screen centre, about a quarter of the screen apart. The lines are only the seed: the previous frame is resampled through an angularly periodic warp every frame. Zoom varies with the polar angle (about 0.85 to 1.05, around a mean of 0.95, with a sine of ang*10 in it), and there is a small uniform rotation of at most +/-0.0125 rad per step. After 5 seconds I expect the lines to be smeared into a swirling, rotating, radially lobed streak pattern around the centre, not two clean lines. The recipe lists the mirror/symmetry score as 0 and a symmetry mechanism estimate of 0.6, so any apparent symmetry is weak and inexact. There are no custom shapes, no motion vectors, no tiling and no random textures. A 1% outer border and a 9.7% inner border are blended black every frame, so a dark frame or vignette is likely at the screen edge and content passing through it is wiped out.

Colour. The wave colour is authored as pure white (r=g=b=1), and max-normalisation keeps it white. The recipe's own steady-state hue family is "grayscale" and its palette block gives saturation 0 and zero hue bins, so from source alone this is a black, white and grey picture, with no hue cycling. The one conflict is the display_estimate, which gives mean saturation 0.42 (interval 0.14 to 0.69) and warm/cool near neutral (-0.006, interval -0.34 to +0.35). That estimate is a statistical fit, not a source fact. I trust the source and expect greyscale, but cannot rule out some tint from the fit or from a source part the recipe does not resolve (the mesh_warp colour palette is listed as unresolved).

Motion. The feedback decay is 1.0, meaning no fade from the decay control, so trails persist. The warp is a rotation of up to 0.0125 rad per step on a sine of fps/8, plus an angle-dependent zoom, plus whole-image translation dx/dy of up to 0.039 UV per step. That is up to about 563 px/s in the recipe's nominal units, as a ceiling and not a typical speed. The picture should therefore swirl and drift continuously, with slow rotation and breathing zoom. The mood block's p95 speed is about 0.84 viewport widths/s (interval 0.83 to 1.69), and display_estimate puts mean flow around 0.28 screen widths/s (0.13 to 0.55), so moderate, clearly moving but not frantic. The recipe has no audio threshold switches, so I expect no abrupt cuts.

Brightness and contrast. This is the least certain part. The wave alpha is only 0.001, which draws very faint lines, but gamma_weight 2 brightens the final image by up to 2x, decay 1.0 lets content accumulate, and the source steady state is a dim grey (display about 0.03, flagged as a lower bound). The display_estimate gives mean luminance 0.35 (0.17 to 0.59), so I expect a mid-dark to mid-grey image with visible white-grey streaks on black, with moderate contrast, not a bright or saturated picture. Flashing: the recipe finds no flash route (coherent transitions 0 Hz), though the display estimate allows about 1 global brightness jump per 10 s (0 to 6.5). I do not expect strobing.

Audio response. The waveform shape follows the left and right audio channels directly, so the two lines wiggle with the music. Bass, mid and treble, both raw and smoothed, all feed the warp translation (dx, dy), and bass also feeds zoom. The gain is not resolved, but the smoothed bands have small weights (bass_att 0.4, mid/treb 0.1 and similar, bass 1.2 on the raw term), so the picture should drift and lurch with the music, bass most, as a continuous response rather than a hard beat flash. There is no audio brightness route (bass RGB effect 0). A quiet or silent input would leave a slow rotation with the lines flat.

Uncertainty. Unknown or unresolved in the recipe: the final palette and any tint; mean luminance from source (only the estimate); actual trail length (decay 1.0 with faint residue, so persistence could be long, but the border wipes and the 0.001 wave alpha limit how much builds up); how dense the swirl pattern becomes; the sizes of the translation and zoom terms at typical audio levels (the ceiling values are large); and how much the lines remain visible against the accumulated feedback. The activity estimate is wide (0.72, interval 0.11 to 0.94, Normal or Intense, Chill not excluded). The mood block's own band is Normal, and the strict source model has no eligible mood. Moods here are uncalibrated.
