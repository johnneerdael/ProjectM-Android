LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r92 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This is a dark, feedback-driven picture built from two thin, translucent custom-wave curves (wave_0 and wave_1, 512 points each, sample = j/(N-1)) that are redrawn every frame and fed back through a warp. Their point positions are closed-form sums of sines and cosines of the sample index and time, with a slow time term and a bass/beat input, so I expect an interlaced, looping Lissajous-like or rosette ribbon wandering around the screen centre (the recipe uses 0.5 offsets and sample multipliers up to about 8), not a single circle or a particle field. The recipe gives no position bounds (position_bounds is null), so I cannot say how large the loop is or whether it clips at the edges. The feedback stage is a nonlinear warp shader (a complex-quadratic, fractal-like map of the previous frame) plus a uniform 0.995 zoom about the centre (0.5 percent contraction per frame, no rotation, no translation, zoomexp 1). The composite adds a periodic ripple or wobble displacement (uv_advection). The result should look like curling, smeared, mildly fractal ribbons that slowly drift inward and are rippled, with a thin black border (about 1 percent of the screen) redrawn every frame. A strong kaleidoscope or mirror effect is not supported (mirror symmetry score 0.0, estimate upper 0.4).

Colour. The curves start from authored base colours of roughly (0.1, 1.0, 0.7) and (0.2, 1.0, 0.6), which are green and teal. Their per-point colours come from sine programs of time and sample and are not constant. The recipe lists hue shares of green 21 percent, orange 21 percent, blue 21 percent, cyan 14 percent, pink 14 percent and purple 7 percent, with mean saturation about 0.91 and mean alpha about 0.33. So the lines should be fairly saturated and semi-transparent, with several hues present along the length and drifting over time. The mean RGB across the points is near grey (0.48, 0.53, 0.50), so the overall tint is roughly neutral to slightly green. The settled steady-state hue family is green (RGB about 0.003, a lower bound only). The warp and composite palettes are unresolved. The composite flat transfer is identity grey, so it adds no tint of its own. The calibrated display estimate gives mean saturation 0.47 (interval 0.19 to 0.74) and warm/cool about neutral (-0.03). Which colours dominate is unknown.

Motion. Content contracts toward the screen centre at 0.5 percent per frame, so features slowly sink inward. Trails fade quickly: the warp gain is 0.94 per frame on all channels, a half-life of about 11 frames (about 0.4 s on the recipe's timing), and flat areas decay to black. Trails should therefore be short and soft, not long smears, but the nonlinear warp can fold and curl what is drawn. The curves themselves keep moving because their equations depend on time and the bass/beat inputs. The recipe estimates a p95 flow of about 0.30 viewport widths per second (interval 0.05 to 0.60), which is partly assumed, and a calibrated mean flow of about 0.09 screen widths per second (interval 0.02 to 0.22). Mean frame-to-frame difference is about 0.035. I would call it smooth, gently flowing motion with some ripple, not fast. No audio-triggered scene switches were found (discontinuities 0 Hz, but the recipe notes that absence is not proof).

Brightness and contrast. Expect a mostly dark picture. The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.14 (interval 0.0 to 0.38). The flat background is black, and only the semi-transparent curves and their short fading trails light it. Contrast is high locally, with saturated thin lines on near-black, but the amount of lit area is small. I would not expect much sustained bright coverage. The activity estimate is 0.22 (Chill, but Normal and Intense are still possible given the wide interval), and the approximate mood tags are Smooth, Psychedelic (score about 69), Fractal and Dark. Brightness jumps are estimated at about 0.7 per 10 s (interval 0 to 5.4), so I do not expect a strobe.

Audio response. The only established audio route is bass into the red, green and blue output of the warp shader, so loud bass changes the brightness or colour of the fed-back image. The gain is not resolved (linear_gain is null), and the assumed peak RGB effect is only about 0.035. The curve equations also read bass and the beat input (bt), so the line shape probably moves with bass, but the recipe gives no gain and does not establish visible strength or beat synchronisation. Mid and treble routes were not found. The flash estimates (about 2.8 to 4 Hz, delta 0.1 to 0.3) are labelled assumed, so I cannot claim visible pulses.

Uncertainty. The confidence is low. The recipe does not give the curve size or shape, final dominant colours, whether the warp's fractal fold is prominent or subtle, or the real strength of the ripple. Mood values are editable assumptions. Several motion, flash and structure numbers are marked assumed, and the calibrated display statistics are estimates with wide intervals. If the warp amplifies strongly, the picture could be busier and brighter than I describe. If the curves have low alpha or small amplitude, it could be nearly black with faint ribbons. This is not a rendered measurement, and the recipe is an approximate source description.
