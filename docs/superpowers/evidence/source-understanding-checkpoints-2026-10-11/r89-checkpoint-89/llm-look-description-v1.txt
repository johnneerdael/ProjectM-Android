LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r89 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a mostly dark, organic, fluid-looking feedback canvas rather than a clean geometric figure. Three shape layers and one custom line wave are drawn into a feedback buffer that a custom warp shader keeps smearing. The shapes are a 12-sided shape repeated 512 times, a single 50-sided shape (nearly a circle) fixed at screen centre with radius about 0.17 and a faint 0.6 outer alpha, and a 20-sided shape repeated 1024 times. The 1024-copy layer is the main moving element. Its centre positions come from long layered sin/cos expressions in time and per-instance index, with a nominal x range of about 0.125 to 0.875 and a y range of about -0.33 to 1.33, so some copies can sit off-screen. Its radius is tiny (about 0.01 times instance times 0.0041), so it should read as a swarm or cloud of small, very faint dots or blobs (alpha 0.03) rather than large solid polygons. The 512-copy layer has positions and radii read from a global memory buffer that the recipe cannot resolve. I do not know where those copies sit. The custom wave's points and colour are unresolved, so I cannot say where the line goes or how bright it is.

Motion and feedback: the warp stage combines gradient-driven flow, self-advection (the image steering its own sampling coordinates via a blur sampler), noise-driven displacement and a log-radius polar mapping. The recipe warns this is not guaranteed to look like a tunnel. Expect smeared, flowing, reaction-diffusion-like structures. The per-frame sampling offsets are small (about 0.003 to 0.008 of the texture), so it should look like a slow-to-moderate swirl. The built-in zoom, rotation and translation are all neutral, so the motion comes from the shader. The mesh sampling ceiling is exported as 0 and the shape-1 trajectory bound is 0, which does not mean the picture is still. The estimated flow is about 0.04 screen widths per second, with a wide interval of 0 to 0.14. Trail persistence differs by channel (the warp is channel-selective). Green is kept whole on flat content (gain 1.0, steady level 1.0, nonlinear), red fades with a half-life of about 12.7 frames, and blue fades fast, with a half-life of about 4.1 frames. Over time the retained content should drift green or yellow-green, with red as a slower-fading secondary and blue short-lived. The warp's own transfer on flat colours gives a pale, slightly green-weighted grey ramp. A flat-field model says empty areas decay to black, and the composite shows flat levels as pure black.

Colours: the colour sources are unresolved in several places. The recipe lists wave_0 as possibly red, shape_1 as grayscale, and shape_2 as blue, purple or pink (r 0.2, g 0, b 0.7 fading to r2 1, b2 1 magenta, at low alpha). The warp and composite shader palettes are unresolved. The composite flat-transfer maps every flat canvas level to black, which means flat regions are dark but says nothing about textured or bright regions. The mood features give an estimated mean saturation of about 0.26 to 0.30 (a muted palette) and roughly three effective hue bins, with warm/cool near neutral. I would expect a low-saturation picture of dark background with greenish retained smears and faint purple or magenta specks. This is a guess from ingredients and not a verified palette.

Audio: the only established routes are bass, mid and treble each feeding the x and y position of the 1024-copy layer, so the swarm's layout shifts continuously with the music. The gains are null, so the strength is unknown. No audio route to brightness, colour or radius was found, and no threshold or beat-switch sites were found (absence is not proof). I would not expect whole-screen pulses or flashes. The flash and brightness-jump estimates are near 0, with an upper interval of about 1.9 jumps per 10 s.

Brightness and mood: the calibrated display estimate says dim, with mean luminance about 0.056 (interval 0 to 0.29), frame-to-frame change about 0.007 (up to 0.04) and activity percentile about 0.009. That puts it in the Chill band, with Normal also possible. This is a source-calibrated estimate and not a measurement of this preset. The loose tags (Psychedelic, Geometric, Organic/Liquid) are approximate interpretation only.

Random, chaotic or undefined: ten state values (q21 to q29 and q31) are redrawn with rand first at about 3.13 s and then every 12.57 s (about 15.7 s, 28.3 s, 40.8 s, 53.4 s and so on). Anything that reads them, probably shape layout, colours or thresholds, will re-roll at those moments, so the picture can change character abruptly at those times. I cannot say which visual attributes depend on them. There are no per-load random textures. The warp uses noise samplers for displacement, so a turbulent shimmer is expected. The wave, the 512-shape layer, the exact palette, trail density and how much of the canvas is filled all remain unresolved.
