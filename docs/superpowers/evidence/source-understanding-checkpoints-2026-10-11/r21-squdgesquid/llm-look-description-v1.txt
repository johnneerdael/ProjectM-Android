LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r21 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):
Construction: a feedback-driven picture. A built-in two-channel waveform (mode 7, two parallel audio traces, additive) plus four custom 512-point waveforms are drawn into a feedback buffer that a custom warp shader smears (image-driven self-advection plus noise-texture displacement), so structure is flowing, smeared and fluid-like rather than crisp geometry.
Built-in trace: its authored alpha is a constant 0.001, so as a direct drawing it is probably very faint; its colour drifts slowly through broad hues (r/g/b each 0.6-1.1, periods 14.4/11.5/8.4 s; green, pink, blue most common over 120 s). Displayed colour is unverified.
Custom point layers: wave_0 cyan-ish, wave_1 pink, wave_2 and wave_3 grayscale/white, each about 0.1 alpha. Point positions are not closed-form, so where and how densely they land is unresolved; independent particles are not established.
Motion: the whole feedback image rotates up to +/-0.01 rad per step (sinusoidal in time, slow sign changes), with the sampling centre wandering about 0.29-0.71 and a small drift (dx/dy up to about 0.003 plus unresolved residuals). Zoom is about 1.009 plus a mid-band term; shader sampling offsets are small (under about 0.5% to 0.8% of the frame). Decay and trail length are unknown (null), so trail persistence is unresolved.
Audio: attenuated bass shifts the built-in trace position (thresholded, smoothed); attenuated mid modulates zoom (clamped). Strength on screen is unresolved; no beat-synchronised flash is established.
The calibrated display estimate (not a measurement of this preset) points to a middling picture: mean luminance about 0.21 (wide interval 0.01-0.46), low-moderate frame-to-frame change, saturation fairly high, slightly warm, activity about 0.47 (Normal, but Chill to Intense are all possible).
Unresolved: feedback decay, actual hue mix on screen, point-cloud coverage, how visible the faint built-in trace is, brightness spikes (estimate 0 to about 4 per 10 s).
Expected: a moderately dim-to-medium, softly flowing, swirling smear of colour, rotating slowly with a pink/cyan/white speckled point-cloud texture and a faint drifting-hue wave line, gentle to moderate motion; details uncertain.
