Expected look: a left-right mirror-symmetric, geometric picture built from nested, rotated triangle copies that recede toward the centre like a droste tunnel. I expect it to be fairly bright and slow, but I am unsure about its colour and its overall brightness.

Structure. The only drawn element is one 3-sided shape at the centre (0.5, 0.5) with an NDC radius of 1.37, so the triangle is larger than the screen and its corners run off it. It is a frame copy. It draws the previous frame inside itself, with a white tint (r, g, b = 1), texture zoom 0.366 and texture angle of about 90 degrees (1.5708 rad). Each frame, the last frame is therefore re-drawn shrunk to roughly a third of its size and turned a quarter turn, inside a triangular window. The copies nest recursively into a rotating, shrinking tunnel of triangles. The shape's own rotation is audio-driven (bass and smoothed bass), so the nested triangles should turn faster or slower with the music. The size of this effect is unknown. Nothing else is drawn. There is no waveform and no custom shader.

Feedback and motion. The warp has a negative zoom (-0.995) and a horizontal stretch of 0.99. The negative value flips the sampling point through the centre, so the retained image is inverted every step. This makes the picture swap or alternate. I cannot predict the exact visible effect. The stored level decay is 1.0, so content does not fade, except in thin black borders at the edges (outer about 2%, inner about 1%), which should show as a dark frame. Because the decay is 1.0, structure persists and accumulates. The mean flow is low, about 0.07 screen widths per second.

Composite. A legacy composite brightens the image with a gamma of 1.85. It also adds a left-right mirrored copy at 50% opacity, so the picture should have two-fold mirror symmetry.

Colour and brightness. There is no coloured source. The drawn shape is white-tinted, so any colour comes from the calibrated estimate only. It gives moderate saturation (about 0.30) with a weak red lead (red about 22%, cyan 16%), so the hue is a guess. The Bright facet is admitted (0.77) with mean luminance of about 0.60, but the source steady-state is near black. I trust the calibrated estimate, with low confidence. Still and Chill are admitted. Pastel is not.

Flashing. no_strobe_probability is 0.90, so occasional brightness pulses are likely. The expected rate is under 1 per 10 s, with the interval running up to about 3.
