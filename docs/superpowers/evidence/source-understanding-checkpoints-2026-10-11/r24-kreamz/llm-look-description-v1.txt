LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r24 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):
The picture is built from a small cluster of drawn points in the middle of the screen (wave_0: 512 points, all inside x 0.40-0.60, y 0.40-0.60, closed-form positions, alpha 1), so the drawn content is a compact central figure, not a full-screen pattern.
Its colour ingredients are mostly blue (47%) and yellow (46%), with a little purple (6%) and green (1%), mean saturation 0.73; they depend on sample position and bass (render.layers wave_0), so the figure should read as two-tone blue/yellow beads or a ring.
A built-in audio-deformed circle (waveform_right, centred 0.5/0.5, grey 0.5/0.5/0.5) is declared but its authored alpha is 0.001, so it is probably close to invisible.
Trails are very short: the warp shader's flat-colour transfer keeps about 0.45 of the previous frame (halving in ~0.88 frames, no offset, no floor), so content should fade almost at once instead of smearing long.
The feedback map has radius-dependent zoom (0.77 to 1.11 across the screen), no rotation, no constant drift (dx = dy = 0), so any motion is radial stretching about the centre, not spinning or sliding.
A periodic wobble/ripple displacement in the warp stage and an image-driven self-advection in the composite (UV offsets 0.4-1.0) add flowing, smeared distortion on top of that.
Bass drives red, green and blue through clamped/threshold routes in the shader warp, so brightness or tint may jump with bass; strength is unresolved.
A nominal brightness-cycle rate of 14.32 Hz exists in the warp shader; whether it shows as visible flashing is unverified.
Unresolved: final palette and dominance (warp and composite palettes unknown), how much of the screen the figure and distortion cover, visible speed, and the real size of the bass response.
No strict mood group is eligible; any mood is only an approximate reading from the recipe.
Expected: a small blue/yellow dotted figure in the centre of a mostly dark screen, quickly fading trails, radial flowing/rippling distortion and bass-linked colour or brightness shifts.
