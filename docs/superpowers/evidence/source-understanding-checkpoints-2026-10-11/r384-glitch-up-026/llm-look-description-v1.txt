Approximate look: a fairly bright, saturated, colourful and organic-looking picture where the whole screen is patterned, not a dark background with a few lines. Estimated mean luminance is about 0.40 (interval 0.22 to 0.61) and saturation about 0.66, with roughly 95 percent of the screen coloured.

How it is built: a single custom waveform of 512 points, authored saturated blue (about 0.05, 0.15, 1.0, full alpha), is drawn each frame as a seed. Its positions depend on audio and on random values that are redrawn during the run, so the shape and placement are unknown. The warp shader then feeds the image back through a band-pass pattern-growth step (a finer level of the image added, a coarser blur subtracted) combined with image-driven self-advection. Seeds like this grow into blobs, cells, splatter and edge patterns that spread over the canvas, and flow in smeared, fluid-like ways. The composite then remaps the result through a log-radius polar lookup with repeat tiling and further self-advection. A tunnel or radial look is a candidate, but it is not guaranteed. I would expect swirling, tiled, cell-like texture that may show repeated tiles or radial structure around the centre. A thin dark-red border (about 1 percent) frames the screen.

Colour: the colours are uncertain. The pooled palette leans green, then blue and pink, with no dominant hue (green about 0.26, blue 0.21 in the calibrated shares). The composite multiplies by four slowly cycling corner tints (periods roughly 15, 16 and 20 seconds), and which hue appears where is random for each load, so the picture's colour changes between loads and drifts over time. The feedback keeps red whole (the red channel is not faded), fades green slowly (halving in about 50 frames) and clears blue almost every frame. So the stored canvas tends toward red, but the displayed steady colour comes out green after the composite. The blue seeds should therefore look sharp and short-lived, with trails in other colours. This hue mapping is an estimate.

Motion: the mesh-warp translation shifts with bass, mid and treble (thresholded routes), so the pattern drifts and jolts with the music. Zoom and rotation are neutral. Estimated flow is moderate, about 0.12 screen widths per second, and the picture is likely to read as drifting and ordinary in energy (Normal, Chill possible).

Flashing: the no-strobe probability is only about 0.49, so I would not call it strobe-free. Estimated brightness jumps are about 0.6 per 10 seconds, with a range of 0 to about 5, giving occasional pulses on loud bass-driven colour changes.
