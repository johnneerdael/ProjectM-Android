Expect a dark screen with a ring of bright coloured dots whose past positions stream outward in glowing, curling trails. The only drawn element is a built-in "audio deformed circle" wave. It is drawn as thick dots, not a line, on a circle of radius about 0.4 (in model units) that rotates at roughly 0.2 rad/s. The right-channel audio waveform pushes the dots in and out, so the ring wobbles in shape with the music. Smoothed bass moves the wave's centre (wave_x and wave_y), so the whole ring drifts slightly with the bass. The dot colour cycles quickly through the spectrum. The source channels swing between about -0.2 and 1.1 and are then scaled so the brightest channel is always full. That gives vivid, saturated dots whose hue sweeps through green, orange, pink, yellow, red, blue, purple and cyan (green is the most common, about 30%). The hue-change rate is up to about 2 cycles per second, so colour change along the ring and over time should be quick.

Motion: each feedback step the whole image expands from near the screen centre by about 3.2% (zoom 1.032), with a small oscillating rotation of up to plus or minus 0.01 rad per step and a slightly wandering zoom centre. Previous frames are kept with a half-life of about 138 frames (roughly 4.6 s). The dots should therefore be dragged outward into long radial streaks that bend into a spiral, giving a warp-speed or starburst look. The recipe also marks the warp and composite as self-advecting (the image steers its own sampling), with a gradient-driven flow in the composite that can move content by up to 0.27 of the screen in UV. Expect smeared, fluid, organic shapes in the trails. Calibrated flow is about 0.15 screen widths per second, which is moderate.

Brightness and colour overall: calibrated mean luminance is low, about 0.10 (range 0 to 0.32), and the dark facet is admitted (0.71 against a 0.58 threshold). Saturation is high (about 0.77) where colour appears. About half the screen carries colour (0.53, range 0.21 to 0.81). The pooled palette is blue, green, pink and yellow with no dominant hue (dominance 0.43). The composite multiplies by four cycling corner shades with periods of roughly 15 to 20 s, whose placement is random per load. So the hue balance and the brightness may differ from one load to another. The composite's own response to grey detail is dim and blue-leaning.

Audio and flashing: reactions are limited to the ring's shape and position. No brightness route from bass, mid or treble was found. The no-strobe probability is 0.94 and calibrated brightness jumps are about 0 per 10 s (up to about 2.2), so it should look steady, maybe with occasional pulses. Activity is Chill to Normal.

Uncertain: how bright the ring is against the dark background, how far the trails reach before fading, and which hues show at a given moment.
