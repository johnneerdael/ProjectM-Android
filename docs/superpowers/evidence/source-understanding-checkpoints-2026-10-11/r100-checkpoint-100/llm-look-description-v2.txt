LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (r100 v2; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. About five seconds after load, the recipe says the whole screen is one flat colour: plain white. The feedback steady state is marked exact (render.feedback.steady_state.exact = true). Nothing feeds the canvas: the warp transfer on flat content gives gain 0 and offset 0 for every channel, so the decay is total and the canvas sits at black (canvas_local_rgb 0,0,0). The recipe lists no drawn layers except the composite shader (no waves, shapes, borders or motion-vector grid). The composite's flat transfer (render.composite.flat_transfer) returns (1,1,1) for every canvas level from 0 to 1, with zero spread across the sample times 2, 5 and 8 s and no non-finite output. So the display is display_local_rgb = (1,1,1) everywhere, and the recipe states "the whole display is this colour". There are no rings, trails, patterns or shapes to describe. By the skill's rule I do not describe texture, structure or a display_estimate brightness for this case.

Colour. White, with hue family "grayscale". There is no palette, tint or colour variation in the exact steady state. The composite does carry colour-targeting audio expressions and random q values, but its flat output does not depend on them.

Motion. The warp map expands from the centre by about 0.15% to 4.5% per step (zoom 1.01, zoomexp 0.227), has a rotation term that depends on radius and time and is not fully resolved, and has zero translation. Because the canvas is black and uniform and the composite output is constant white, none of this movement has any visible content to move. I would expect no visible motion: a static white field. The recipe's motion numbers are sampling-map facts, not observed movement.

Brightness and contrast. Maximal brightness and no contrast: every pixel is (1,1,1). The calibrated display_estimate in the recipe (mean luminance about 0.67, with a wide interval) does not apply here, because the exact steady state overrides it. The recipe's flashing records (a nominal brightness-cycle rate of about 0.0796 Hz in the warp and composite, plus the mood block's coherent-transition estimate of about 4.3 Hz) are source mechanisms, not shown flashes. With a constant white output they should not produce visible flashing.

Audio response. The recipe lists nine routes from bass, mid and treble to the composite's red, green and blue colour. All are thresholded or clamped, with no linear gain. They sit inside random-draw selectors. Their visible strength is unresolved. Since the composite output is constant white in the flat model, I expect little or no visible audio response: the screen stays white whatever the music does. Q values q21 to q29 and q31 are redrawn randomly at about 3.1 s and then every 12.6 s. The recipe only supports an effect if those draws change what the composite shows.

Uncertainty. The recipe is source-only and approximate (uses_rendered_images is false). The white result rests on the exactness claim for flat canvas content. The composite also samples a procedural 3D noise texture (noisevol_hq) and has a periodic spatial displacement mechanism, and the recipe does not say whether these could add structure over a non-flat canvas. With the canvas at black and nothing drawing into it, I take them to have no effect. The mood and activity numbers in the recipe (Chill/Normal bands, activity 0.34, "Flashing" tag) conflict with a static white screen and should be read as source potential, not as what is displayed. If the exactness claim fails, the look would be unknown and could differ a lot.
