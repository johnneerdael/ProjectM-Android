LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r100 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. The recipe lists no drawn shapes, waves, borders or motion-vector grid. The only layer is one custom composite shader (mechanism uv_advection, "a periodic spatial displacement field (ripples or wobble)"), and it samples a 3D noise-volume texture (noisevol_hq, linear, wrapped). So about 5 seconds in I expect a full-screen field with no discrete objects: a soft, fine-grained noise texture that ripples or wobbles slowly, rather than lines, rings or particles. The warp stage adds a gentle expansion from the screen centre: zoom 1.01 with zoomexp 0.227 gives an effective 0.15% to 4.5% growth per frame, with the strongest end set by the radius dependence. The recipe also gives rotation as a function of radius and time, a small amplitude (0.005 factor with a sine of time) combined with an EEL bitwise-and, which the recipe could not resolve. Any twist is therefore slight and its direction is unknown. Translation (dx, dy) is zero and the centre is (0.5, 0.5), so there is no sideways drift. No mirror or kaleidoscope symmetry is supported (symmetry score 0.0). The overall image is organic and noisy, not geometric.

Colour. The recipe does not resolve the palette: the composite palette is unresolved and hue and saturation are null. Two indirect cues exist. The steady-state estimate and the flat composite transfer both come out as white or grayscale (1,1,1 at every flat canvas level), so the display is not forced to black. The calibrated display estimate gives low mean saturation, about 0.18 (10th to 90th percentile 0 to 0.45), and a warm/cool balance near neutral (0.01, range -0.32 to +0.37). I would therefore describe it as mostly pale, grey-white to lightly tinted, with at most a faint cast. Colour is not strongly vivid. Colour channels are modulated by bass, mid and treble through thresholded expressions that depend on randomly redrawn values, so a faint hue may appear and shift. I cannot name a hue.

Motion. Expect a slow to moderate, smooth, breathing motion: an outward zoom flow from the centre plus a rippling wobble in the noise. The calibrated mean flow is about 0.08 screen widths per second (0.007 to 0.20), and the mean frame-to-frame change is small, about 0.043 (0.012 to 0.090). The recipe's smoothness score is 88 out of 100. Trails and persistence are essentially absent. The warp transfer gain is 0 for all channels, with a stored-level half-life of about one frame, so the previous frame is not carried forward as a smear. What you see is mostly the current frame's composite, not accumulated streaks. A periodic nominal brightness cycle of 0.0796 Hz (about 12.6 s) appears in both the warp and composite stages. Ten q-values are redrawn at random at about 3.1 s and then every 12.6 s. At 5 s one redraw has already happened. Anything that depends on those values (offsets, ripple phase and tint) will have jumped to a new random state once, and will change again near 15.7 s.

Brightness and contrast. The display estimate puts mean luminance at about 0.67 (0.49 to 0.91), so a fairly bright picture, not a dark one with a few bright elements. Contrast is unknown. The flat composite output is white, which suggests the texture detail appears as variation around a light base, but the recipe says nothing about depth or contrast. The calibrated activity percentile is about 0.34 (Chill to Normal, wide interval 0.03 to 0.84). Calibrated global brightness jumps are about 4 per 10 s (0.7 to 18), so occasional brightness shifts are plausible. The recipe's own coherent-transition estimate of about 4.3 Hz is mostly assumption, and I cannot treat it as a verified strobe.

Audio response. Bass, mid and treble each feed the red, green and blue colour terms of the composite. All nine routes are thresholded or clamped, not smooth linear gains, and none is smoothed, so the colour reacts in steps rather than gradually. The assumed per-band RGB effect is small, about 0.05, and the displayed strength is unresolved. No audio route is recorded for zoom, rotation or geometry, so the motion should be mostly independent of the music, while tint and brightness respond to it. Audio-driven drawing is unknown because there is no drawn element.

Uncertainty. The recipe is source-only and approximate. It does not state the composite's actual palette, the noise scale or contrast, or how strong the ripples are. The rotation term could not be evaluated (bitwise-and), the mood speed, acceleration and flash values are partly assumed, and the random q redraws mean the exact look differs per load and after about 3.1 s. The white flat-field result could also mean a washed-out look, so the dominant risk is that I am wrong about brightness and colour. The description above is the most likely reading: a bright, pale, finely textured, softly rippling and slowly expanding full-screen noise field with little trailing.
