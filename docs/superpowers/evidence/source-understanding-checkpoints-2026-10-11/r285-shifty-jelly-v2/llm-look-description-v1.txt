Expected look: a dark screen with a bright, colour-cycling waveform line through the centre. The line is smeared by a swirling, outward-stretching, liquid-looking feedback, leaving short coloured trails on a mostly black background. I expect it to be smooth rather than jittery, and not very bright overall.

Drawn element. The only drawn element is the built-in "angled_waveform_trace" (mode 6), centred at (0.5, 0.5) at line angle 0, so it should be a roughly horizontal trace that follows the left-channel audio waveform. It is thick and drawn alpha-over. Its colour changes slowly over time (raw r, g and b each between 0.35 and 1.05, then normalised so the brightest channel is full). So the line stays vivid while its hue drifts through green, pink, blue, orange and cyan, with a little grey. Colour changes at no more than about 0.33 cycles per second.

Feedback and motion. The warp zoom is exactly 1 at the centre and rises toward about 1.11 at the edge (zoomexp about 1.21), so the picture expands outward, faster near the edges. There is a swirl of up to plus or minus 0.03 rad per step and a moderate warp distortion (0.26). The zoom and rotation centre wanders within roughly 0.39 to 0.61. The composite adds gradient-driven and image-driven (self-) advection, which smears the picture into a fluid, organic flow. Trails are short, with a half-life of about 0.5 s (decay 0.966). The decay is not linear: dim content below about 6% brightness disappears quickly, so faint residue does not build up and the background stays near black. Calibrated flow is about 0.25 screen widths per second, which is moderate.

Colour and brightness. Mean luminance is low, about 0.13 (range 0 to 0.35), and the Dark facet is admitted (0.80). The composite maps flat canvas to nearly black, and a coloured texture comes out dim, so colour appears only where the line and its smears are. The calibrated saturation is high, about 0.81, and the coloured share is about 41%. Blue leads the hue estimates (about 21%), ahead of green and red (about 15% each), but the lead is small, so I would call it blue with green and red close behind. A random tint is applied at each load, so the dominant hue varies.

Audio. Only the waveform shape reacts, continuously. There is no band-driven brightness or position route.

Flashing. no_strobe_probability is 0.88, with a brightness-jump estimate of about 0 per 10 s (interval up to about 2). I expect mostly steady output, with occasional pulses possible.
