Approximate look: a dark, blue-led kaleidoscopic feedback picture. The calibrated mean luminance is low (about 0.12, interval 0 to 0.34) and the dark facet is admitted (probability 0.75 against a 0.58 threshold). The composite folds the image into a four-fold mirror, left-right and top-bottom about the screen centre. The result is a symmetric, mandala-like or tiled pattern, with the symmetry slightly inexact because of zoom and drawing.

What feeds the pattern: a small dotted ring from the built-in audio-deformed circle, drawn in pink-magenta (1.0, 0.5, 1.0) at full alpha. Its effective base radius is about 0, so it is a tight cluster of dots near the centre that wobbles with the right-channel waveform, rather than a large circle. The warp shader adds a complex-quadratic (fractal-like) map plus two small scalings, one just under 1 (0.992) and one just over (1.004). Together with the mesh warp's rotation control of 1 rad per step, the content should be swirled and folded repeatedly, giving intricate, fractal-looking filaments that stay roughly centred.

Colours: the calibrated palette is blue with green as the second hue (blue about 0.67, green about 0.32). Blue is the dominant hue. Saturation is moderate (about 0.4), the balance is slightly cool, and the pink dots may show as a minor accent. Colour trails are channel-selective: red fades within about 6 frames, green lasts about 28, and blue about 170 (roughly 5 to 6 s). Old content therefore tends to turn blue and linger as dim blue haze. The composite itself adds no colour, only grey scaling.

Motion: the drift is tiny (about 0.14 px/s), but the strong per-step rotation and zoom variation (including a zoom range that reaches negative values) mean the pattern keeps churning. The calibrated flow is about 0.13 screen widths per second, moderate. Bass, mid and treble, plain and smoothed, all drive the zoom continuously, so the tunnel breathes with the music. The gain is unknown.

Flashing: the no-strobe probability is only 0.52, so it is not guaranteed strobe-free. Estimated brightness jumps are about 0.9 per 10 s (range 0 to 5.7), so occasional pulses are possible. Energy is likely Chill to Normal. Uncertain: the real luminance, since the dark start may leave it nearly black, the zoom's audio gain, and how the pink dots show.
