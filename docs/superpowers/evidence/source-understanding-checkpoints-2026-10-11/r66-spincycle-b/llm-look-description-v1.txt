LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r66 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a mostly dark, near-grayscale feedback tunnel. Each frame the previous image is resampled with a centre-outward zoom (zoom 1.0095, zoomexp 5.43), so everything drifts out from the screen centre, slowly near the middle (about 0.17% per step) and fast near the edges (up to about 8.8% per step). On top of that a thin two-trace audio waveform line is drawn (two parallel traces at the centre, additive, very faint: wave alpha is only 0.001), plus three 54-sided near-circular polygons that move around and change size with the music. Two of them (treble and mid) are filled black with full alpha, so they act as dark discs that cut holes in the feedback. The third (bass) has red = 1 with the other channels not resolved, so it could read as a red to orange, yellow or green blob, or grey. Its colour is uncertain. The composite then overlays a 1.49x magnified, left-right mirrored copy at 50% opacity, giving an approximately two-fold mirror-symmetric, doubled look (upper estimate; zoom and drawing make it inexact). A thin 2% outer border is blended black every frame, so the screen edge stays dark and content is cleared as it flies out there.

Colours and brightness: the recipe lists the palette as mostly grayscale (waveform grey 0.65, black shapes), with possible red-to-green hues only from the bass shape. The mesh/feedback palette is unresolved. The steady-state canvas colour is very dark (about 0.01 on the canvas, near 0 on the display, hue family grayscale). That figure is only a lower bound. The calibrated display estimate gives mean luminance about 0.18 (wide interval, roughly 0 to 0.41), mean saturation about 0.45 (0.17 to 0.72) and a neutral warm/cool balance, so expect a dim to moderate, fairly muted picture, possibly with coloured accents from the bass shape. Feedback decay is 1 (nothing is faded by the decay control), so drawn marks can persist and smear outward rather than vanish; only the black border and black shapes erase. Trail length is not established.

Motion and audio: motion is constant outward expansion, with the warp's x/y translation driven by an expression that mixes treble, mid and bass, time and positional terms. The source marks these as threshold or clamped, so I cannot say how strong the pull is, and the picture likely wobbles or sways with the music. The three shapes react to separate bands. Treble moves the first, mid the second and bass the third, each with a continuous radius gain of 0.1 and clamped position responses. So the discs bounce and pulse with their band. The waveform follows the left and right audio channels. Estimated flow is about 0.19 screen widths per second (0.07 to 0.39). The frame difference is moderate. The estimated activity is Normal (about 0.52, possible bands Chill to Intense). One shape shows a brightness cycle of about 0.21 Hz and the flash estimate is about 0.43 Hz, with a coherent delta up to about 0.8. That is a source-level mechanism, so a slow pulsing or flicker is possible, but it is not a verified visible strobe. Global brightness jumps are estimated at about 0.9 per 10 s (0 to 6).

Random or chaotic parts: the recipe lists no per-load random switches or random redraws. The picture is unpredictable mainly because the audio-driven translation and shape paths are unresolved and feed back through the zoom, so the long-term look can be chaotic and music-dependent. Shape positions and sizes, the bass shape's colour and the trail density are the least certain details. This is an approximate description, not a pixel-exact one.
