LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r64 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a centred, roughly circular pinwheel/spiral built from several layers. A custom wave (wave_0, 512 points per frame) traces a closed-form radial curve, a parametric loop that is probably circular or spiral but not proven an exact circle (exact_circle_proven false). It fills most of the frame, with x about 0.15-0.86 and y about 0.05-0.96, so it may touch the top and bottom edges. The loop is driven by two per-frame values (value1, value2) in its x/y offsets, so its shape may wobble with whatever state those carry; the recipe does not say where they come from. A 100-sided polygon (effectively a disc) sits at the centre with radius 0.73 and is white-ish by its authored colour, though it may be hidden or overdrawn. A built-in angled waveform line (mode 6, centred, angle 0) follows the audio waveform, but its authored alpha is only 0.001, so it is almost invisible. I would not expect a bold audio-shaped line. Everything is drawn over a feedback canvas, so the ring is repeated and smeared by the feedback.

Colours: the custom wave's points cycle through all six hue families in nearly equal shares (green, yellow, pink, cyan, red, blue, about 17% each), at full saturation and full alpha. Colour is a function of position along the curve plus slow time drift (sample times 6.284, offset 0.1 times time, with phases 0, 2.09 and 4.19 rad for r, g and b), so the line should look like a rainbow or pinwheel with hue rotating around the loop and slowly changing. The recipe's steady-state estimate for the overall canvas is grayscale at about 0.24, a lower bound. The calibrated display saturation is about 0.47 (interval 0.19-0.74), so the picture is moderately colourful, not neon, with a mix of grey haze and rainbow strokes. Warm and cool are balanced (warm/cool about 0). The white polygon and grey feedback haze will wash colours out in places. A 3.5% black border blends in every frame, so there is a faint dark frame at the screen edge.

Feedback and motion: the warp is a plain uniform zoom of 0.9916, so content contracts toward the screen centre by about 0.84% per frame. There is no rotation, translation or radial zoom variation, and the small warp term (0.01) is a constant. Decay is 0.999, which is nearly no fading, so strokes should leave long, persistent trails. The recipe leaves the actual trail length unresolved and says only that the stored level may stall near full. Each ring, drawn at the large radius, shrinks toward the centre as repeated, nested copies, giving a tunnel or concentric inward-flowing look. If the wave moves or wobbles, those copies will form spiral or pinwheel arms. The exact motion is not computed. The estimated mean flow is low, about 0.036 screen widths per second (interval 0-0.13), and the frame-to-frame change is small, so I expect a calm, smooth, hypnotic drift rather than fast motion. Mood tags are Smooth and Hypnotic (estimated, low confidence). The symmetry estimate is moderate (0.6) from the radial structure.

Audio: reactivity is weak in the recipe. There are no audio bindings. The only audio route is the near-invisible waveform line. The wave_0 equations and the zoom do not read bass, mid or treble. The "beat detect [synchro]" in the filename is not evidence, and the recipe finds no beat-driven brightness or flash route. Expect the picture to look nearly the same quiet or loud, with at most subtle changes through the value1/value2 inputs if they are audio-derived, which the recipe does not establish.

Brightness and uncertainty: estimated mean luminance is dark to medium, about 0.14 (wide interval, roughly 0-0.38). The estimated activity is Chill, possibly Normal. Brightness jumps are 0 (interval up to about 2.5 per 10 s), and nothing in the recipe suggests flashing, but flash behaviour is marked unknown, not proven absent. Nothing is stated to be random per load: random_redraws is null and there are no per-load random switches. The source can still be chaotic if the persistent state behind value1/value2 or the loop geometry evolves, and the long-lived feedback could accumulate into dense, busy overlapping rings over time. Most quantitative values (speed, acceleration, trail half-life, mean luma) are assumed or unknown. Overall: a calm, mostly dark rainbow-ring pinwheel with long inward-shrinking trails over a greyish haze, low audio reactivity and no strobing.
