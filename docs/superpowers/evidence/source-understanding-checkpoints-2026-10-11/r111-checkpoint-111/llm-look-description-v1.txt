LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r111 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. About five seconds in, I expect a mostly dark screen with thin, soft, smeared white-grey line work flowing over it. Four custom waves (512 points each, drawn as white lines at full authored alpha) trace paths whose positions come from per-frame state (endx/endy, x2/x3/x4, vx3/vx4, a sine/cos with a small 0.004 step). The recipe gives no position bounds, so I cannot say where they sit or whether they cover the screen. Two of the four also read bass/mid/treble. The built-in wave is mode 7, two parallel waveform traces about 0.25 units either side of the screen centre (0.5, 0.5), drawn additively. Its authored alpha is only 0.001, so it is likely barely visible; max-normalisation turns its 0.65 grey into full white where it does show. Two shader stages smear everything. The warp samples the previous image with a small self-steered offset (about -0.0074 to +0.0126 UV). The composite shifts samples by up to about +/-0.275 UV along image gradients, using the blurred copy. Together they should turn crisp lines into flowing, organic, reaction-diffusion-like smears and wisps. There is no symmetry, kaleidoscope or tunnel, and no mirroring (symmetry score about 0.2, low confidence). The ordinary warp controls are all neutral (zoom 1, rotation 0, no translation, centre 0.5, 0.5), so any drift comes only from the shader advection.

Colour. The sources are all grayscale: custom waves are pure white and the built-in wave is grey that gets normalised to white. The colour comes from the composite. It multiplies by cycling corner shades (hue_shader) whose per-channel ranges are 0.67 to 1.0, with cycle periods of about 14.6 s (R), 19.6 s (G) and 16.2 s (B). Which tint appears where is random per preset load, so I cannot name one fixed hue. The steady-state hue family is given as green. The flat-canvas display colours are very dim, about 0.002 in each channel, with a slight green lean and a time-varying spread. I expect a faint, low-saturation tint that slowly drifts between hues, not vivid colour. The display estimate says mean saturation about 0.63 (interval 0.36 to 0.90), which sits oddly with the source-side saturation of 0. I trust the dim tinted-grey picture more but cannot resolve the conflict. Warm versus cool is about neutral (0.03, interval -0.31 to +0.38).

Motion. Feedback persistence is long: the warp keeps about 99.6% per frame (half-life about 172 frames, roughly 5.7 s), with no colour offset. Bright strokes therefore leave long, slowly fading trails that the advection drags into streaks. Source-side motion is slow to moderate. The estimated flow is about 0.05 screen widths per second (interval 0 to 0.16), and the p95 sampling speed about 0.22 viewport units per second (interval 0.09 to 0.55). Mean frame-to-frame change is small, about 0.008 (interval 0 to 0.044). Composite sampling can move up to 0.275 UV, but that is a ceiling, not a typical speed. The wave paths are closed-form in time and the per-frame state, so they should glide smoothly. The recipe finds no audio-driven switches or discontinuities, so I expect no hard cuts.

Brightness and contrast. Dark overall. Estimated mean luminance is about 0.08 (interval 0 to 0.32), with white line work against near-black. The steady background is close to black (about 0.002 to 0.03). Long trails may build up a hazy mid-grey tint around busy areas, but the recipe does not say how much. Contrast should be high between the thin bright strokes and the background, and softer where smearing spreads them. Brightness jumps are estimated near zero (about 0.05 per 10 s, upper bound about 2.9), and no flashing is identified. The recipe cannot say whether the display is strictly dark, because texture-rich advection could look brighter than the flat transfer suggests.

Audio response. The built-in traces follow the left and right waveform continuously, so they ripple with the music, although alpha 0.001 makes that muted. Two custom waves (wave_2 and wave_3) read bass, mid and treble in their position programs, so I expect their paths to react to the music in shape or position. The size and direction of that response are not established. There is no audio route to brightness or colour (bass RGB effect 0), no thresholded beat trigger and no whole-screen pulses. Overall I expect a calm, fluid response with no beat-synchronised flashing.

Uncertainty. Confidence is low. Wave positions and coverage are unresolved, so I cannot say if the screen shows a few thin strands or a dense web. Actual trail density and brightness depend on the feedback and composite, which the recipe only bounds. The palette is a random per-load draw. The recipe gives a "green" steady-state hue family, but the display saturation estimate points to something more colourful than the grayscale sources. The approximate mood is Chill (activity about 0.05, interval 0 to 0.49, so Normal is possible), smooth and organic/liquid. It is an editable estimate, not a rendered measurement.
