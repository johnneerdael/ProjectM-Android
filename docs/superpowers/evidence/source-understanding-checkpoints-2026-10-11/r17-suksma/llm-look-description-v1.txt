A slow inward pull: the feedback image contracts toward the screen centre by 0.5-5.5% per step
(zoomexp 0.35 makes the rate radius-dependent), with no rotation. A dense 63x47 grid of white
motion vectors is drawn into the feedback each frame, pointing where the warp samples from, so the
vector field itself becomes visible texture. The per-vertex translation (dx/dy) depends on position,

flow sideways rather than flashing it. Warp and composite shaders are present: the warp shader
rescales the previous frame (x0.90 and x1.15), and the composite self-advects (image content
steering its own sampling: smeared, fluid structure). Palette is unresolved from source.

~0.17, saturation ~0.58, frame difference ~0.04, flow ~0.14 screen widths/s, jumps ~1.2 per 10 s.

converging on the centre, with fluid smearing from the composite; calm to moderate activity.
