Approximate look: a warm, orange-to-yellow swirling tunnel on a mostly black-to-dim background, with soft smeared trails spiralling inward. Based only on the static recipe; nothing was rendered.

Colours and brightness: the composite stage pushes the canvas steeply through a warm ramp (flat_transfer): very dim canvas content shows as dull red-orange, mid-dim content as yellow-orange, and anything at about half level or above goes to white. Its colour matrix draws almost entirely from the red channel, so the picture reads as orange and yellow with white hot spots rather than a multicolour palette. The pooled palette says orange leads (about 68%) with yellow second (about 29%), a small blue trace, and no random tint. Calibrated brightness is moderate (mean luminance about 0.27, range 0.09 to 0.48) and saturation is low to moderate (about 0.28, wide uncertainty), because bright areas wash out to white. The wave's own colour is authored pure red. The warp keeps a faint steady green floor (about 0.1) on the canvas, so the empty background is probably not pure black but a very dark tint, which the composite then maps through its ramp. That is a mild uncertainty.

Structure: a custom wave of 512 points forms a radial or circular curve (not proven to be an exact circle) around the middle. A thin outer border (1% of the screen) darkens the edges slightly each frame. The warp shader adds spatially oscillating radial bands with a period of about 0.31 radial units, with the phase moving at roughly -10 rad/s, plus a small four-tap blur that grows toward the edges. Together these likely give ring-like ripples and soft haze, though ring count and contrast are unverified.

Motion: the whole image contracts toward the centre every frame, by about 1% near the middle up to about 45% per step in the outer parts, and turns by -0.12 rad per step. That is a strong spiral-inward flow, so drawn detail should smear into curved streaks that converge on the centre. The composite also uses image-driven self-advection, which should add fluid, smeared distortion. Red and blue persist with a half-life of roughly 27 frames (about 0.9 s), while green is reset each frame, so trails fade over about a second. Calibrated flow is modest (about 0.12 screen widths/s).

Audio: bass drives the composite colour channels, but through thresholds or clamps with unknown gain, so the strength is unresolved. A random value (q10) is redrawn at unspecified times and may change colours or shapes.

Flashing: the strobe classifier gives about 0.73, so occasional brightness pulses are likely and the preset cannot be called strobe-free. Estimated global jumps are about 0.6 per 10 s, but the range runs from 0 to nearly 5. Because the composite ramp is steep, small canvas changes could swing brightness hard. Energy is probably Chill-to-Normal, but the facet confidence is low.
