LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r93 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. About five seconds in, I expect a full-screen, textured, organic field rather than a few clean shapes on black. The warp stage is a blur-difference feedback (fine level added, coarser blur subtracted every frame, /render/mechanisms blur_difference_feedback, pattern_growth) combined with gradient-driven self-advection and an 8-tap blur. That is the recipe for small seeds growing into blobs, cells and splatter that spread over the canvas. The composite then shows this through a four-direction relief (emboss) stage and a non-mirrored repeat tiling, so the surface should read as embossed, lit-relief texture, possibly with repeated tiles, with edges and texture standing out and flat areas collapsing to one level. Seeds come from a 100-sided shape (so effectively a circle) drawn 100 times, with positions and radii read from shared global memory (megabuf), so the layout of the 100 discs is not recoverable from the recipe. They may form a scattered or gridded cluster of small to medium discs; this is unknown. A built-in audio-deformed circle (radius about 0.06 model units, thick, centre x 0.5, y 0.04, so near one horizontal edge of the screen, and I cannot tell top from bottom) is drawn at alpha 0.004, so it is essentially invisible and contributes almost nothing. The shape's own alpha is 1 with a second colour at 0.5, and its border alpha is 0. There is a hairline outer black border (about 1% of the screen) and an inner border in magenta (1.0, 0.0, 0.75) covering about 0.0% of the screen, which is negligible.

Colour. Feedback colour is channel-selective: the red channel is multiplied by 0 each frame (cleared), green is kept whole (gain 0.99 plus 0.01 offset, steady level 1), and blue decays slowly (gain 0.996, half-life about 172 frames). So the retained canvas drifts to green and cyan-green, and the recipe's steady state is a green hue family (about 0.26, 0.84, 0.22 locally). The composite flat-level ramp is a mild warm tint on grays (a 0.25 level shows as about 0.44, 0.33, 0.25, white stays white), and the empty-canvas background settles in about 2.8 s to a dark blue-gray (0.12, 0.15, 0.21). I expect a green or teal-green dominant look, with warm-tinted highlights from the composite and blue-gray in flat areas. The saturation estimate is about 0.42 (interval 0.15 to 0.69). The source-only palette feature says cool (warm_cool -0.42) while the calibrated display estimate is near neutral (+0.11, interval -0.22 to +0.47). These disagree, so the warm or cool balance is unsure. Disc colours are not resolved in the recipe, so any other hue there is unknown. No random textures or per-load random branches are listed.

Motion. Mesh translation, rotation and warp are all zero and zoom is 1, so the base feedback does not drift, rotate or zoom (the zoomexp of 1.50 has no effect at zoom 1). All motion comes from the shader warp: a small sampling scale of 0.992 (a slight contraction each frame), advection offsets up to about 0.006 in x and 0.0085 in y, and pattern growth. I expect slow, smooth, liquid evolution: blobs and cells growing and morphing in place, no fast travel. The estimated mean flow is about 0.05 screen widths per second (interval 0 to 0.15) and the mean frame difference is about 0.037 (0.007 to 0.081). The smoothness score is high (93), and the shape instances are marked as moving, but their motion path is unknown.

Brightness and contrast. The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.47 (interval 0.29 to 0.71), so mid-brightness, not dark, and neither very bright nor black. Relief shading should give moderate local contrast at edges. The recipe's own flat-field and steady-state levels are lower bounds and explicitly do not describe the final picture. Global brightness jumps are estimated at about 0.35 per 10 s (interval 0 to 4), so flashing is unknown but probably low. Activity percentile is about 0.15 (Chill, possibly Normal), at low confidence.

Audio response. The only audio route is the built-in circle following the right waveform channel, and at alpha 0.004 it is nearly invisible. There are no bass, mid or treble bindings (audio_bindings is empty), no thresholds and no beat-driven colour or size routes. Music should therefore have little visible effect. Any response would come indirectly, through the shape memory values or the shaders, and the recipe does not show that. The picture should look much the same with or without music, apart from a faint wave line.

Uncertainty. Unknown: the global-memory contents (where the 100 discs sit, their sizes and colours, and whether they move); the feedback decay for the shape and wave layers (decay is null); how strongly the gradient relief shows against the green canvas; whether tiling is visible or the screen-wide field hides it; whether the composite and warp shaders give the grain, blotch size or any cell structure I imagine; which screen edge the wave sits near; and flashing. The recipe is marked confidence low, and the strict source model lists no eligible mood group. My estimate that the field is green and mid-bright rests on the steady-state hue and the calibrated luminance, not on a measured frame.
