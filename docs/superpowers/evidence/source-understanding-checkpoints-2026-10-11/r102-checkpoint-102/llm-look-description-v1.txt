LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r102 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. "Flexi - flower demo 3.milk" (name not used as evidence) is a feedback-driven pattern-growth preset, not a drawn-geometry preset. The only drawn elements are very weak: a 100-sided polygon at the exact screen centre (0.5, 0.5) with NDC radius 0.01 (a dot about 1% of the screen), black fill at alpha 0.1 (shape_0 constants), and a built-in circular audio-waveform ring (effective radius 0.06 model units, base position x 0.5, y 0.04, white, thick, not additive) whose alpha is only 0.0044. The ring is therefore essentially invisible, and the centre dot is a small, faint seed. What carries the picture is the warp shader: the recipe lists blur_difference_feedback (finer level added, coarser blur subtracted every frame, intent pattern_growth), gradient_driven_advection (reaction-diffusion-like flow) and image_driven_advection (self-advection). By the skill's reading, small seeds grow into blobs, cells or splatter that spread over the canvas, so about 5 seconds in I expect most of the screen to be patterned (soft organic blobs, cells or flower-like lobes spreading outward from the centre seed), not a clean background with a few shapes. Mesh-level controls are inert: zoom 1.0, rot 0, dx and dy 0, sx and sy 1, warp 0. zoomexp is 0.92 but with zoom 1 it does nothing. So there is no tunnel, spin, drift or mirror/kaleidoscope (mirror score 0, tiling absent). How big, how many and how dense the lobes are is not given by the recipe.

Colour. The warp stage only keeps the green channel of the canvas: gain 0.97 and offset 0.03 on green, and red and blue are cleared every frame (channel_selective, r and b gain 0). Flat regions settle in about 1 s on pure green (0, 1, 0). The composite then recolours the canvas. Its flat transfer maps a grey level 0 to yellow (1,1,0), 0.5 to dark yellow (0.5,0.5,0), and 1 to black. The steady-state record gives a display colour of about (1.0, 0.007, 0) with hue family red. My reading is that the untouched background is a saturated red, and where the pattern lowers the green level the display moves toward orange and yellow, and toward black where green is saturated. So the expected palette is red with orange and yellow structures and some dark areas, warm overall (display_estimate warm_cool 0.83, interval 0.50 to 1.0; palette warm_cool is 0.0 in the mood block, so the two estimates disagree). Saturation is estimated as low to moderate (0.24, interval 0 to 0.51), which does not match a fully saturated red field. I treat the red as likely but not certain, because the composite flat transfer was evaluated on grey levels, not on a green-only canvas, and the recipe itself says the empty-canvas state does not describe the displayed picture. The shape and wave add no colour of their own (black and white). No random textures or random palette draws are listed, so I expect the same hues on every load.

Motion. There is no global motion: translation is 0 px/s, rotation 0, uniform zoom 1, and the shape is stationary (maximum speed 0). Any movement comes from the shader's self-advection and gradient flow, so the patterns would slowly crawl, swell and morph in place as they grow outward from the centre, with a liquid or organic feel. Source speed is only assumed (0.2 viewport units per second, interval 0 to 1). The calibrated display estimate is much lower: mean frame difference about 0 (interval 0 to 0.03) and mean flow about 0.004 screen widths per second (interval 0 to 0.076). I expect slow, smooth change, not fast motion. The mood block gives smoothness 93 and low intensity (10), with possible tags Smooth, Hypnotic and Organic/Liquid. Trails follow the green-channel persistence of 0.97 per frame (offset 0.03), but the recipe gives no half-life because the non-linear bits make it unresolved.

Brightness and contrast. The recipe is unsure here. Mean luminance is estimated at 0.08 (interval 0 to 0.32), which is dark to moderate. A saturated red field would have a somewhat higher luma than that, so either the field is darker than I think or the interval's upper part applies. I would say moderate to dim overall, with contrast set by the patterned edges between red, orange, yellow and dark regions. Brightness jumps are estimated at about 0.01 per 10 s (interval 0 to 2.7), and flashing is estimated at 0 (coherent transitions 0 Hz). So I expect no strobing and a steady level. Activity band is Chill, with Normal still possible in the mood-block interval; the calibrated activity estimate is 0.011 (interval 0 to 0.22).

Audio response. Essentially none that can be seen. The only audio route is the built-in waveform ring (waveform_right), and its alpha is 0.0044, so it adds almost no visible content. audio_bindings is empty, there are no bass, mid or treble routes, and the mood block reports no audio brightness route. With music playing I expect the look to be nearly the same as in silence. At most there may be a faint, hard-to-see ring near the bottom centre that wobbles with the waveform, and the shader's flow might respond to the waveform only through the faint ring seed.

Uncertainty. Main uncertainties: (1) whether the display is red-dominated as the steady-state record says, or less saturated and darker as the display estimate suggests; (2) the scale, density and shape of the grown pattern, since the recipe says only that the blur-difference mechanism sharpens seeds into blobs or cells and flower-like structure is not established; (3) how much of the screen the pattern fills at 5 s, given that the shader would need time to grow from a tiny seed with alpha 0.1, and the shader palette depends on the canvas contents; (4) brightness is an interval from 0 to 0.32; (5) the y position of the ring (0.04) may be near the bottom or near the top depending on the axis convention, and in any case it is almost invisible; (6) the recipe has no half-life, no hue rate and no mean encoded luma (all unknown), and speed, acceleration and jerk are assumed values.
