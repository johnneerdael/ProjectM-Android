Expect a dark screen with many small white, round-ish dots (14-sided polygons) scattered and bouncing with the music, leaving cool blue-green trails. Four shape groups are drawn, all authored plain white (RGB 1,1,1, saturation 0): 126, 128, 32 and 16 instances, each with a radius of about 0.03 in normalised units. That makes small dots, roughly 3% of the screen half-width, and together about 300 of them. Their positions follow bass, mid and treble through thresholded or clamped routes, and their opacity follows all three bands. So the dots should jump or scatter around with the music, and fade in and out with loudness. The recipe has no fixed centre for them, so I can't say where they cluster. A built-in "two parallel traces" waveform (grey 0.65, additive) is also present, but its alpha is 0.001, so it is effectively invisible.

Colour: the dots themselves are white, but the warp shader gives the feedback a colour cast. It keeps green and blue almost fully each frame (gain about 0.997) while red decays faster (gain about 0.969, half-life about 22 frames). White trails therefore drift toward cyan and blue as the red falls away. The settled feedback colour is cyan (about 0.16, 0.57, 0.56). The calibrated hue shares are blue about 0.46, green 0.22 and cyan 0.10, so the picture likely reads as blue with green and cyan accents. The pooled palette is blue, green and cyan with no dominant hue (0.46). Calibrated saturation is moderate to high (about 0.64), and about 57% of the screen carries colour (range 0.25 to 0.85). Estimated mean luminance is low, about 0.15 (range 0 to 0.37), and the dark facet is admitted (0.62 against 0.58). The composite is a pass-through apart from a small image-driven wobble, with offsets up to about 0.02 of the screen, so it adds no tint.

Motion: the base warp is gentle. The image contracts toward the centre by 0.05% per step, with no rotation or translation, and a small warp amount of 0.01. The warp shader also resamples the picture at scales of 0.95 and 0.98, which pulls content slowly inward. Trails last with a half-life of about 1.85 s. Dots should therefore leave short, fading, inward-curling streaks. Calibrated flow is low, about 0.10 screen widths per second. Source smoothness is 0 because the dots' positions can jump, but calibrated activity is Chill (about 0.19) with the Chill facet admitted at 0.90.

Audio and flashing: strongly audio-reactive in dot position and opacity. The no-strobe probability is 0.87, between 0.7 and 0.95, so occasional pulses are likely but not a strobe. Calibrated global brightness jumps are about 0.1 per 10 s (up to about 2.9). The source model assumes about 2 position jumps per second, but that is an assumption.

Uncertain: how many dots are visible at once, where they sit, and how bright the cyan trail wash gets.
