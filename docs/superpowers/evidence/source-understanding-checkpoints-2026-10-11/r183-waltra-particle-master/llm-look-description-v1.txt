A bright, cool-toned screen of outward-rushing colourful streaks and glowing dots, tinted cyan, teal and white. The composite shader maps dark canvas levels to deep blue, mid levels to cyan-teal and bright levels to white (flat transfer: 0.06 shows blue-teal, 0.25 pale cyan-green, 0.5 and above white). Calibrated estimates agree: fairly bright (mean luminance about 0.61, range 0.42 to 0.85), moderately saturated (about 0.45) and cool (warm-cool about -0.40). The bright upper half of the canvas saturates to white, so expect a lot of white and pale cyan with blue-teal fringes, not a dark scene.

Structure: the dominant element is a field of dots from a custom audio waveform, drawn additively in a colour that cycles continuously through the rainbow (green, pink, blue, orange, yellow, cyan, purple, red over time, always at full brightness after normalisation). The dots form two parallel traces tilted to a roughly vertical line angle (1.57 rad) whose position responds to bass, mid and treble. Over this sit three polygons: a large 15-sided pink-magenta shape at the centre (fill from pink centre to magenta edge, fairly transparent) that copies the previous frame into itself, a 63-sided white disc at upper right (about 0.81, 0.30) that also copies the previous frame, rotated and shrunk, and a tiny cyan dot whose position is audio-controlled. So expect a pink centre glow, a pale disc at upper right, and a small cyan marker moving about.

Motion: strong. Every frame the whole picture expands outward from the centre by about 8 percent, with short trails (half-life roughly 0.45 s). Dots and drawn detail therefore stretch into long streaks radiating from the centre, a starburst or warp-speed look, softened by a small blur that grows towards the edges and by image-driven smearing in the composite. No rotation. The recursive shape copies add nested echoes that also stream outward.

Audio: the waveform shape and the dot trace position follow the music continuously. No audio route to brightness was found.

Flashing: no flash mechanism identified; the colour of the dots drifts at most about half a cycle per second, so it changes smoothly. Estimated brightness jumps are low. Uncertain: how dense the streaks look, and how much white washes out the colour.
