A dark, high-contrast, swirling picture with no obviously drawn shapes. The recipe lists no waveforms, polygons or point clouds; the only things injected into the feedback are a thin outer border (about 2 percent of the screen, colour unresolved) and whatever the audio-driven warp and shader bring in. So I expect mostly near-black space with thin, bright, curved colour streaks that originate at the screen edges and are pulled inward by the swirling warp. The calibrated mean luminance is lowish (about 0.24, interval 0.05-0.48), but it is very uncertain.

Colour: the composite multiplies the picture by four blended corner tints that cycle slowly (periods about 14.6, 19.6 and 16.2 s, each channel between two thirds and full strength), with the starting hues chosen randomly at every preset load. So each time it loads it may be tinted differently, with one hue in one corner and another elsewhere. The composite is also extremely steep: levels below about 0.25 stay almost black, levels around 0.5 and up become pure white. The result is mainly black and white-hot cores with tinted fringes, not a broadly coloured scene. Calibrated saturation is modest (about 0.32).

Motion: the feedback keeps very little (about 0.45 of the previous frame survives), so trails are extremely short, roughly one frame. The mesh warp zooms and shifts differently across the screen and is driven by bass and smoothed bass, so the swirl lunges and relaxes with the beat. An 8-tap blur whose radius depends on screen position and audio smears things into soft haze. Calibrated flow is moderate (about 0.16 screen widths per second).

Flashing: this is the most flash-prone of the group. The source model reports a shader brightness cycle of 14.3 Hz and a coherent transition estimate above 30 Hz, with peak changes near 0.26. Estimated brightness jumps are high (about 21 per 10 s). Treat it as potentially flickering or strobing, and uncertain in strength.

Audio: bass drives zoom, translation, the warp shader's colour and the composite; smoothed treble adds to the composite colour.
