Colour is mixed with no single dominant hue: leading hues are blue (~25%), green (~21%) and red (~19%), then pink and orange. Estimated saturation is moderate (~0.32) and about 43% of the screen is coloured. Uncertain: actual hue placement shifts when the random q values are redrawn at about 3.1 s and then every 12.6 s.

Expect swirling, smeared, liquid movement. Trails are nonlinear: dim detail dies quickly, mid-level detail persists. Estimated flow is about 0.19 screen widths per second.
