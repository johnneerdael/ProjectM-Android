LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r80 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This looks like a soft, swirling, liquid-style feedback image rather than a clean geometric figure. A smeared, zooming and twisting feedback field fills the screen (self-advection plus gradient flow in the warp shader, with a radius-dependent zoom of about 1.02 to 1.38 and zoomexp 1.35). Three faint dotted custom waves (512 points each) and a stereo X/Y dotted trace from the built-in wave are drawn into that field. Two centred polygons are also drawn: a triangle (radius about 0.49) that slowly rotates through a full turn every ~63 s, and a 14-sided polygon (radius about 0.30) that stays still. The recipe lists no mirror or kaleidoscope symmetry. The wave dots and shapes are seeds that the feedback then smears into curved, flowing streaks, so I expect a hazy radial/spiral flow centred on the middle of the screen with a dotted trace and the polygons showing through as ghosted outlines. How strongly the dots, polygons and trace read on screen is unresolved. Dot positions are only known as authored bounds, and independent particles are not established.

Colour and brightness. The warp transfer keeps about 0.90 of the previous frame and adds 0.04, which gives a half-life of roughly 6.6 frames (about 0.2 s). Trails are therefore short and the canvas settles at a mid level, not black. The composite is an inverting, clamped ramp: flat canvas 0 shows white, 0.25 shows about 0.57 grey, 0.5 shows about 0.13, and 0.75 and above show black. The estimated settled display is a dim, slightly green-tinted grey of roughly 0.23 to 0.26 per channel (steady_state hue_family "green", a lower bound). The calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.30 (10th to 90th percentile 0.11 to 0.53), moderate saturation about 0.47 (0.20 to 0.74), and a roughly neutral warm/cool balance of about +0.1 (interval -0.23 to +0.46). The built-in wave colour cycles over time. Its raw channels sweep broad ranges with periods of about 6 to 7 s, and over 120 s it visits green (25%), blue (18%), pink (16%), red, yellow, cyan, purple and orange. These are raw source colours, not confirmed displayed colours, and the composite inversion can change how they appear. The custom waves are authored orange (wave_1 and wave_3) and yellow (wave_2) at low alpha, around 0.10 to 0.13. They are faint, so I would expect an orange/yellow warm cast in the dots. Shape 1 is authored yellow-orange (1.0, 0.9, about 0) at the centre fading toward a purple-ish perimeter (0.6, 0.3) with a black border, and shape 2 is white fading to transparent. The final palette and which hue dominates are conditional and unverified.

Motion. There is no net drift, because the dx and dy translation is 0. The motion is zoom and rotation per step. The rotation term is about 0.62 rad per feedback step in the recipe's nominal control, modulated by slow sinusoids and by radius bands, which should read as a continuous twisting swirl. The warp includes a small constant deformation of 0.0124 and sampled-offset advection of up to about 0.005 to 0.008 UV per step. The calibrated estimate puts typical flow at about 0.09 screen widths per second (0.02 to 0.23) and mean frame-to-frame difference at about 0.06. Activity is "Normal" (percentile 0.60, with Chill and Intense both possible). The approximate mood estimates are smooth, organic/liquid and psychedelic, with medium confidence and a not-calibrated flag. The strict source model gives no eligible mood group.

Audio reaction. The built-in wave trace follows the left/right waveform continuously, so the dotted XY figure wobbles with the sound. Mid drives the blue channel of shape 1's fill and perimeter. Smoothed bass and treble drive the red, green and blue levels in the composite shader, but every route is threshold or clamp type with no established linear gain. So I expect colour or brightness shifts with bass and treble that are smoothed rather than snappy beat pulses. Visible strength is unresolved. The estimated global brightness jumps are about 26 per 10 s (wide interval, about 8 to 98), which suggests noticeable brightness fluctuation. This is an estimate and not a flash certification, and the mood layer's flash assumptions (about 4 Hz, delta about 0.18) are assumed values.

Random, chaotic or undefined. The recipe lists no per-load random branches, no random redraw palette and no hue-shader. The composite output is not marked undefined and has no non-finite share. The built-in wave colour channel with a "log" term and some rotation branches (value range null in the warp and a log or pow of a sine in the outer radius bands) are unbounded or unresolved in the source model, so the outer-edge twist could be erratic. The image is a feedback system with self-advection, so fine detail will be chaotic and I cannot predict exact patterns. Several features are unknown in the recipe: mean encoded luma and feedback half-life in the mood layer. Treat the brightness, colour dominance and shape visibility above as approximate.
