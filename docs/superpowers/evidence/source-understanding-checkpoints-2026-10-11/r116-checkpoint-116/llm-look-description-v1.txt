LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r116 v1, core recipe view; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This is a layered, centred, mostly symmetric-looking composition built on a feedback canvas with short trails. About 5 seconds in, I expect a soft, hazy, bluish-white field with a dotted audio-waveform trace running through the screen centre (built-in wave mode 6, drawn as additive dots, wave position 0.5/0.5, angle 0, so a horizontal dotted trace that follows the left-channel waveform). Over it sit several custom point-cloud waves of 512 points each (wave_0 blue, wave_2 white/grey, wave_1 orange, wave_3 a faint 100-point blue one), plus three 100-sided (so effectively circular) polygons all centred at the middle of the screen: a large disc of radius about 0.6 NDC (shape_2, red-to-green/teal gradient authored, very transparent at the edge), a small solid red disc of radius 0.1 (shape_1, alpha 1, fading to green at its rim) and a faint white-to-blue disc (shape_0, alpha 0.08, radius unknown but bass-driven). A grid of 18 columns by 23 rows of motion vectors (414 short line segments) is also possibly drawn over the whole screen. The composite stage mirrors and tiles the image (cartesian mirror fold plus repeat tiling, not mirrored tiles), so the picture likely reads as a kaleidoscopic or repeated pattern rather than one single scene. The point waves' positions range well beyond the screen (wave_0 x from -0.52 to 1.49), so only parts of them are visible and they can look scattered. The recipe does not say how many tiles or what the fold looks like; that is unresolved.

Colour. The overall hue cue from the recipe is blue (steady_state hue_family blue, display_local_rgb about 0.011/0.011/0.015, which is a lower bound only). The built-in wave colour shifts slowly (red 0.6-1.4, green 0.45-0.65, blue 0.8-1.0, then clamped and max-normalised): over 120 s it spends roughly 80% of the time pink, 15% purple, 5% blue, so the dotted trace should look pink to magenta-lilac, drifting slowly (hue rate about 0.12 cycles/s upper estimate). wave_0 and wave_3 are blue/pale blue, wave_2 is white-grey, wave_1 is orange, and shape_1 is a vivid red dot in the middle. The overall palette estimate is mildly saturated (about 0.41 from source, 0.31 calibrated, with a wide interval 0.04-0.58) and slightly cool (warm/cool about -0.13 source, -0.06 calibrated, so roughly neutral to cool). The composite flat transfer is neutral grey (R=G=B at every level, 0.67 at full level), so no tint comes from the composite on flat areas; coloured parts come from the drawn elements. A blue tint with pink/purple dots and a red centre dot is my best guess, but the final dominant colour is conditional and not established.

Motion. Mostly slow. The feedback warp has no rotation, no translation (dx, dy = 0), stretch 1 and centre at the middle; there is only a radius-dependent zoom (zoomexp 0.99984, so near 1) plus a small warp amount 0.0168. The zoom itself is audio-linked and its value range is unknown, so the pull toward or away from the centre could be gentle, pulsing with the music. All three polygons are stationary at the screen centre. The dotted waveform and the point clouds move with sample and time (closed-form positions), so they move or shimmer continuously with the music. Trails are short: the warp keeps about 0.82 of the previous frame per step (half-life about 3.4 frames, about 0.11 s), linear and the same on all channels, so streaks fade within a fraction of a second and the picture is not smeared. Source-estimated speed is low (about 0.3 viewport widths/s assumed, interval 0-1), while the calibrated display estimate says mean flow about 0.2 screen widths per second (interval 0.08-0.40) and frame difference 0.075, so a moderately lively, not frozen, image. These numbers are estimates, not observed motion.

Brightness and contrast. Not resolved from source alone: brightness.mean_encoded_luma and feedback half-life are unknown. The calibrated display estimate gives mean luminance about 0.29 (interval 0.11-0.53), so a mid-dark to medium picture, with a faint dark background and brighter dots and discs on top. The border darkens the centre diamond a little each frame (up to 3/32 per frame). The built-in wave alpha is 0.001, so the dotted trace may be very faint (additive with tiny alpha) even though its colour is at full level; its visibility is uncertain. wave_0, wave_2 have mean alpha about 0.2 and shape_0 alpha 0.08, so these are soft, translucent layers. The red centre disc (alpha 1) is the one fully opaque element, if not cut by later layers or the composite. The calibrated estimate predicts about 28 global brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 9-106), which suggests noticeable pulsing.

Audio response. Bass, mid and treble (and their smoothed versions) all drive the feedback zoom, continuously, with no known gain, so the whole image likely breathes or pushes in and out with the music. Bass enlarges the faint shape_0 disc (gain 0.3 on radius) and bass and treble both gate the opacity of the large shape_2 disc (threshold or clamped), so the large red/teal disc may flash in and out on beats. The smoothed treble drives the warp shader's red, green and blue (thresholded), so a treble-linked colour shift or brightening of the whole feedback image is likely. wave_1 reads bass as a frame input. The built-in dotted trace follows the left waveform shape. The mood layer assumes about 2 Hz beat-coupled changes and about 3.6-4 Hz coherent transitions; these are assumed, not measured, so I cannot say it is a strobe, but flashing is possible and is not ruled out. The source model lists no eligible mood group; approximate estimate: reactive, medium to high energy (intensity score 49, display activity 0.83 with Intense listed, though the interval 0.34-0.99 spans all bands).

Uncertainty. Confidence is low. Unknown or unresolved: the zoom value range, the radius of shape_0, positions of wave_1, the actual composite look after mirror and tiling (including how the tiling and fold show up), whether the point waves are visible given positions partly off-screen, whether the very low alpha on the built-in trace hides it, and the true overall brightness. The recipe notes the final palette and dominance are conditional, mesh palette is unresolved, and it is an approximate recipe, not a pixel specification. Real frames will differ because of drawn content, audio and native rounding. Mood tags are empty; possible tags Reactive and Flashing are assumption-based only.
