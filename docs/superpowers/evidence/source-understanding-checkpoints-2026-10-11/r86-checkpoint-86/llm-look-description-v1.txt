LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r86 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. The picture is a glowing, warm-toned feedback tunnel built around the screen centre. A single built-in audio waveform line (mode 1, "stereo polar rotating trace", centred at 0.5/0.5, ring radius about 0.53 model units, not dots, not thick, not additive) is drawn each frame and swung round the centre at a nominal 2.3 rad/s. Its shape follows the left and right waveform, so it is a wobbling ring or loop that turns with the music. The composite shader then shows the canvas through a reciprocal-radius polar (tunnel-like) mapping and adds two repeating radial glow-spot fields (a grid of soft bright dots, gain/distance from each cell centre). So the expected look is a ring-shaped trace seen through a tiled, tunnel-style lattice of glowing spots. The tunnel is a candidate only: the recipe says tunnel appearance is not guaranteed, and how prominent the glow grid is on screen is conditional. Whether the spots read as particles is unestablished. There is no mirror symmetry (score 0).

Colour. The wave is authored orange: red 0.9, green 0.45, blue 0, but it is max-normalised, so the brightest channel scales up to 1, giving a full-strength orange line (roughly 1.0 / 0.5 / 0). The wave's alpha is authored at 5.9, so it is effectively opaque. The only source-derived hue for the overall picture is orange. The settled-canvas hue family is orange, with a very dark lower-bound level (display about 0.025/0.021/0.018). The composite's colour palette is otherwise unresolved. It depends on the sampled canvas, and bass-driven colour terms in the composite are threshold or clamp shaped with no known gain. I would expect an orange, ember-like, mostly warm picture on a dark ground, with some colour variation from the composite. The recipe does not say which other hues appear. The composite maps flat canvas grey through a strongly boosted ramp: black goes to about 0.02, 0.25 goes to about 0.52, and 0.5 or more goes to white. So mid-level content is pushed bright and washed out. The calibrated display estimate puts mean saturation at about 0.32 (interval 0.05 to 0.59) and warm/cool at about 0.35 (wide interval 0.02 to 0.70). I treat that as weak evidence and the hue as uncertain.

Feedback and motion. Each frame the previous image is multiplied by about 0.932 (no custom warp shader, decay applies). That gives a half-life of about 8 frames, or roughly 0.27 s, so trails are short, not long smears. Bright content persists better than dim content. The warp map is a uniform zoom of 1.00049 about the centre (about 0.049% expansion per step, no rotation, no warp term), so drawn content drifts slowly outward from the centre, and the ring and its echoes creep outward. There is also a per-pixel translation (dx, dy) that depends on x, y, time and the treble, mid and bass inputs (treb, treb_avg and others). Its form is nonlinear and unresolved, so I can not state its direction or size. It is a source of extra wobble or drift that probably reacts to the music. A thin outer border (about 0.4% of the screen) is blended in black each frame. The calibrated estimate for movement is about 0.16 screen widths per second flow (interval 0.06 to 0.35), and frame-to-frame change is moderate. This is an estimate, not a measurement.

Audio reaction. The waveform line follows the audio continuously, as a shape response. Bass, mid and treble all feed the warp's x/y translation, so the whole feedback field shifts or wobbles with the music. Bass also feeds the composite's red, green and blue terms, so bass should modulate brightness or colour of the glow field. The gains are unknown and the responses are thresholded or clamped. The recipe gives no visible strength and does not establish a beat-synchronised pulse or whole-screen flash. A global brightness jump rate is estimated at about 2.4 per 10 s, but the interval is very wide (0.1 to 11.5). Flash-rate numbers in the mood block are assumed values, so I do not rely on them. Overall activity is estimated Normal (0.46, plausible range Chill to Intense), so I describe it as moderately active.

Brightness. The recipe gives mean luminance of about 0.19 (interval 0.002 to 0.43), so the picture is probably medium-dark with a bright orange ring and glow points. It could be much darker or brighter than that. The brightness comes from the display estimate, not from the flat-canvas lower bound.

Random, chaotic or undefined. No per-load random branches, random redraws or random textures are listed (random_textures is empty, random_redraws is null). No undefined or nonfinite shader output is reported (nonfinite share 0). The warp feedback persistence is a conditional estimate from flat colours. The bass colour response, the translation field and the glow-grid prominence are unresolved. Nothing here establishes a chaotic or strobing look, but I can not rule one out.
