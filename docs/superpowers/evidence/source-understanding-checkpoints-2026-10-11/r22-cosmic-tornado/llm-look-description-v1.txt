LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r22 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):
Expect a centred, glowing circular waveform ring (built-in wave mode 0, "audio_deformed_circle" at x=0.5,y=0.5, additive, thin line) whose outline follows the right-channel audio waveform and slowly rotates (about 0.2 rad/s nominal).
That ring is smeared by feedback: a radius-dependent zoom (zoom varies with rad, zoomexp 1) and a radius-dependent swirl (rot depends on rad and bass_att), plus image-driven self-advection in the warp shader (sample offsets up to about +/-0.0135 UV). Expect flowing, smeared, spiralling, tunnel-or-vortex-like trails around the centre, not a crisp geometric figure.
There is no drift: dx and dy are 0, so nothing slides sideways; motion is swirl and zoom about the screen centre. Decay/persistence is unresolved (decay null), so how long trails last and how dense they get is unknown.
Colour: the wave's green is constant (0.65), red cycles slowly (period about 18.8 s), blue oscillates extremely fast (period about 0.014 s, a raw source formula, not a visible flicker rate). Over 120 s the raw colour passes through green (26%), blue, orange, pink, yellow, cyan, with little red or purple; broad multicolour, fairly saturated (about 0.66), roughly neutral between warm and cool. These are raw wave colours, not verified displayed colours; the warp and mesh palettes are unresolved.
Brightness: gamma weight 1.7 can brighten up to 1.7x before clamping, and wave alpha 4.1 is additive, so the ring may look bright; mean brightness and fill are not resolved from source. Flat areas decay to black, so expect a dark background with the glowing swirl on it.
Audio: smoothed bass (bass_att) changes the swirl rotation, with a clamped response and unknown gain; the waveform shape itself follows audio continuously. No beat-flash route was found, but absence is not proof.
Unresolved: trail decay, ring thickness and visible coverage, how much the multicolour palette is actually visible, final brightness, and real on-screen speed (the recipe's mood speed values are assumed).
Approximate mood (estimated, not strict eligibility, which is empty): smooth, hypnotic, psychedelic, organic/liquid; activity most likely Chill, possibly Normal, with medium confidence and no flashing mechanism found.
Expected: a dark screen with a bright, multicoloured (green/blue/orange/pink) audio-wobbled ring at the centre, smeared by a slow, smooth bass-modulated swirl and zoom into liquid spiral trails; calm to moderate, non-flashing, trail length unknown.
