An abstract, left-right mirrored colour field with no discrete drawn objects that I can identify. The recipe lists no waveform, shape or point layer at all. Everything on screen comes from the feedback map plus a composite shader that appears to generate the image itself from eight randomly redrawn values (q1 to q8, set from random state that changes at unknown moments). So any rings, blobs or bands are produced by that shader and are a random draw: the layout and colours probably differ from one load to the next, and I cannot describe a specific shape.

Symmetry: the composite blends in a mirrored copy, so the picture is almost exactly symmetric about the vertical centre line (score about 1.0, two-fold). Expect a mirrored, kaleidoscope-like or Rorschach-like composition.

Colour and brightness: the evidence conflicts, so this is uncertain. The composite applies a randomly phased four-corner colour tint that cycles over roughly 15 to 20 seconds, and it acts as a negative: on a nearly black canvas it shows a pale, light grey-green, goes dark at mid canvas levels and returns to light at full levels. Read alone, that suggests a pale to bright image. The calibrated estimate gives moderate brightness (mean luminance about 0.46, bright facet just short of its threshold) and fairly strong saturation (about 0.62, with about 60 percent of the screen coloured). Leading hues are green (about 0.27), blue (0.17) and pink (0.13), with no clear single winner after that. Because the tint is random per load, name the hue as changing between loads. Warm versus cool is balanced.

Motion: feedback is very light. The image contracts slowly toward the centre by 0.05 percent per frame, and bass, mid and treble (smoothed) shift the translation in both axes, though gains are unresolved and clamped. Decay is 1, so nothing fades from the warp itself and old content persists; the border is the only drain, a thin inner band (about 4 percent of the screen) at half strength. Estimated flow is about 0.1 screen widths per second, slow and smooth, with a drifting character (facet probability 0.61, just above threshold).

Flashing: the no-strobe probability is only 0.44, so it is not strobe-free. The jumps estimate is low (about 0.5 per 10 seconds, interval 0 to 4.4), so I expect occasional soft brightness pulses, not a strobe. Audio responds mainly through slow translation shifts, so reaction to beats should be gentle and smeared. Largest uncertainty: what the composite actually draws.
