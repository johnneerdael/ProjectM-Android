Preset 203: look description (prediction from the static recipe only)

Overall look: a cool, blue-leaning, fluid and cellular picture of medium brightness. Small bright seeds in the middle of the screen grow into flowing, smeared blobs and splatter that spread over the canvas. It should look organic and liquid rather than geometric, and the motion is fairly lively.

Structure: the only clearly visible drawn element is a 512-point custom wave (wave_2) confined to a central box (about x 0.31 to 0.69, y 0.30 to 0.70). Its dots are mostly blue and moderately saturated, with full alpha. The built-in two-trace wave is drawn as dots at alpha 0.001 in a dull orange-brown (0.5, 0.4, 0.3), so it is essentially invisible. A thin black border band (about 1% of the screen) is redrawn every frame at the edge, so the edge is probably kept dark. The warp shader has two key mechanisms. One adds a fine fed-back level and subtracts a coarser blur (band-pass sharpening, with flat-content gain about 0.91). That grows seeds into blobs, cells and splatter, so most of the screen is patterned rather than a dark background with a few dots. The other is image-driven advection, where the picture steers its own sampling coordinates, giving flowing, smeared, fluid structure.

Motion: there is a constant slow turn of -0.02 rad per step. The zoom varies across the screen from about 0.53 to 1.73 with a strong radial exponent (2.0), so parts pull inward and parts expand, with no translation. The zoom is also modulated by smoothed bass, mid and treble, so the flow speeds up and slows with the music. Trails are short, with a half-life of about 6 frames (0.27 s). The look is therefore continuously regenerated by the pattern-growth stage, not a long smear. The calibrated flow is fairly high, about 0.32 screen widths per second (0.15 to 0.60), with moderate frame-to-frame change.

Colour: the steady state is blue (display about 0.18, 0.33, 0.39), and the calibrated warm/cool is cool (-0.38). The composite is steep: a canvas level of 0.0625 shows at about 0.53, and 0.125 and above clip to white. Detail in the grey test comes out white and unsaturated, so bright cell edges likely wash toward white, with blue-teal in dimmer areas. Calibrated saturation is about 0.54 and luminance about 0.38.

Audio and flashing: the music mainly modulates the zoom flow. Brightness jumps are estimated at about 3.5 per 10 s (0.5 to 15.8). That means occasional pulses, possibly more frequent flicker, because the composite is steep and small canvas changes swing brightness hard. It is not a designed strobe.

Uncertain: the exact cell structure, how much of the screen is white or blue, and how strongly the zoom reacts.
