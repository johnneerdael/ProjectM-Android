Approximate look (source-only, uncertain): a busy, fairly bright, strongly reactive picture built from a feedback haze with a ring-like audio waveform and many small 32-sided polygons drawn into it.

Brightness and colour: the calibrated display estimate gives mid-level mean luminance (about 0.39, plausible range 0.20-0.62), moderate saturation (about 0.57) and a warm lean (warm/cool about +0.31, but the interval reaches slightly cool). The steady-state hue cue is orange, with a tan/amber display level around RGB (0.53, 0.44, 0.29). The composite shader shows the canvas through a time-varying ramp: where the canvas is near black it reads as dark blue (about 0.15, 0.24, 0.47), mid levels go muddy grey-brown, and bright levels go amber/tan. So expect a blue-dark background with amber-tan highlights, with the tint drifting over time. Shape colours depend on bass, mid and treble, and I cannot name a dominant hue from them.

Structure: a circular waveform line centred on the screen (radius about 0.5 model units, drawn white and max-normalised, but with very low alpha 0.001, so it may be almost invisible by itself). Three groups of 32-sided polygons (33, 7 and 33 instances) are drawn; one group copies the previous frame into itself. Their positions are audio-driven and unknown, so I cannot say where they sit. A thin black border takes about 1% of the screen edge.

Motion: the warp zooms about the centre by 0 to about 41% per step, with bass raising it. There is no rotation and no drift. A custom warp shader steers the image by its own content with small offsets (about 0.005-0.011 of the screen) and an 8-tap blur that grows towards the edges. That should give smeared, fluid, soft streaks and haze. Trail persistence is unresolved, since the warp-transfer result looks degenerate.

Audio: the zoom pulses with bass. Polygon sizes, opacity and colours respond to all three bands, and the composite colour responds too.

Flashing: the estimates say strong. Activity is Intense (about 0.999), frame difference is high (about 0.20), and brightness jumps are estimated at roughly 110 per 10 s, with a very wide interval. Treat it as a likely flickery or strobing preset. The strict source model grants no flash-safe certification.
