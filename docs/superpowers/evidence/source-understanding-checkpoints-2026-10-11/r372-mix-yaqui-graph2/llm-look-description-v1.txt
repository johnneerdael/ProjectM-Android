Approximate look (static source facts only, nothing rendered): a warm-toned, organic, blotchy pattern that grows and flows by itself, seeded by many small round dots, with a black background showing between the patterned areas.

Colour and brightness: the empty canvas shows as black, so the picture starts dark and the patterns carry the light. The calibrated display estimate gives moderate brightness (mean luminance about 0.32, range 0.15 to 0.54; the bright facet is just over its threshold) and moderate saturation (about 0.48). Hue shares point to a warm palette with orange leading (about 57%), then yellow (22%), cyan (11%) and a little green, red and blue. The composite turns mid-level content orange or salmon and strong content a saturated red, while the feedback stage keeps only green and blue and wipes red completely each frame. So I expect cyan-green seeds that the composite re-colours into orange, red and yellow, with some cyan fringes. The leading-hue gap is large, so orange is a reasonable lead, but the exact balance is uncertain.

Structures: the base is a mixed pattern-growth feedback. A fine-scale green-lane image is boosted (gain about 1.3) and a coarser blur is subtracted each frame, so small seeds grow into blobs, cells or splatter over much of the screen instead of staying as isolated marks. The composite adds a relief or embossed look and self-steering flow, so the texture should read as lit, smeared, fluid-like ridges. Seeds come from four shape layers of small 25-sided polygons (about 0.02 to 0.03 radius, so near-circular dots, roughly 34, 106, 84 and 94 copies), four custom wave or point layers of 512 points each, and a built-in two-trace waveform that is nearly invisible (alpha 0.001). The wave layers resolve to a tight cluster near x 0.9, y 0.9 in the source, but this may change with audio. Where the dots sit is otherwise unresolved.

Motion: the mesh has no rotation, drift or stretch, and only a tiny warp, but zoom is driven by bass, mid and treble (clamped), so expect pulsing in and out. The shader samples shift the picture by about 1 to 2% of the screen in the feedback, and by more in the composite. Estimated flow is about 0.24 screen widths per second, moderate. No trail half-life could be resolved.

Audio: bass and treble move the dots, all three bands change their colour and opacity, and all three drive zoom.

Flashing: the no-strobe probability is about 0.89 and estimated brightness jumps are under about 3 per 10 s, so expect mostly steady with possible occasional pulses.
