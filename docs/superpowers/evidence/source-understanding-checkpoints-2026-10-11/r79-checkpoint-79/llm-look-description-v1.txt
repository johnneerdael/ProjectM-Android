LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r79 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a self-feeding, fluid-looking field rather than a clean drawn figure. Three small drawn seeds are injected every frame: a built-in dot-mode stereo "XY trace" (mode 2, dots only, centred at 0.5,0.5, so scattered points following the left/right waveform), a custom wave of 512 points per frame that stays roughly in the x 0.16-0.83, y 0.06-0.94 region, and one stationary, soft 10-sided polygon in the middle (radius about 0.42, pale blue-white fill at alpha 0.2 fading to transparent blue at the edge, no border). The warp shader then does the real work: it adds a fine copy of the previous frame, subtracts a coarse blur (band-pass sharpening), advects the image along its own gradients and through blurred copies of itself, and shows it through local slopes (embossed relief). So the seeds should grow into blobs, cells, splatter and smeared, organic flow that covers most of the screen, not a dark background with a few dots. A mesh warp with spatially varying zoom (depends on radius, x, y) adds a slowly breathing tunnel or expansion about the centre. There is a thin black outer border (about 1% of the screen) redrawn each frame. The recipe finds no mirror or kaleidoscope symmetry (score 0), so the picture should be asymmetric. I would call it organic, liquid and psychedelic (the recipe's own estimated tags), as an approximate interpretation only.

Colour: the final palette is not resolved. The built-in wave colour cycles over time (r up to 0.22/s, g 0.27/s, b 0.36/s; over 120 s about green 19%, pink 19%, red 15%, orange 13%, blue 12%, yellow 12%, purple 7%), and the custom wave admits broad hues. These are raw source curves, not verified displayed colours. The feedback steady state points to a yellow-ish family (display RGB about 1.0, 1.0, 0.08), a lower bound that is only partly reliable. The feedback loop keeps red and green almost fully (gain 0.996 and 0.99, with a small positive offset), while blue is cut hard (gain 0.8, halving in about 3 frames). Over time this should tint trails toward yellow, orange or green and strip out blue, apart from fresh blue/white seed content. The composite shows flat canvas levels as neutral grey, then clips to white from about half level upward, so bright areas may blow out to white. Calibrated estimates give high saturation (about 0.80, interval 0.52-1.0) and a neutral-to-slightly-warm balance (0.57, interval 0.23-0.92). Treat these as estimates with wide uncertainty.

Motion and feedback: trails are long in red and green (no fade on flat content, since the steady level from full is 1.0) and short in blue. The shape does not move (centre speed bound 0). Motion comes from the feedback map: advection offsets of up to roughly 0.13-0.14 in UV units, plus zoom, rotation and translation controls. The calibrated mean flow is slow, about 0.06 screen widths/s (interval 0 to 0.18), with a small frame-to-frame difference (about 0.03, interval 0.0006-0.07). So expect slow, smeared drifting, not fast motion. Whether trails visibly pile up or settle is not established. Gap fill, density and coverage are unverified.

Audio: the built-in dot trace follows the waveform continuously (left/right channels), so points shift with the sound. Mid and treble bands drive the mesh warp's zoom, rotation, x/y translation and x scale, but the recipe marks all of these as threshold-or-clamped with unknown linear gain (the y scale gain is 0), so the strength and shape of the response are unresolved. Bass has no listed route. I would expect treble/mid hits to cause kicks or shifts in the warp rather than smooth pulsing, but I cannot say how visible they are or whether they sync to the beat.

Brightness, flashing and uncertainty: estimated mean luminance is about 0.39 (interval 0.20-0.62), which is moderately bright with a wide range. Global brightness jumps are estimated at about 0.34 per 10 s (interval 0 to 3.9), so occasional jumps are possible but flashing is unverified. The estimated activity is low (0.14; Chill, possibly Normal), which is a calibrated source estimate, not a measurement. The strict model gives no eligible mood band. No random per-load switches or random redraws are listed (random_redraws is null), so I would not expect per-load randomness from the recipe. The less certain parts are the palette dominance, the exact visible audio response and the screen coverage of the patterns. The relief stage's flat-area look, whether the output is chaotic, and any undefined output are not flagged as undefined (output_undefined false, nonfinite share 0).
