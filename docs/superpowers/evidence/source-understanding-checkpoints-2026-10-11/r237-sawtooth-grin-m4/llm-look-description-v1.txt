Likely a bright, busy, fast-moving swirl: many small polygons being dragged into a spiral that sucks toward the screen centre, shown with high contrast and mixed grey and cool colour. The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.52 (interval 0.35 to 0.74), and the bright facet is well above its threshold, so it should read as bright rather than dark. Saturation is moderate (about 0.47) with about half the screen coloured. Hues are green (0.30) and blue (0.26), a gap of only 0.04, so I would call them equals, with smaller shares of pink, red and yellow. Much of the picture may look grey or white. The warp shader keeps green and blue almost whole while red fades (red half-life about 50 frames), which pushes persistent trails toward cyan, blue and green. The composite is steep: dark canvas levels (under about 0.06) display as black, and mid-to-bright levels jump sharply toward white. Expect crisp bright structure on a dark ground.

Structures: two polygon shape layers, a 14-sided one with 512 instances and a 23-sided one with 1024 instances (rotated about 0.44 rad). Their positions, radius and colours depend on audio and on random q values that are unresolved, so their placement and tint are unknown. A built-in two-channel waveform drawn additively at the centre has alpha 0.001, so it is probably nearly invisible. The composite repeats the image in mirrored tiles, so I expect a tiled or kaleidoscopic repeat of the pattern.

Motion: this is the strongest feature. Each feedback step zooms the image toward the centre by about 15.7% and rotates it 0.1 rad, a strong tunnel or vortex. The warp also steers its own sampling from the blurred image (self-advection), which smears and bends the structure into a fluid, organic look. Trails retain about 96% per frame. Ten of the q values are redrawn at random at about 3.1 s and then every 12.6 s, so shapes and colours probably change abruptly at those moments.

Audio and flashing: bass, mid and treble drive shape position, opacity and colour through thresholded routes, and bass and treble enlarge shape radii by a small linear gain (0.03 to 0.04). The calibrated no-strobe probability is only 0.53, so it flickers and is not strobe-free. Estimated brightness jumps are about 2 per 10 s, with an interval from 0 to about 9. The activity estimate is Normal to Intense, and the drifting and normal facets are above their thresholds.

Uncertain: the actual shape colours and sizes, how much of the screen the shapes fill, and whether the tunnel looks smooth or jagged.
