LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r124 v1, core recipe view; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. After about 5 seconds I expect a mostly dark screen with a sparse, geometric picture drawn on it, not a dense tunnel or fractal. The main drawn element is a group of five-sided polygons (shape_1: sides 5, 256 configured instances). Their centres and radii are unknown in the recipe (centre_path "unknown", radius null, center_source_xy [null, null]), so I cannot say whether the 256 pentagons form a ring, a scatter, or a stack at one spot, or how large they are. Beside them there is a built-in audio waveform drawn as a circle of dots around the screen centre (wave_x/wave_y 0.5, dots true, thick true, additive). Its alpha is only 0.001 (wave_a 0.0010000000474974513), so those dots will be faint to invisible on their own. They may only show where the polygons or feedback add to them. The feedback transport pulls the whole image toward the centre by 2% per step (zoom 0.98, zoomexp 1, rot 0, dx and dy 0). There is no rotation, no sideways drift, and no mirroring or kaleidoscope (symmetry about 0.2, with no mechanism found). A thin 2% black border is drawn at the outer edge every frame. Overall structure score: geometric 0.8, fractal 0.0 to 0.3, organic about 0.15, all low-evidence.

Trails. The warp multiplies the previous frame by only about 0.184 per frame on every channel (gain_rgb 0.184, halving about 0.41 frames, steady level from full 0). Trails are therefore almost absent. Whatever is drawn is mostly redrawn fresh each frame, and any leftover is gone in two or three frames. The contraction at 2% per step is hard to see because so little survives to be moved. The picture should look crisp and clean, not smeared or echoing.

Colour. The composite lifts the canvas strongly toward pink and white. For a flat canvas level of 0.25 it shows (1.0, 0.42, 1.0), a hot pink-magenta. At 0.5 it shows (1.0, 0.80, 1.0), a pale pink. At 0.75 and above it shows pure white. This does not change over time (time_varying false). So any polygon that lands on the canvas should read as bright pink to white, and low-level content is pushed up in brightness. The wave's own colour is also magenta-pink (1.0, 0.49, 1.0). The recipe's settled-colour estimate is pink (display about 0.45, 0.18, 0.30). The colour of the polygons themselves is unresolved (colour mode_code null, audio routes drive their red, green and blue). Expect a pink, magenta, white palette on black. The recipe's palette numbers lean slightly cool (warm_cool about -0.24 from the source, +0.24 from the calibrated display estimate), which is contradictory, so I would not claim warm or cool. Saturation is moderate: about 0.6 from source, about 0.41 (0.13 to 0.68) from the display estimate. Hue variety is low (about 1.9 effective hue bins) and nothing cycles through hues (hue rate 0). Because the polygon shader colours depend on audio, some drift in tint toward other hues during loud passages is possible, but I cannot size it.

Brightness and contrast. The calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.10 (10th to 90th percentile range 0.0 to 0.34). That points to a dark picture with bright pink and white elements. Because the composite maps even weak content to bright pink or white, contrast between the black background and the drawn shapes should be high and the lit areas should look saturated, not dim. The wave is nearly invisible, so brightness depends mostly on how large the polygons are and how many overlap. That is unknown, which is why the luminance interval is wide and reaches both nearly black and fairly bright.

Motion. It is calm to moderate. The estimated mean flow is about 0.043 screen widths per second (0 to 0.14), and the mean frame-to-frame difference is about 0.014 (0 to 0.05). The 2% inward contraction per step is the only guaranteed motion, and with almost no trails it is hardly visible as flow. The source model's speed estimate of 0.35 viewport units per second (0.18 to 0.63) is an upper-style bound from assumed rates and I would not treat it as typical. The built-in wave nominally rotates at 0.2 rad/s, but it is nearly invisible. The shapes are marked as moving, and their audio-driven radius changes could make them pulse or swell, but their position paths are unknown. No audio-triggered scene switches were found (discontinuities 0 Hz, though that is not proof of absence).

Audio response. The pentagons' radius and red, green and blue are tied to the smoothed bass, mid and treble bands. The links are continuous for colour and thresholded or clamped for radius, with no known gain values. So expect the pentagons to swell and shift tint with the music, gradually, because the bands are smoothed. The shader stages also read raw bass, mid and treble for their colour, as thresholded or clamped responses, which could produce quicker tint or brightness changes on louder hits, with unknown strength. The waveform dots follow the right audio channel, but at alpha 0.001 you will barely see it.

Flashing. I do not see a source mechanism for whole-screen flashing. There is no strobe gate and no audio-driven scene switch. The assumed numbers in the recipe (about 4 Hz coherent transitions with a peak step of 0.2 to 0.3, brightness jump about 0.1 to 0.3) are placeholders for the audio-driven colour routes, not found events. The calibrated estimate says about 0.5 global brightness jumps per 10 seconds (0 to 4.7), and its activity band is mostly Chill, possibly Normal (activity 0.05, range 0 to 0.54). The older source-only mood score leans Normal, possibly Intense. I read this as: no regular strobing, but bass-linked brightness or tint pulses on strong beats are plausible. Because the composite sharply boosts any bright content to white, a sudden growth in polygon size on a loud hit could look like a noticeable flare, so I would not call it flash-safe.

Uncertainty. The biggest unknowns are where the 256 pentagons sit and how large they get, their actual colours, and the shader-stage details (the warp and composite shader palettes are unresolved except for the flat-canvas colour mapping above). Overall brightness cannot be pinned down: it could be nearly black with small pink specks or a fairly full pink and white scene. The wave's contribution is likely negligible. The warm or cool reading is contradictory between estimates. Everything here comes from source numbers and calibrated estimates, not from a rendered frame.
