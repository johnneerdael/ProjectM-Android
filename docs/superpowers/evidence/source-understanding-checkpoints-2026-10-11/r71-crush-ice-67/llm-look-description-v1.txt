LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r71 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a dense, smeared, fluid-looking feedback canvas rather than a clean dark background with a few shapes. Two shader stages steer the image with its own blurred copy (self-advection in both warp and composite), and the warp also adds a fine level and subtracts a coarser blur every frame (band-pass sharpening). Small seeds therefore grow into blobs, cells and splatter that spread over most of the screen, with a liquid or ice-crush texture (the recipe tags it "Organic / Liquid"). Drawn on top are a built-in audio waveform line (a rotating stereo polar trace, mode 1, additive, not dots, base radius about 0.53, rotating about 2.3 rad/s), a small 12-sided shape (2 instances, radius about 0.1, red source colour, moving), a very large 36-sided shape (radius about 0.82, near-black green source colour, moving with a position driven by audio) and a 512-point custom waveform. Where these sit on screen is unknown: the shape centres and the waveform position are random or audio-driven and not resolved. How visible each layer is stays conditional.

Colour: the recipe does not give a firm palette. Source ingredients are blue for the built-in and custom waves, grayscale/red for the small shape and grayscale/green for the large shape. The feedback and warp palette is unresolved. The composite is a custom shader that maps the canvas through a colour ramp: black content shows pale yellow-green or cream, and bright content shows a blue/teal, with orange and yellow in between (flat-transfer levels 0 to 1 go from about (0.96, 1.0, 0.83) through orange and amber to about (0.16, 0.72, 0.88)). So the picture is likely a warm-to-cool, saturated mix. The settled steady-state hue family is pink (about 0.96, 0.69, 0.82). The calibrated estimate gives mean saturation about 0.61 (0.33 to 0.88) and warm/cool about +0.26, with a wide interval (-0.08 to 0.61), so it leans slightly warm but is uncertain. There is no source hue cycling (hue_cycle_per_s 0). The recipe shows no per-load random hue switch, only the random redraws below.

Feedback and motion: the previous frame is multiplied by roughly 0.996 per frame. The warp transfer on flat colour is about 0.994 gain plus a small 0.006 offset in red and green and unity in blue, so flat areas do not fade. They saturate to white in about 4.3 s. The recipe says this empty-canvas state does not describe the real picture, since edges, drawn elements and advection change it. The warp zoom contracts toward the screen centre by only about 0.049% per step, a slow inward drift. Rotation is zero. The sharper motion comes from the advection offsets, up to a few percent of the screen in the warp and up to about 0.1 of a screen in the composite. Together these give smearing, flowing, liquid streaks. The calibrated flow estimate is about 0.32 screen widths per second (0.15 to 0.61) and the frame-to-frame change is moderate (about 0.078).

Audio: bass, mid and treble all feed the warp translation (x and y), the built-in wave position, and the large shape's position. All of these are thresholded or clamped responses and the recipe gives no linear gain, so they probably push the picture around in jumps, and the strength is unknown. The waveform line follows the left and right audio continuously, so the trace shape wobbles with the music. The recipe does not give a beat-locked whole-screen pulse. Brightness jumps are estimated as very low (about 0.08 per 10 s) but the interval runs up to about 3 per 10 s, so occasional jumps cannot be ruled out.

Brightness and activity: mean luminance is estimated at about 0.43 (0.24 to 0.67), so mid-bright and probably light and busy rather than dark. The calibrated activity percentile is 0.77 (Intense), but the interval is very wide (0.20 to 0.94) and the possible bands span Chill, Normal and Intense. Treat this as moderately to highly active but low confidence. Strict eligible mood groups are empty.

Random or chaotic: q1, q2, the wave x/y and the shape positions are redrawn with random values at unspecified times (no event times or period are given). Wave and shape placement and the colours that depend on them are a random draw, so they differ from load to load and change during playback. The advection and band-pass feedback loop is chaotic, so fine detail will not be repeatable. The overall look (a smeared, liquid, splatter-textured field) should be similar every time. The recipe is an approximation and not pixel-exact.
