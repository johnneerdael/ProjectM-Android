LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r68 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a kaleidoscopic, tiled, mostly grey-white feedback pattern. The composite folds the image into 7 angular mirror sectors and also repeats it in unmirrored tiles, so everything drawn is multiplied into a symmetric, patterned field. The drawn seeds are an audio waveform line (built-in mode 6, an angled trace through the screen centre, thick and additive, drawn at about 31% alpha in flat grey-white 0.65/0.65/0.65) and four small polygon shapes (a 4-sided one near x=0.37, two 5-sided ones that drift slowly in small sine paths of about 5 and 4 seconds, and a 6-sided one of radius about 0.16 that is yellow at the centre fading to red, with white borders). Shape sizes and spin involve a random draw at preset load (rand in the shape init), so exact sizes and starting angles differ each load; I cannot say by how much. The waveform plus shapes appear to be what the mirror and tiling stage replicate, but how prominent each is on screen is not established.

Colour: the recipe resolves almost no hue. The steady state is "grayscale" and the composite flat-colour map is neutral grey (no tint, not time-varying), so the base look is monochrome white and grey. Colour can only come from the 6-sided shape (yellow to red, possibly pink or orange) and from the bass, mid and treble routes into the warp's red, green and blue, whose strength is unresolved. The estimated saturation is moderate (about 0.5, wide range 0.23 to 0.78) and warm/cool is slightly warm but uncertain. I would expect a mostly white-grey kaleidoscope with warm yellow or red accents, not a rainbow. This is an estimate from calibrated source facts, not a measurement.

Motion and feedback: the warp has no translation or rotation. It is a radial zoom (base zoom about 0.9, with zoomexp near 2.99, so the effect depends strongly on distance from the centre) that changes with bass, mid and treble and with time. The feedback is not a simple fade. The warp wraps colour levels around the range, which the recipe calls continuous colour cycling with a period of about 3.35 s (about 100 frames), and mid-grey content is retained at roughly 97 to 98.5% per frame. The ordinary trail half-life is unresolved (null). The expected look is a tunnel-like pull towards or away from the centre, repeated through the 7-fold mirror, with brightness bands cycling slowly through the picture. Estimated flow is about 0.19 screen widths per second (range 0.08 to 0.39), with moderate frame-to-frame change.

Audio reaction: bass, mid and treble all feed the zoom and the warp colour channels, but through clamps and thresholds, so the strength is unknown. The waveform line follows the left-channel waveform. I would expect the tunnel zoom and overall brightness or colour to lurch with the music, and the line to wiggle with the signal. No beat-locked whole-screen pulse is established.

Brightness and chaos: the steady-state estimate is bright (display near white, 0.995, as a lower bound), while the calibrated mean luminance estimate is lower and uncertain at about 0.32 (range 0.13 to 0.55). The calibrated estimate also suggests many brightness jumps, about 44 per 10 seconds with a very wide range of 14 to 167. The name suggests strobing, but that comes from the name, which is not evidence; the recipe's own estimate gives an Intense activity band (about 0.86, possibly Normal). The strict source model records no eligible mood group. Random per load: the shape radii and rotations. There is no per-load branching in the composite and no random palette redraws (random_redraws is null). Chaotic or erratic: the audio-driven zoom through clamps, and the wrap-around colour cycling, may make the picture jumpy. Treat the flash rate as unresolved.
