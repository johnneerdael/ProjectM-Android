LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r118 v1, core recipe view; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. A feedback-driven, smeared and swirling field of cyan and blue light, seeded by a ring or loop of dots drawn at the screen centre. The only drawn element is the built-in waveform in mode 5 ("rotating_quadratic_stereo_trace"), drawn as dots (not a line), centred at (0.5, 0.5) and slowly rotating at a nominal 0.3 rad/s. It reads the left and right waveform channels, so the dot figure is a stereo trace that wobbles with the audio. Each frame the previous image is resampled through a warp that does two things at once: a band-pass step (a finer fed-back level is added and a coarser blur subtracted), which makes small seeds grow into blobs, cells and splatter spread over the canvas, and a self-advection step where the image steers its own sampling coordinates (offsets up to about 0.004 of the screen), which gives a flowing, smeared, fluid look. After about 5 seconds I expect most of the screen to be patterned with soft organic cells and streaks rather than a dark background with a few dots. The pattern is densest and most legible around the centre where the dots are injected. A thin frame of colour sits at the screen edge: an outer border of about 2% of the screen in dark red (0.5, 0.01, 0.01) at alpha 0.95, and an inner border of about 2% in dark blue (0.01, 0.01, 0.5) at alpha 0.95, redrawn every frame. I cannot say from the recipe whether the red outer edge stays visible or is washed out by the cyan composite, since the composite re-colours every canvas level. No mirror or kaleidoscope symmetry is supported (symmetry score 0.0).

Colour. The final colour is set mostly by the custom composite, which maps the grey canvas level to a fixed cyan ramp (the recipe says it is not time varying): black stays black, 25% level shows as saturated teal-blue (about 0.02, 0.44, 0.66), 50% as bright cyan (about 0.23, 0.75, 0.86), 75% as pale icy cyan (about 0.71, 0.93, 0.97), and full white stays white. So the image should read as black-to-blue-to-cyan-to-white, cool and fairly saturated. The source steady state names the hue family as blue, with the settled display colour about (0, 0.05, 0.23), a deep blue. The calibrated estimate agrees: cool (warm_cool about -0.44, interval -0.78 to -0.09) and fairly saturated (about 0.72, interval 0.44 to 0.99). The waveform dots have an authored base of 0.5 per channel, plus audio terms, so they start as mid grey and tint with the music. Because the composite only sees canvas level, the dots mostly show as bright cyan-white points on the blue field. The warp's own colour terms (treble to red, bass to green, mid to blue) have unknown gain in the recipe, so any drift of the palette toward green or red with the music is possible but not quantified. I do not expect warm colours except possibly the thin red border or brief tints.

Motion. The warp has a per-step rotation of up to +/-0.04 rad (this is per feedback frame, not per second; its time derivative is small, at most about 0.018 rad/s control rate) and a radius-dependent zoom of 1.20 to 1.31 per step, acting about the screen centre. A zoom above 1 here samples closer to the centre, so content should expand outward from the middle. That is a strong expansion per step, but the recipe is unsure how much of it applies given the custom warp shader, so I treat the outward flow and swirl as likely rather than certain. There is no translation (dx, dy are 0). The dot figure itself rotates slowly (0.3 rad/s). Trails fade with gain about 0.95 per frame, a half-life of about 13.6 frames or 0.6 s, with a floor of about 0.012 of full level, so streaks are short and soft rather than long. The calibrated estimate gives a mean flow of about 0.28 screen widths per second (interval 0.13 to 0.54) and a mean frame difference of about 0.05 (0.02 to 0.10): moderately active, smooth, liquid-like movement. The mood layer scores smoothness high (about 88) and tags it Smooth and Organic / Liquid.

Brightness and contrast. The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.28 (wide interval 0.09 to 0.52), so a mid-dark to medium picture. Black stays black through the composite, so dark gaps between cells and streaks should remain, and contrast between deep blue and pale cyan should be fairly high. The recipe's "steady state" brightness is only a lower bound. Because the band-pass feedback fills the canvas with texture, the picture is likely brighter than the flat-field blue implies. The estimate expects about 4 global brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 0.7 to 18), i.e. noticeable swells with the music but not a hard strobe. The strict source model records no eligible mood group.

Audio response. Wave dot colour: bass raises red, treble raises green, mid raises blue, each with a linear gain of 0.5 on a 0.5 base, with no clamp or threshold (continuous response). So the dots shift in tint and brightness with the spectrum. The wave alpha is authored at 1.396 (above 1, so fully opaque dots). The wave shape follows the left and right waveform, so the dot figure deforms with the sound all the time. The warp shader also reads treble (red), bass (green) and mid (blue) in its colour terms, with gain unknown. All these are continuous brightness and colour routes, not beat triggers. No bass-driven zoom or rotation is established. Whole-screen pulses and beat synchronisation are not established, though the assumed flash estimates (about 3.6 to 4 Hz, small delta 0.13 to 0.3) are labelled assumed, not supported. With loud, full-spectrum music the figure should look busier and brighter; in quiet passages the dots and growth seeds weaken and the feedback fades toward deep blue.

Uncertainty. Unknown: exact dot count and size, the true size of the central figure on screen, and how the stereo trace looks for real audio (a quiet or mono signal collapses the figure towards a line or point). Unresolved: the warp shader's colour gains, the actual sampled palette of the feedback, whether the border colours survive the composite, and how much of the 1.2 to 1.3 zoom is really applied. Not established: particles (the points are a waveform, not tracked particles), tunnel or fractal structure, symmetry, strobing. The mood and activity numbers are low-confidence, uncalibrated or source-calibrated estimates with wide intervals (activity about 0.71, bands Normal or Intense, interval 0.18 to 0.95), not measurements of this preset. Colour hue family and the cool, saturated look are the best supported parts; fine pattern shape and speed are the least certain.
