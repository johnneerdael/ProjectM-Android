Approximate look: a busy, organic, saturated picture of blobs, cells and splatter that grows and spreads over most of the screen. It looks like reaction-diffusion or embossed relief, shown through a tinted composite. Calibrated mean luminance is mid-low (about 0.28, interval 0.10 to 0.50), saturation is fairly high (about 0.52), and most of the screen is coloured (share about 0.7). The Cool facet is admitted (probability 0.63).

How it is built: the warp shader adds a fine copy of the fed-back image and subtracts a blurred coarse copy every frame. This sharpens small seeds into growing blobs, cells or splatter. At the same time the warp and composite shaders steer their own sampling by image values (self-advection). The composite also shows the image through its local slopes, so edges and texture look embossed or lit, while flat areas read as one level. The seeds are two groups of tiny triangles, about 310 and 280 copies at radius about 0.033, scattered around the screen with audio-driven positions and colours. One group is a textured copy of the previous frame, with a 0.63 rad texture angle, and both rotate slowly through a sinusoidal angle (periods about 18 to 21 s). Two small coloured point rows sit in the left corners, near (0.1, 0.9) and (0.1, 0.1). They hold blue, yellow, green and orange hues and are probably minor, since the growth rule spreads colour.

Colours: the composite multiplies by four corner shades whose placement is random on each preset load, so the exact hue layout changes per load. Black shows as a dark blue floor (about 0.12 in blue). Overall the likely palette is blue and green with pink, with no dominant hue. Treat it as a mixed, vivid set rather than one colour.

Motion: there is no global drift or rotation (zoom 1, zoomexp 1.001). The movement is internal, as blobs flow, divide and smear along image gradients. Calibrated flow is about 0.10 screen widths per second, gentle to moderate. Trails are short (colour halves in about 14 frames, roughly 0.5 s).

Audio: bass, mid and treble drive the seed triangles' colour, opacity and position, and also the red, green and blue gain of the warp. Most of these routes are continuous, but opacity and position are clamped or thresholded. The strength is unresolved.

Flashing: the no-strobe probability is only 0.18 and estimated brightness jumps are about 3 per 10 s (range 0.4 to 14). It likely flickers or pulses with the beat and is not strobe-free. Intensity is Normal by the calibrated estimate, though the source-only score reads higher. Uncertain: the real balance of seeds against growth, and the random tint.
