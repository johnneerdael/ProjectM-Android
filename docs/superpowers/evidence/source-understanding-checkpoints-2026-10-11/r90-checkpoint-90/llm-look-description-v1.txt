LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r90 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a single audio-deformed circle (built-in wave mode 0, thick line, not dots, not additive) centred on the screen, drawn with a ring radius of about 1.1 model units, so the ring is large and probably partly runs off the screen edges. Its shape wobbles with the right-channel waveform (sample radial gain 0.4) and it rotates slowly (nominal 0.2 rad/s). The circle is fed back through a warp every frame, so it leaves repeating, fading copies and swirling streaks rather than staying a thin line. The composite then adds a left-right and a top-bottom mirrored copy of the image, each at 50% opacity (echo zoom about 0.9996), so the final picture should read as a four-fold, kaleidoscope-like mirrored pattern of concentric, twisting rings. The recipe calls the symmetry an upper estimate: the zoom, per-step scale and the live drawing make it inexact, and the content is otherwise uncorrelated with its mirror.

Colour: the recipe does not resolve the palette. The wave colour is driven by per-frame equations: mid drives red, bass and mid drive green, and treble drives blue (mostly threshold or clamped responses). The wave is max-normalised, so when its largest channel is above 0.01 the line is scaled to a fully saturated colour; otherwise it is drawn near black. The result is a line that changes hue with the music, which the recipe marks as colour cycling, but no specific hue, hue count or warm/cool balance is established. The source-derived steady state is grayscale at a very low level (about 0.006 displayed), and the recipe says to treat that as a lower bound only. The calibrated display estimate puts mean saturation at about 0.63 (interval 0.36 to 0.90) and warm/cool near neutral (interval about -0.34 to +0.35). Treat these as calibrated guesses, not measurements. There are no random textures. I would expect a vivid, shifting line on a dark background, with no fixed named colour.

Motion and feedback: the warp has no net drift (dx = dy = 0). Motion comes from a radius-dependent swirl (rotation depends on rad and x), a radius-dependent zoom, and a tiny constant extra shrink (sx about 0.9999, sy about 0.99990, zoomexp 1.0001, warp 0.009). The zoom and rotation equations are audio-gated and their exact values are unresolved. Trails are moderate: stored decay is 0.98 per frame, giving a half-life of about 35 frames (about 1.2 s at 30 fps), and faint content stalls at stored level 24-25 out of 255 instead of fading to pure black. The calibrated estimates suggest slow to moderate motion, with flow around 0.14 screen widths per second (interval 0.045 to 0.31). The "mean frame difference" estimate is low at about 0.03. The recipe offers no typical visible speed.

Audio reaction: bass, mid and treble, including the smoothed variants, all feed the zoom and rotation through thresholded or clamped switches (bass, mid and treble thresholds compared against a 1.3/0.96-style schedule). Reactions are therefore stepwise, not smooth proportional pulses. In particular, bass and mid make the zoom and swirl jump when they exceed their running thresholds, which should give occasional pushes or twists on beats. The wave's own colour channels respond to the bands in the same way. No linear gains are given, so how strong any reaction looks on screen is unresolved. The recipe also states that the outer waveform data and history are unknown.

Brightness and uncertainty: the calibrated display estimate gives mean luminance of about 0.23 (wide interval 0.04 to 0.46), which means a moderately dark picture. Brightness jumps are rare (about 0.7 per 10 s, interval 0 to 5), and the composite gamma redraw brightens up to 1.49x before clamping. The estimated activity is about 0.28, with a plausible band of Chill or Normal, and a low-confidence approximate mood. Strict eligible bands are empty, and unknown features include palette saturation, hue count and mean luma. Nothing in the recipe is random per load. The unresolved or undefined items are the exact palette, the true zoom and rotation values (they depend on audio thresholds), and how much of the ring is clipped. The bitwise-AND operations in the zoom and rotation equations are flagged as unsupported by the analyzer. The overall look is an approximation: the motion and flashing values are assumed, not measured, and real frames will differ.
