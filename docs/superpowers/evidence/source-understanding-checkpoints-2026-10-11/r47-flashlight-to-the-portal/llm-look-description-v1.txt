LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r47 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a self-advecting, fluid-looking feedback field centred on the screen. A white circular waveform ring (audio-deformed circle, centre 0.5/0.5, radius about 0.5 model units, slowly rotating at about 0.2 rad/s, thin line, not additive) is drawn each frame over three groups of 32-sided polygon shapes (33, 7 and 33 instances) whose positions move with sin(time) formulas. Their exact colours and sizes are unresolved. Because zoom is applied about the centre, with a radius-dependent exponent, and a warp shader steers its own sampling from the image, the ring and polygons smear into flowing, wobbling, ripple-like structure rather than staying crisp. A thin black outer border (about 1% of the screen) is stamped every frame. No mirror symmetry is found.

Motion: no translation or rotation drift. Motion is a radial zoom of 0 to about 41% per step, driven by bass (and smoothed bass), so the picture breathes outward on bass hits and is calm in between. Periodic displacement wobble in both warp and composite stages adds rippling. Trails are long: red is kept almost whole, while green and blue halve in about 170 frames. Nine random values redraw at about 2.9 s and then every 12.6 s, so shapes and colours shift to a new random draw at those moments.

Colour: unresolved at the source level. The recipe's steady state points to a green-leaning cast (dim, with a wide spread). The composite maps flat canvas through a green-tinted ramp, with green strongest and red and blue lower. Bass, mid and treble all feed shape fill, perimeter and opacity colours, so hues likely vary with the music. The ring is white by authored base colour.

Brightness: the display estimate gives a mean luminance of about 0.34 (interval 0.15-0.57), moderate saturation of about 0.5, and a slightly warm-neutral balance (interval spans cool to warm). Global brightness jumps are estimated at about 13 per 10 s, with a wide interval of 4-51.

Audio reaction: bass drives zoom pulses and shape position and radius. Bass, mid and treble drive shape radius, colour and opacity. The ring follows the right waveform channel. The estimated activity is 0.83 (Intense, possibly Normal), which is a calibrated source estimate, not a measurement.

Uncertain: the real palette and dominance, how visible the polygons are, the exact density of the smear, whether the ring stays legible, and whether the brightness jumps are visible flashes.
