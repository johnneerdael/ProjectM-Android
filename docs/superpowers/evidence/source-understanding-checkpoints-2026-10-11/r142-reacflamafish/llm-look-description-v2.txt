Approximate look (source-only, uncertain): a dense, textured, feedback-driven picture that fills most of the screen rather than a few lines on black. The composite is a relief (emboss) stage, so edges and texture stand out like lit slopes while flat areas collapse to one dull level. That flat level is a dark purple-grey (about 0.04, 0.03, 0.05), but the textured parts should look much brighter. The calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.28 (interval 0.10-0.52), high saturation about 0.88, and a warm lean (0.46). The canvas hue is red (about 0.31, 0.02, 0.02). So I expect saturated red or warm relief texture over dark purple flats, not a dark picture. A cycling four-corner tint multiplies the composite. Its three channel periods are about 14.6, 19.6 and 16.2 s, and which hue sits where is random per load, so colour drifts slowly and differs between loads.

Drawn content is weak and small. The built-in two-trace waveform (mode 7, additive) has alpha 0.001, so it is almost invisible. The custom point waves have 512 points each. One (wave_1) is pink, alpha about 0.1, in a tiny box just left of and above centre (x 0.50-0.55, y 0.46-0.50). The other three have unresolved colour and position. These are seeds for the feedback, not a crisp structure.

Motion: every frame the image expands from the centre by 1.3% and rotates within +/-0.01 rad. The result is a zoom-tunnel or outward flow with slight twisting that changes direction. Estimated flow is about 0.14 screen widths per second (0.05-0.31), with moderate frame-to-frame change.

Trails differ by channel. Red is inverted each frame (gain -0.32 plus offset), so it has no stable trail and may flicker. Green is never faded and accumulates, so it may build up. Blue fades slowly (half-life about 34 frames). The overall trail look is uncertain.

Audio: smoothed bass and treble shift the composite colour channels (thresholded or clamped, strength unknown). Bass nudges the faint waveform position, and the waveform line follows the audio.

Flashing: uncertain. The calibrated estimate predicts many global brightness jumps (about 33 per 10 s, range 10-127) and an Intense activity lean. The red inversion and audio-driven tints are possible sources. Treat it as likely lively and possibly flickery, and not as safe for a no-strobe setting.
