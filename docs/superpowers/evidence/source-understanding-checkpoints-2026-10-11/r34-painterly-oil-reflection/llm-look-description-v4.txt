LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (r34 v4; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a circular audio waveform ring centred on the screen (built-in wave, mode 0, base radius about 0.5 model units, slowly rotating at about 0.2 rad/s, deformed by the right-channel waveform), with three families of 32-sided polygon shapes drawn over it (33, 7 and 33 instances, all moving). The picture is shown through a composite shader that samples the canvas in polar/radial coordinates (a tunnel-like, reciprocal-radius mapping) and is disturbed by noise textures, so expect a turbulent, shimmering, painterly tunnel rather than crisp geometry.

Feedback and motion: the warp is a radial zoom about the screen centre, from no change up to roughly 41% expansion per step, stronger toward the outer radius. Bass raises it. There is no rotation or drift. The warp keeps only about 30% of the previous frame, so trails are very short (about one frame); the look is carried mostly by freshly drawn shapes and the composite shader. A thin black outer border is redrawn each frame.

Colour: the source gives no fixed palette. The composite multiplies by cycling corner tints, with red, green and blue cycling over roughly 15, 20 and 16 seconds in a 0.67 to 1.0 range. Their placement is random per preset load, so I name no single tint. The settled canvas hue is grayscale, so colour comes from the shapes and the hue cycle. The composite maps any non-black canvas level to full white before tinting, so lit areas read bright and washed rather than dim.

Per-load variants: the composite branches on a random value drawn at load. In 60% of loads one variant applies, in 40% the other. I cannot say which looks different, so both are possible.

Brightness and activity: the calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.26 (wide range 0.06 to 0.49), moderate saturation about 0.35, neutral warm/cool, flow about 0.19 screen widths per second, and many brightness jumps (about 36 per 10 s, very uncertain). Activity is estimated Intense, though Chill or Normal remain possible.

Audio: bass drives the zoom and the shapes' size, position, opacity and colour. Mid and treble also drive shape size and colour. These are routes, not measured strength.

Uncertain: shape sizes, which composite branch dominates, the actual tint, how much of the screen the ring and shapes cover, and whether brightness jumps read as flashing.
