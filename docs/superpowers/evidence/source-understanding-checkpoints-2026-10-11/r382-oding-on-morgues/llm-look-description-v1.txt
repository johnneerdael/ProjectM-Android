Because the warp shader keeps only about 10 percent of the previous frame (gain about 0.106, halving in a third of a frame), nothing lingers, and each frame is mostly the freshly drawn vector field. I would expect a lattice of short strokes that bends and swirls with the warp field, not smooth smoky persistence.
