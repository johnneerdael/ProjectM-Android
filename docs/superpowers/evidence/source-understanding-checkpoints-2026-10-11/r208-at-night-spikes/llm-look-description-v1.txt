Approximate look: a cool, blue-led, four-way mirrored kaleidoscope with fast swirling motion, built around a central circle. Brightness is moderate to dim. Whether the composite brightens it is uncertain.

Colours: the calibrated estimate gives mean luminance about 0.34 (range 0.16 to 0.56), saturation about 0.5, and a cool lean. Likely hues are blue (about 0.52), then green (about 0.19), purple (about 0.11) and pink (about 0.11). The sources agree. The built-in circle has blue 0.98, with red and green varying with bass and treble. The 512-point custom wave is blue, purple and cyan at about half alpha. A 100-sided circular shape at the centre (radius about 0.54) has a centre colour that ranges from cyan to purple to blue. Its edge is black, and its opacity follows smoothed bass, mid and treble. A static steady-state estimate suggests a near-white grey display, but it is a lower bound that the calibrated estimate does not support.

Structures: a small thick audio-deformed ring in the middle (effective radius about 0.1), a larger soft-edged disc around it, and the point trace near the centre. Everything is repeated four ways. The feedback step flips the image left-right every frame, and the composite adds left-right and top-bottom mirrored copies at 50%. A black inner border covers about 7% of the screen.

Motion: strong and fast. Each feedback step contracts the image about 10% toward the centre while rotating it a full radian, with a strong warp. This gives a whirling, inward-spiralling kaleidoscope. Bass, mid and treble push the horizontal and vertical offsets, so the pattern jumps and sloshes with the music, up to about 324 px/s vertically. Estimated flow is about 0.16 screen widths/s. There is no net fade (decay 1.0), though the black border clears the centre.

Flashing: the no-strobe probability is 0.67, below 0.7, so it flickers and is not strobe-free. The estimate is about 1.3 brightness jumps per 10 s (0 to 7).

Uncertain: the real brightness, how busy the swirl is, and the precise shape sizes.
