LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r75 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This looks like a slow, smooth feedback tunnel, not a crisp drawn shape. The only drawn content is two mirrored custom point waves (wave_0 and wave_1, 512 points each per frame, closed-form positions). A built-in stereo XY dot trace also exists, but its authored alpha is 0.001, so it is effectively invisible. The previous frame is fed back every step with a uniform 1% expansion from the screen centre. A spatially varying rotation of up to +/-0.02 rad per step is applied on top, with 5 angular lobes that turn at about -0.2 rad/s (rot is roughly 0.02*sin(5*ang + time)). I expect a soft, swirling, five-lobed spiral-and-zoom smear in which the seeded points are pulled outward and twisted into curved streaks. The warp centre itself orbits (cx, cy = 0.5 +/- 0.25, period about 25 s), so the whole swirl slowly wanders rather than sitting fixed. Uncertainty: the recipe gives the point-cloud position bounds as huge (x about -1632..146, y about -79..7666 in source units, mirrored for wave_1), so I cannot tell how much of each wave lands on screen. The visible seed may be sparse, partly clipped, or mostly off-screen, and the picture may be dominated by whatever the feedback has smeared. Nothing here shows tiling, kaleidoscope mirroring (the mirror symmetry score is 0), particles, or a tunnel with a defined vanishing point. The "tunnel" feel is only the zoom plus rotation.

Colours. The seed points are purple (mean RGB about 0.5, 0.4, 0.8, mean saturation about 0.5, mean alpha about 0.48, with alpha varying along the wave through cosine terms). The recipe also lists the built-in wave as possibly orange, but at alpha 0.001 I treat that as negligible. Per the recipe the composite is a shader that is black for canvas levels at or below about 0.5 and a grey of about 0.72 to 0.85 for bright levels (flat_transfer; steady-state hue family "grayscale"). So only the brightest accumulated feedback shows through, and it tends to be neutral grey or white. Faint purple content may be crushed to black by that transfer, so I cannot say how much purple actually survives to the display. My best guess is a mostly dark scene with soft grey-white and lilac-tinged curved streaks. The display estimate is moderately cool and moderately saturated: warm/cool -0.23 (interval -0.56 to +0.12) and saturation 0.41 (0.13 to 0.68), which is wide and uncertain. The recipe says the palette is conditional on sampled feedback contents, so I do not claim a fixed palette.

Motion and feedback. The decay is 0.99 per frame, giving a trail half-life of about 2.5 s (74 to 80 steps), and stored levels below about 50/255 never fade further. Trails are therefore long and soft, with faint residue left behind, and the zoom plus rotation keep carrying old content outward and around. Translation (dx, dy) is zero. The motion is smooth and continuous with no discontinuities found. The recipe's p95 motion speed estimate is about 0.22 viewport units per second (interval 0.16 to 0.38), but the recipe marks it as partly assumed. The display-estimate mean flow is about 0.07 screen widths per second (0.003 to 0.19), so I expect a gentle, slow drift and swirl rather than a fast rush.

Audio. The recipe lists no audio bindings (audio_bindings is empty, and the bass response effect is 0). The only audio routes are weak. The wave positions and alpha read smoothed bass_att (wave_0) and mid_att (wave_1), and the stereo XY trace uses the left and right waveform, but it is nearly transparent. I therefore expect the picture to respond only mildly and slowly to music: the seed points' position and opacity breathe with the smoothed bass and mid levels. There should be no beat-synchronised flash, pulse or whole-screen change. The recipe gives no basis for a strong audio reaction, and the flash metrics are unknown (0 to 4 Hz interval marked unknown), so I state that as unresolved rather than as proof of calm.

Brightness and mood. The display estimate gives mean luminance about 0.43 (interval 0.24 to 0.66), so it is mid-brightness with real uncertainty and could read from fairly dark to fairly bright. Brightness jumps are about 0.2 per 10 s (0 to 3.4), and mean frame-to-frame change is small (0.019, up to 0.058). The activity estimate is low, about 0.10 (Chill, possibly Normal), and the approximate mood is Smooth and Hypnotic with low confidence. This is a calibrated estimate from source facts, not a measurement of this preset's frames.

Random or chaotic aspects. The recipe lists no per-load random branches (random_redraws is null and there are no per_load_random entries), so I do not expect a random palette or variant per load. Some unpredictability remains. The wave point positions use a persistent per-frame "advance" state, and the point positions are enormous in range. The slowly orbiting warp centre and the 5-lobed rotation also make the exact swirl pattern depend on the elapsed time since preset start. I expect the overall look to repeat from load to load, but the fine detail of the streaks will not be reproducible.
