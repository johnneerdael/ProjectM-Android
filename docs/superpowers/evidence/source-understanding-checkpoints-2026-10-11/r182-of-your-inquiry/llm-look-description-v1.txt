A warm, orange-to-yellow picture built from hundreds of small polygons that jump around with the music. The drawn elements are grey or white in the source, but the composite shader recolours the canvas: dark levels stay black, mid levels turn red then orange, bright levels go yellow and finally white (flat transfer: level 0.5 shows as roughly orange, 0.75 as pale yellow). Calibrated estimates agree: strongly saturated (about 0.70, range 0.42 to 0.97), strongly warm (about 0.67) and moderately bright (mean luminance about 0.31, range 0.12 to 0.54). Expect glowing orange and yellow shapes on a mostly dark red-black ground, with white-hot cores where content piles up. This tint is fixed over time, not cycling.

Structure: two groups of many-sided polygons are drawn repeatedly, one group of 14-sided shapes (512 copies) and one of 23-sided shapes (1024 copies, tilted about 0.44 rad). Their positions and sizes depend on audio, so they scatter into clouds of overlapping discs or ring-like clusters whose layout I cannot pin down from the recipe. A thin, very faint pair of parallel waveform traces runs horizontally through the centre (alpha only 0.001, so barely visible). A thin dark border frame, about 1% of the screen, is drawn black each frame.

Motion: a slow overall contraction toward the centre (about 0.05 percent per frame), plus an angular periodic warp that nudges the image sideways and up and down by up to about 1 percent of the screen per step, with beat-driven stretching on both axes. Trails fade quickly, with a half-life of about 1.1 seconds, so shapes leave short smears. The calibrated flow is moderate to fairly fast, about 0.2 screen widths per second. Possible mirrored or symmetric patterns from the angular warp, uncertain.

Audio: bass and treble shift the warp, scale it, move the shapes and change their radius continuously (small gains of about 0.01). Bass, mid and treble all switch shape opacity through thresholds, so shapes should pop in and out on beats.

Flashing: possible. Opacity switches on beats could produce brightness pulses, though estimated whole-screen brightness jumps are low (under one per ten seconds, wide uncertainty up to five). Not a certified strobe or no-strobe preset.
