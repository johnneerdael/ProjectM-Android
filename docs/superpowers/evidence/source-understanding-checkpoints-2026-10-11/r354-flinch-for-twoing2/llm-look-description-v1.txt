Expect a fluid, smeared, mostly magenta-pink picture on a dark background, with structure showing as bright edges rather than filled areas. The composite zeroes the green channel (green output is 0 for any input) and ties red and blue together, so the picture is built from magenta, pink and purple tones. The calibrated hue shares agree: pink about 0.37 (clear lead), then purple, blue, green and red. The green and red shares are probably estimator noise, so I trust the magenta cast over the minor hues. Flat canvas areas map to black at every level, so the background should be black, and detail turns into grey-magenta texture (mean about 0.22 on test noise). Calibrated mean brightness is moderate to dim (about 0.25, range 0.07 to 0.47), with a balanced-brightness probability near 0.70.

Structures: a thick built-in waveform line is anchored near the lower left (about x 0.1, y 0.9). Its mode, colour and alpha are driven by bass and mid and the mode is also redrawn at random, so its shape changes over time and I cannot say what form it takes. A 63 by 47 grid of motion vectors (2,961 short lines) covers the screen and is drawn into the feedback loop, so the warp field may show as a fine mesh of streaks. Thin borders of about 2% and 5% of the screen are blended around the edges, with unknown colour.

Motion is the main character. The warp is a self-advecting, gradient-following shader (fluid or reaction-diffusion style), with slow drift of about 14 to 58 px/s horizontally and up to 43 px/s vertically. Zoom, rotation and scale vary across the screen with position, and bass, mid and treble all route into them, so the flow lurches with the music. Trails are fairly short (gain about 0.8, half-life about 3 frames, plus a small floor), so it reads as smeared flowing lines, not long ribbons. Estimated flow is about 0.18 screen widths per second.

Flashing: the no-strobe probability is only about 0.08 and brightness jumps are estimated at about 3 per 10 s (range 0.5 to 15), so occasional pulses are likely. It is not strobe-free. Wave mode switches add abrupt changes, assumed at about 2 per second.

Uncertain: the wave line's look, and whether the vector mesh is visible or only feeds the flow.
