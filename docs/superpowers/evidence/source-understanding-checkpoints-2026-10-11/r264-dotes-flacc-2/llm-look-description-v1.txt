Likely look: a busy, high-energy, mid-bright picture, green-led and fairly muted or pastel in colour. Rings and discs pulse with the music over a growing blobby, splattered texture. It flickers noticeably.

Colour and brightness: the calibrated hue shares put green clearly first (about 0.33), then blue and pink (about 0.14 each) and yellow (0.10). Green leads by a wide margin. Saturation is modest (about 0.39, with a wide interval), and the pastel facet clears its threshold, so expect softened rather than neon colours. Estimated mean luminance is about 0.33 (interval 0.15 to 0.54). The facets lean balanced rather than dark or bright. The composite itself is a neutral grey passthrough, so colour comes from the drawn shapes and the warp. Both the warp and composite shaders take colour from bass, mid and treble, so the hue and tint shift with the music. A thin black outer border (about 1% of the screen) frames the picture.

Structure: three groups of 32-sided polygons (essentially circles) with 33, 7 and 33 instances each. These make many small to medium discs or rings scattered around, whose positions and sizes follow the music (positions via smoothed bass, mid and treble). One group copies the previous frame. A built-in audio-deformed circle is also set, but at an alpha of 0.001 it is almost invisible. The warp stage adds a blur-difference (band-pass) feedback, so small seeds grow into blobs, cells and splatter that spread across the canvas, plus self-advection that smears and swirls the picture. Expect most of the screen to be patterned rather than a few clean shapes on black.

Motion: fast and restless. The feedback zoom is driven by bass (gain 0.05, plus a smoothed bass term) with a radial exponent of about 1.47, so the image breathes and pulses in and out, by up to roughly 40% per step in the extreme. There is no rotation or translation. Trails are short (decay about 0.95, half-life about 0.6 s). Estimated flow is about 0.27 screen widths per second, and the intense facet is strong (0.81).

Audio: bass, mid and treble all drive shape colour, opacity and radius continuously, and bass pulses the zoom. This is a clearly music-reactive preset. Beat strength is unresolved.

Flashing: this one flickers. The no-strobe probability is only about 0.01, with an estimated 46 global brightness jumps per 10 s (range 15 to 170), which is strobe-like. It is not safe for flash-sensitive viewers.

Uncertain: the exact layout of the discs, how bright the splatter gets, and how much of the screen the green dominates.
