LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r108 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. A mostly dark, grayscale-leaning frame built from a few centred elements on a cleared-each-frame canvas. The biggest element is a nearly circular 100-sided polygon (shape_0) at the exact screen centre (0.5, 0.5) with source radius 0.8, no rotation and no centre movement (maximum centre speed 0). It is a soft disc: white at its centre with alpha 0.5 fading to mid-grey (0.5) with alpha 0.01 at the rim, so it reads as a dim, wide, radially fading glow rather than a hard-edged shape. It may be clipped by the screen edges (radius 0.8 in NDC is large) and its border is off (border alpha 0). Over it sits a custom point wave (wave_0): 512 white points at alpha 0.1, clustered in a tiny region right around the middle, roughly x and y 0.477 to 0.523, so a small faint speckled core about 5 percent of the screen across. The built-in two-trace waveform (mode 7, two parallel traces offset 0.25 from centre, additive) is authored with RGB 0,0,0, so additive black adds nothing; I expect no visible waveform lines from it, though that is a source reading, not confirmed. The warp stage is image-driven: it samples the previous image through a blur sampler and a random bundled texture (slot rand02, picked per load) to steer its own coordinates, plus a spatially varying rotation. That should smear or swirl whatever is drawn into soft, flowing, fluid-like structure around the centre. No mirror, tunnel, kaleidoscope or fractal construction was found.

Colour. Essentially neutral: white, grey and black. The recipe's steady-state canvas is grayscale at about 0.04 level (a lower bound), and the composite passes colour through unchanged (flat transfer is identity). The wave_0 points have a dynamic RGB formula that admits many hues, and the warp shader has audio-coupled colour terms, so some tinting could appear on the point core or in the smear, but the recipe does not say which colours are reached. The recipe's own palette fields say saturation 0 and no coloured support; the calibrated display estimate is more open, with mean saturation about 0.46 (interval 0.18 to 0.73) and warm/cool near neutral. I would describe it as mainly grey-white with possible faint, uncertain colour in the centre and flow.

Motion. Flat content does not persist: the warp's transfer on flat colours has gain 0, so there are no classic long trails (half-life about 1 frame). Only the image-driven advection carries structure from frame to frame, so any trails come from that rather than from simple decay. The disc itself is static in position and size apart from audio-coupled texture zoom and rotation. The point core moves only within its small box. Display estimate: mean flow about 0.13 screen widths per second (interval 0.04 to 0.28), mean frame difference about 0.04 (interval 0.008 to 0.084). That is gentle to moderate drifting and swirling around the middle, not fast travel.

Brightness and contrast. Dim to medium and low contrast. The display estimate gives mean luminance about 0.24 (interval 0.06 to 0.48), a wide range. The disc is a faint glow (alpha 0.5 at centre falling to nearly 0), the points are alpha 0.1, so there are no hard bright elements. Brightness jumps are estimated at about 1.4 per 10 s (interval 0 to 7.9). I read the overall picture as soft and smooth, with a lighter haze in the middle and darker surroundings.

Audio response. Bass and treble drive the shape's texture zoom and texture rotation, and the warp shader's red, green and blue colour terms, through thresholded or clamped routes. Gains are unresolved. Estimates of flash-type brightness steps are small (peak delta about 0.1 to 0.3, roughly 3 to 4 Hz upper range from audio gating), and bass-to-RGB peak effect is about 0.035 per element. The wave_0 point formulas also read bass, mid, treble and beat-timing state, so the core speckle likely shifts position and tint with beats. The built-in waveform follows left and right channels but is black, so it likely contributes nothing visible. Expect a modest, smooth pulsing and slight swirl change with the music, not strobing.

Uncertainty. The weakest points are: whether the black built-in waveform is truly invisible; the real colour of the point core and any tint in the smear; how far the random texture (rand02) and blur sampling shape the swirl pattern, which changes per load; how bright the centre disc actually looks after clipping and the advection feedback; and the real strength of every audio route. Mood estimate is approximately Normal activity (0.37, interval 0.01 to 0.84, so Chill or Intense are not ruled out); the strict source model lists no eligible mood group.
