LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r26 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):
Construction: a feedback-driven scene. Two custom line waveforms (wave_0, wave_1) are drawn into a canvas that is re-sampled every frame, so I expect line-like marks smeared into flowing, fluid-looking structure rather than a clean drawing (render.mechanisms; shape and thickness of the waves are not resolved).
Warp motion: uniform zoom 1.009 (the image expands about the centre by 0.9% per step) plus a slow rotation of -0.002*treb rad per step (at most -0.004), so content spreads outward and turns slowly; dx/dy are 0, so no sideways drift. The warp and composite shaders also self-advect (image steers its own sampling, offsets up to about 0.02 UV in the warp), adding swirly distortion.
Trails: the warp transfer keeps about 0.97 of the level per step (half-life about 21 frames, roughly 0.7 s at 30 fps), with a small positive offset, so marks fade into long soft trails and the canvas settles near 0.08 rather than pure black.
Composite: the flat-canvas transfer is strongly non-linear: 0.25 shows as 0.13 grey, 0.5 as 0.93, 0.75 and above as white. Faint content is darkened and mid-to-bright content is pushed to near-white, so the look is high contrast with bright cores over a darker field.
Colour: unresolved. Both waves have dynamic full-range RGB (any hue possible, none verified); the shader palettes are unknown. Bass (attenuated) modulates the red, green and blue composite terms through clamped/threshold routes, so overall colour and brightness likely pulse with bass, strength unknown.
Audio: treble drives the rotation continuously; no beat-switch site was found, which is not proof of absence.
Calibrated estimate (not a rendering): luminance about 0.25 (interval 0.06-0.49), saturation about 0.47, warm/cool near neutral but very uncertain, activity 0.54 (Normal, but Chill to Intense all possible), brightness jumps about 7 per 10 s (wide interval).
Unresolved: wave geometry and size, actual colours, how much of the screen is filled, visible speed, and whether the composite contrast produces flashes.
Expected: a soft, glowing, fluid swirl of smeared line trails that expands outward and slowly rotates, fairly high contrast with bright cores, colours unknown but bass-pulsing, moderate overall motion.
