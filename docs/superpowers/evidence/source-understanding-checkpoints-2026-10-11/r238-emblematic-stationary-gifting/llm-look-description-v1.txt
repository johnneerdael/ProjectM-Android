Likely a fairly dark screen with a swirling field of thick coloured dots, twisted around the centre, and noticeable pumping with the music. The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.21, but the interval is wide (0.03 to 0.43), so brightness is uncertain. Saturation is moderate (about 0.35) and under half the screen is coloured (about 0.47). The picture is mixed: green (0.19) and red (0.15) lead, but the gap is only about 0.04, so I would call them near-equals, with cyan, blue, pink and yellow in smaller shares. Parts of the image may look grey, because the composite itself adds no colour and only brightens the canvas.

Structures: the only drawn element is a built-in waveform in dot mode (a horizontal momentum trace). It is drawn as thick points centred on the screen, using the left and right audio channels. The line colour is max-normalised, so the brightest channel is scaled to full strength and the dots should look fully saturated. Red follows bass, green follows treble and blue follows mid (gain 0.4 each), so the dot hue shifts with the balance of bands. Its opacity follows bass, so the dots fade up on bass hits. Bass also nudges the wave position or shape with a small gain (0.1). There are no polygons or textures.

Motion: the feedback map is spatially varying. Zoom changes with radius and rotation is radius-dependent too, a twist where the centre and outskirts turn and zoom by different amounts. That should smear the dots into curved, spiral or tunnel-like streaks around the centre. Bass and treble feed the zoom through thresholded routes, so the zoom probably kicks in on loud moments. The map has no constant translation, centre stays at 0.5, 0.5. The warp shader is nonlinear: very dim content is erased (below about 0.01 retention is zero), mid-dim content is kept (up to about 0.9), and content around 0.5 is cut almost entirely. Trails therefore look uneven and posterised, not a smooth fade, and the canvas should not wash out to a bright haze. The calibrated flow is about 0.29 screen widths per second, which is moderately fast.

Audio and flashing: the no-strobe probability is only 0.39, so it flickers and should not be called strobe-free. Estimated brightness jumps are about 2 per 10 s, with a range from about 0 to 10. The intense facet is above its threshold and the activity estimate is Intense (0.82).

Uncertain: how bright the accumulated dots get, whether it reads as concentric rings or spokes, and the final colour mix.
