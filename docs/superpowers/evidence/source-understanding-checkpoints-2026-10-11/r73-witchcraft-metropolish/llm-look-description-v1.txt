LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r73 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This looks like a field of many tiny drawn marks sitting in a feedback canvas, not one large figure. The recipe lists four polygon-shape layers: 311 small triangles (radius about 0.015, centre black, outer colour magenta/purple but with zero outer alpha), another 311 triangles (radius 0.01, black, opaque, textured), 1024 very small 12-gons (radius about 0.003, alpha about 0.1) and 92 12-gons whose radius is not resolved. There is also a 512-point custom line wave whose red, green and blue programs are all 0, so its source colour is black or greyscale, and a built-in two-trace waveform (mode 7, additive) whose authored alpha is only 0.001. That built-in wave is probably almost invisible; the recipe flags the mode-specific alpha boost and audio history as unresolved. Where each mark lands is mostly an unresolved expression (the position curves are "unknown"), so I cannot say whether they form a ring, a grid or a scatter. I would expect a dusting or swarm of small dark and tinted specks that slowly rotate (the triangles' rotation is a sine with an 18 s period).

Colour and brightness. The palette is conditional and mostly unresolved. The feedback warp shader treats the channels differently. Red is mostly kept each frame (gain about 0.95, steady level about 0.85). Green and blue are inverted every frame (gain about -0.95, offset about 0.96), so those two channels flip between roughly complementary levels at frame rate and settle near 0.49. Dark canvas areas therefore drift towards cyan and bright red areas towards dark. That predicts a restless red versus cyan/teal flicker in the trails, though the recipe gives no sampled image to confirm it. The composite stage maps a flat black canvas to light grey (about 0.70) and every flat level of 0.25 or more to black. A flat canvas settles near mid-grey (about 0.56), which the composite shows as black. So the steady background may be black or dark with the bright content coming from the busy parts. Overall brightness is uncertain; I lean towards a dark-to-moderate picture with high contrast. The recipe also records a nominal brightness cycle of 0.0796 Hz (about 12.6 s), but whether it is visible as a flash is unverified.

Feedback and motion. Each frame the previous image is pulled outward by a radial zoom. Zoom is 1 at the centre and reaches about 1.29 near the edges when bass is around 1, so content streams outward like an expanding tunnel, faster at the edges, and bass pushes it harder. A tiny sine drift (about 0.0002 of the screen, roughly 3 px/s at most) is added in x and y, which is minor next to the zoom. There is no rotation and no stretch. A periodic ripple or wobble displacement is also listed in the warp shader (amplitude and spatial period not resolved). The recipe gives no usable decay or trail length. The warp transfer above is the only persistence information, and the channel-selective inversion means the usual fade-out trail picture does not apply.

Audio. Bass raises the zoom (continuous). The built-in wave's colour follows smoothed bass, treble and mid with a gain of 0.2, but it is nearly transparent. The positions of the 311 textured triangles depend on bass and treble through thresholded or clamped expressions, so those marks probably jump or scatter on louder hits. The warp shader's colours depend on bass, mid and treble through thresholds or clamps. Strength of any of these routes is not established, and a beat-synchronised pulse is not proven.

Random and chaotic parts. Ten shape/wave state variables (q21 to q29 and q31) are redrawn at random at about 3.1 s and then every 12.6 s. Any colours, positions or counts that depend on them change at those moments, so the look differs from load to load and shifts every 12.6 s. Together with the per-frame channel inversion and the unresolved wobble, the motion is probably busy and somewhat chaotic. The approximate mood layer is an assumed estimate. It suggests high activity and fast coherent transitions, but the strict source model records no eligible mood group and flashing is unverified.

Main unknowns: the actual arrangement of the marks, the wave_0 visibility, the real trail brightness, whether the green/blue inversion shows as strobing, and the final palette.
