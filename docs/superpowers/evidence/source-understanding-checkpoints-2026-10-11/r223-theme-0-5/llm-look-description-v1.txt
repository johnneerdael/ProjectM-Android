Approximate look (from static source facts only, nothing rendered): a fairly dark, cool blue-led picture of smeared, turbulent, fluid-looking structure that churns quickly and flickers hard with the music.

Colour: the calibrated estimate puts the display at roughly 85% blue hue (interval 0.68 to 1.0) with small shares of green, pink and red. The cool facet probability is about 0.93 and the warm one about 0.02. The composite shader produces blue from grey detail (mean RGB about 0.22, 0.32, 0.47, saturation about 0.48), so structure on the canvas is coloured blue. Mean luminance is about 0.21 (0.02 to 0.43), so mostly dark with brighter blue patches, and saturation is about 0.44. The warp is strongly channel-selective. Green is kept almost whole (gain 0.998, half-life of hundreds of frames), while red and blue are inverted each frame (gain about -0.71, offset +0.71). Red and blue therefore flip between bright and dark on alternate frames, an alternating magenta-versus-green shimmer in flat areas. The steady-state display colour is black, and how it combines with the blue composite is uncertain.

Structures: one large 100-sided shape (circle) whose position, radius, colour and opacity are all driven by bass, mid and treble. Its size and location are not fixed in the recipe. A built-in circular waveform at the centre has alpha 0.001 and black colour, so it is essentially invisible. A custom wave layer exists, but its details were not exported (uncertain). Image-driven and noise-driven advection in both the warp and the composite should give a turbulent, smeared, liquid look rather than crisp outlines.

Motion: the mesh warp has an extreme radial zoom exponent (30000) and a per-step stretch of 0.99 (a slight outward pull). The zoom itself is audio-driven, and no rotation or constant drift was found. Calibrated flow is about 0.19 screen widths per second with high frame-to-frame change (0.08), so it is lively. Noise displacement of up to about 2% of the screen per step adds shimmer.

Audio: bass, mid and treble all feed zoom and deformation (continuous for deformation, thresholded for zoom). They also push the shape's position, radius and opacity, and the warp colour. Reaction should be strong and visible but is not quantified.

Flashing: no-strobe probability is only 0.005, with an estimated 31 brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 10 to 114). This is strobe-like flicker, and it is not safe for flash-sensitive viewers. The cause is the frame-by-frame red and blue inversion together with audio-driven opacity.
