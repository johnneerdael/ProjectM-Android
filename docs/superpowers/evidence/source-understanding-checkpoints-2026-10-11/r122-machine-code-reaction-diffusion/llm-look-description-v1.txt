LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r122 v1, core recipe view; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture
After about 5 seconds I expect a feedback-smeared field rather than a clean line drawing. The only drawn element is one thick, solid (not dotted) built-in audio waveform line (mode 4, a "horizontal momentum trace" driven by the left and right waveform), anchored at x=0.5, y=0.0. It is drawn with alpha 1.577 (so effectively opaque) and its colour is max-normalised: any channel set is scaled so the largest becomes 1, giving a fully saturated-or-bright line unless the authored colour is almost black (largest channel <= 0.01). The line's own colour curves are unknown in the recipe, so its hue is not established. Each frame the previous image is passed through a warp shader that averages 4 neighbouring samples at an offset of about 0.1, then the result is re-drawn on top. That blur accumulates into soft haze and smeared streaks, so the line should leave blurred, widening trails that fill a good part of the screen rather than stay a thin wire. There is no tiling, mirror symmetry (symmetry score 0), tunnel, kaleidoscope or fractal construction, and no random textures. A thin outer border (about 1% of the screen) is blended at alpha 0.6 with a very dark red [0.01, 0, 0], which is close to a faint dark frame and is unlikely to be noticed.

Colour
The recipe does not resolve the final palette: the warp and composite shader palettes, and the line colour channels, are "unresolved". What it does give is the warp's per-channel behaviour on flat content, and that is strongly channel-selective: red has gain -0.14 with offset +0.14 (it is inverted and pulled to about 0.12-0.14, steady level about 0.27 of full, halving in about 1 frame, so red does not build up); green has gain 0.987 and is kept almost whole (steady level 1.0, no fade by the warp); blue has gain 0.976 (linear, steady level about 0.76). So over time the feedback canvas favours green, with moderate blue and little red, which points to a green to cyan/teal canvas (canvas steady-state about R 0.14, G 0.99, B 0.51). However the composite's flat transfer is an identity grey ramp (no tint), and the steady-state block labels the displayed hue family as grayscale at about 0.83 level. These two hints disagree, so I cannot say whether the screen reads as teal-green or as mostly neutral grey/white. The calibrated display estimate (not a rendering) leans neutral: mean saturation about 0.08 (10th-90th percentile 0 to 0.34) and warm/cool about 0.03 (-0.31 to +0.38), i.e. low colour, with a possible faint green-cyan cast. The line itself could be a more saturated colour on top of this.

Motion
Constant mesh controls: zoom 1, zoomexp 1, rotation 0, no horizontal drift, vertical translation dy = -0.006 per feedback step. By the recipe's convention a negative dy moves content up the screen, about 2.88 px per step or about 86 px/s (at the recipe's reference size), with a mesh sampling ceiling of about 0.32 viewport units per second. A small constant warp amount (0.01) adds a slight procedural wobble. So expect trails and haze drifting steadily upward, with no spin, zoom or tunnel motion. The drawn line itself changes shape continuously with the waveform. Calibrated estimate: mean flow about 0.13 screen widths per second (0.04 to 0.29) and mean frame-to-frame difference about 0.074 (0.036 to 0.13), i.e. gentle to moderate movement, mostly from the line and the upward smear. The recipe's smoothness score is 91 and the intensity score about 13 out of 100 (10 to 20).

Brightness and contrast
Source facts alone give a lower bound only. The calibrated estimate puts mean luminance at about 0.58 (wide interval 0.39 to 0.82), so a mid to fairly bright picture, not a dark one. The green channel never fades through the warp, so green content can pile up toward saturation, while red is held low. Contrast is likely moderate: bright line and streaks over a blurred, hazy, mid-tone background. This is uncertain because the steady-state hint is bright grey (0.83) while the canvas hint is green with mid blue.

Audio response
The only audio route is the waveform shape of the line (continuous, unsmoothed, left and right channels). There are no bass, mid or treble bindings, no thresholds, no audio-driven blur radius and no audio-driven colour. The recipe finds no audio switch site, so no beat-triggered pulses are expected. Expect the line to wiggle with the sound's waveform and the smear to follow, but the overall brightness and colour should not pump with the beat. The calibrated estimate gives 0 global brightness jumps per 10 seconds (upper end of the interval about 2.5).

Flashing and strobing
No source flash mechanism is found: coherent flash transitions 0 Hz, no discontinuities, no brightness jump route (these are source-absence statements, not proofs). One caveat is the red channel, which inverts each frame (gain -0.14); because the gain magnitude is small and it converges quickly, I expect at most a very faint frame-to-frame red shimmer on flat areas, not a strobe. A large, well-saturated line that flips between full colour and black is possible only if the line colour sits near the 0.01 normalisation threshold, which the recipe does not show. I do not expect strobing.

Uncertainty
- Line colour, composite palette and the shader colour mixing are unresolved; the hue could be green/teal, neutral grey, or something else.
- Brightness has a wide interval (0.39 to 0.82), and the steady-state and canvas hints conflict.
- The warp is a custom shader with a non-linear transfer, so the trail length is not a simple decay: green persists, red does not, and the exact persistence of line streaks is unknown.
- How much of the screen the blurred haze fills is conditional on the line being drawn and on the sampled content.
- Motion figures come from nominal source rates; real visible speed depends on the content.
- The strict source model records no eligible mood group; the approximate estimate is Chill (high stated confidence, but based on assumed features), with the calibrated activity percentile about 0.33 (0.05 to 0.86, so Chill or Normal and possibly Intense). My overall guess is a calm to gentle, smooth, upward-drifting hazy waveform picture with low colour variety.
