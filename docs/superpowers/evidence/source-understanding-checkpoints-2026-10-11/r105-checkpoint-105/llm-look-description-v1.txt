LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r105 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. A dark screen carrying a red-and-pink dotted cross at the centre, smeared by feedback into swirling, centre-pulling streaks. The drawn content is four 512-point custom waves (mechanism "point_cloud_primitive"/"custom_wave_primitive", independent particles not proven). Two of them (wave_0, wave_2) lie in a thin horizontal band across the middle: x about 0.18 to 0.82, y only 0.49 to 0.52, so a roughly horizontal dotted bar through the screen centre, about 64% of the width. The other two (wave_1, wave_3) lie in a thin vertical band: x about 0.48 to 0.52, y 0.12 to 0.88, a vertical dotted bar about 76% of the height. Together they read as a plus or cross, with sample positions that move with time (closed-form in sample and time, so the bars wobble or sweep rather than sit still). A built-in "stereo_xy_trace" dot wave is declared at the centre, but its alpha is about 0.001, so it should contribute essentially nothing visible. The feedback warp then pulls everything inward: the zoom is spatially varying, from about 0.835 to 0.97 (always below 1), so each frame the previous image is resampled so that content contracts toward the centre by roughly 3 to 16 percent per step, in angular lobes (zoom depends on ang and rad). A radius-dependent rotation (swirl) is added on top. With a decay of 0.997 the cross is therefore dragged into inward spiralling or tunnel-like streaks and copies of the dots around the centre, not left as a clean cross. The composite also adds a left-right mirrored echo at 50% opacity (echo zoom 0.9966), so the picture is close to left-right symmetric (the recipe gives a symmetry upper estimate of 1.0, inexact because of zoom and later drawing). A thin frame sits at the edges: an outer border covering 3% of the screen in dark blue (RGB 0, 0, 0.2) and an inner border covering 1% in pure red (RGB 1, 0, 0), both fully opaque, each redrawn every frame so nothing persists there. So expect a dark navy rim with a thin bright red line just inside it.

Colour. Mostly red to pink on a near-black background. wave_0, wave_1 and wave_2 are about 91% red-family dots (mean RGB roughly 0.50, 0.16, 0.13 for wave_0; wave_2 slightly more magenta at 0.50, 0.16, 0.18; mean saturation about 0.68 to 0.78), with about 9% grayscale/whitish dots. wave_3, the vertical one, is about 90% pink (mean RGB roughly 0.41, 0.01, 0.13, saturation about 0.97, a deep crimson-magenta) with about 10% grayscale. The recipe says wave colours are dynamic and "admit broad hue possibilities", so the reds could drift or shift in hue with time or music; I only trust the red/pink lean because the source-derived steady state is pink ("hue_family": "pink", canvas RGB about 0.034, 0.021, 0.027, which is very dark). Border colours are exact: dark blue outer, pure red inner. The feedback canvas colour itself is "palette unresolved" for the mesh warp, so the exact tint of the smeared trails is not established. Overall saturation is estimated at about 0.69 (interval 0.41 to 0.96) and the scene leans warm (warm/cool estimate +0.30, interval about -0.04 to +0.65), consistent with reds and pinks plus a small amount of blue only at the rim.

Motion. The warp has no translation (dx and dy are exactly 0), so nothing slides sideways. Motion is inward contraction (zoom about 0.835 to 0.97 per frame, lobed by angle) plus swirl from the radius-dependent rotation, so streaks spiral toward the centre. The dot bars move on their own through time-dependent sample positions, with the horizontal pair and the vertical pair oscillating at different phases. The calibrated estimate gives a modest mean frame-to-frame difference of 0.031 (interval 0.002 to 0.074) and a mean flow of about 0.14 screen widths per second (interval 0.05 to 0.31), i.e. a flowing but not frantic picture. A nominal speed estimate near 0.36 viewport-widths per second is mostly assumed rather than measured.

Brightness and contrast. Mostly dark with bright red and pink dots and a bright red inner border line, so contrast is high locally but the average level is low to moderate. Estimated mean luminance is 0.26 (wide interval 0.08 to 0.50), so it could look anywhere from quite dim to mid-bright depending on how much the feedback accumulates. Decay 0.997 with a contracting warp means trails persist for a long time (history operator half-life ceiling about 230 evaluations) but the heavy zoom keeps pulling them into the centre, so the middle probably builds up denser and brighter than the edges. The borders are redrawn every frame so they stay crisp. Flashing risk appears low: estimated global brightness jumps about 0.28 per 10 seconds (interval 0 to 3.7), and no coherent flash transitions were found at the source level.

Audio response. The only established audio links are attenuated bass (bass_att) into the zoom term and into the rotation term, so bass should change how hard the picture contracts and how fast the swirl turns. The recipe gives no linear gain, so the strength is unresolved. wave_1 holds bass at 1.0 in its colour/position evaluation, suggesting the vertical bar may react to bass, but the recipe does not establish this. The stereo_xy_trace builtin wave reads left and right waveform, but it is nearly transparent, so waveform shape is probably not what viewers see. The custom wave dots are closed-form in sample and time rather than being driven by the audio waveform, so I expect the cross shape itself to keep moving without much waveform detail. Expect a smooth, slightly bass-modulated tunnel pulse, not sharp beat flashes (approximate mood: Chill, possibly Normal; smooth, warm, symmetric, hypnotic; confidence medium; activity estimate 0.30).

Uncertainty. The recipe does not say how the dots are rendered (point size, whether each point is a single pixel or larger), so how dense or bold the cross looks is a guess. The exact hue of the trails, the final accumulated brightness, how bright the centre gets, the rotation direction and angular lobe count of the zoom pattern, and the exact motion of the dot bars (which are time-dependent but not described as a simple sweep) are not established. Whether wave_1 and wave_3 share the same vertical line or oscillate apart is unknown. The wave colours could change hue over time. The mirror symmetry is an upper estimate. A displayed brightness anywhere from dim to mid-bright is consistent with the recipe. The motion speed numbers are partly assumed. Nothing here comes from a rendered frame.
