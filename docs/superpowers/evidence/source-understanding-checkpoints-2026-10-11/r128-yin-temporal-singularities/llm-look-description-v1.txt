LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r128 v1, core recipe view; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: after about 5 seconds I expect a dense, smeared, flowing field rather than clean shapes. The only drawn element is a built-in waveform in mode 7 (two parallel audio traces, wave_x 0.5, wave_y 1.0) drawn as dots, not lines (dots true, thick false), blended additively at alpha 0.998. Those dotted traces are fed into a feedback loop. The warp is a custom shader that steers its own sampling coordinates from the image (self-advection), plus a per-pixel zoom that varies across the screen with x and y (zoomexp is about 0.99998, so there is almost no extra radial exponent, rotation is 0, warp is 0, centre is 0.5/0.5). Together these should pull the dots into streaks, swirls and liquid-looking structures that flow and smear, with a "tunnel or expansion" feel from the zoom. I do not see mirroring or symmetry (symmetry score 0, fold 1), no tiling and no particle system. The picture should look organic and fluid, not geometric.

Trails: the feedback is very persistent. The warp on flat colours keeps about 0.9989 of the level per frame (halving about 626 frames, roughly 20 seconds at 30 fps), with a tiny positive offset (about 1e-5). Marks drawn in the first seconds therefore stay and accumulate, so by 5 seconds the screen should be well filled with long-lived smeared trails instead of a few bright dots on black.

Colour: the recipe leaves the final palette unresolved (the warp RGB path depends on the image contents). Known facts: the wave red is fixed at 0.4, alpha 0.998, and green and blue are driven by bass, so the dot colour shifts with the bass between reddish and greener or bluer tints. The steady-state estimate leans slightly green (display_local_rgb about 0.046, 0.060, 0.052, which is a very dark, nearly neutral green-grey). The composite applies a legacy hue tint at amount 1.0 whose phase is random per load, so which hue appears where is not fixed. The calibrated display estimate gives mean saturation about 0.44 (10th to 90th percentile 0.17 to 0.71) and warm/cool about 0 (neutral, wide interval). I would say moderately coloured, mixed hues, no dominant warm or cool cast, with the exact tint random.

Brightness and contrast: the composite gamma redraw can brighten the image up to 4.99x before clamping, so the build-up of additive dots can push the image quite bright. The calibrated estimate for mean luminance is about 0.40 (interval 0.21 to 0.64). The steady-state lower bound (very dark) and this estimate disagree, so brightness is uncertain: I expect mid-level, with bright streak cores against darker gaps. Borders: outer border at alpha 0.1 and an inner border of black at alpha 0.5 every frame, which darkens or frames the edges a little.

Motion: continuous and flowing. The estimated mean flow is about 1.2 screen widths per second (interval 0.7 to 2.1), and mean absolute frame difference about 0.14 (0.09 to 0.22), so this is not a still picture. The zoom, translation and wave position are bass-, mid- and treble-driven, so the flow direction and zoom wander with the music. Mesh-field speed in the approximate mood layer is only about 0.2 viewport per second (assumed, interval 0 to 1), so the two estimates disagree on speed; I lean toward steady medium-fast drifting and swirling rather than violent motion. Acceleration and jerk numbers are assumed, not source-proven.

Audio response: bass drives the zoom, x and y translation of the warp and the wave's position offset (wave_mystery) and its green and blue. Attenuated mid and treble (smoothed) also push zoom and translation. The waveform traces follow the left and right waveform continuously. All routes are threshold or clamp limited and their linear gains are unknown, so I cannot say how strong the response is. Expect the picture to breathe, drift and shift tint with the beat and loudness rather than cut abruptly.

Flashing and strobing: no audio switch site was found and the discontinuity rate is 0 (absence is not proof). The mood layer assumes coherent transitions of about 0.8 Hz (0.8 to 4 Hz) with a small peak delta (0.03, up to 0.3), and brightness jumps about 0.015 (up to 0.3), all assumed. The calibrated display estimate predicts about 21 global brightness jumps per 10 seconds (interval 6 to 81), which is a higher-than-expected figure. Net: I do not expect a hard strobe or full-screen flash, but bass-linked colour and brightness pulsing with a few rhythmic brightness steps per second is plausible, and the gamma boost could make bass-coloured pulses read stronger. Treat it as probably not strobing, with real uncertainty.

Uncertainty and mood: the strict source model records no eligible activity band (confidence low; Chill, Normal and Intense all remain possible). The calibrated display estimate puts activity at about 0.98 (Intense), while the editable source mood layer gives intensity about 12 out of 100 and smoothness about 93, so those two disagree strongly. My own reading is medium to high activity because of the constant flow and persistent bright accumulation, with a smooth, organic, liquid character and some psychedelic quality (psychedelic score about 40). Biggest unknowns: the final colours, the actual brightness level, how much the dots accumulate by 5 seconds, the warp strength (zoom and translation values are unknown), and whether the bass-driven pulses become visible flashes.
