LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r84 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This looks like a busy, organic, fluid-like abstract field with a live audio waveform drawn over it. A built-in two-channel "parallel traces" waveform (mode 7, additive, not dots, not thick, centred at 0.5,0.5 with a 0.25 normal offset between the two traces) is the clearest drawn element. Over it sit four groups of polygon shapes: a 100-sided shape (essentially a circle, 7 instances), a 23-sided shape with 1024 instances, a 32-sided shape with 33 instances, and tiny triangles (radius about 0.01, 311 instances). The instance counts are configured draw counts, not particles, and how much of each is visible is not established. The composite stage works from the image's own gradients and uses the blurred image to steer its sampling (gradient-driven and self-advection mechanisms). I expect smeared, flowing, reaction-diffusion-like texture over most of the screen, not a dark background with a few clean shapes. No mirroring or kaleidoscope symmetry is recognised (symmetry score 0). No random textures are used.

Motion and feedback. The warp expands the picture outward from the screen centre, with a nominal 0.146% to 4.48% per step, stronger with distance from the centre (zoom 1.01, zoomexp 0.227). There is also a radius-dependent swirl whose rotation is an unresolved expression (an unsupported bitwise-and operation), so the twist direction and strength are unknown. Translation is zero. Shapes are flagged as moving, but the recipe gives no centre paths (path kind unknown). The warp is a custom shader, so the plain decay value is null. The intent block's "half-life 0.033 s, decay 0.0" for trails is therefore not reliable. Trail persistence is unresolved, so I won't claim long trails or short ones. The bounds on motion speed are small (about 0.014 viewport units per second for the shader lookup). The calibrated estimate nonetheless puts overall flow at about 0.23 screen widths per second (interval 0.10 to 0.47) and frame-to-frame change at about 0.17 (0.11 to 0.25). I read it as constantly churning, with an outward flow and a swirl.

Colour. The palette is mostly unresolved. The wave's colour is dynamic and the source admits broad hue possibilities. Its raw red, green and blue values swing from about -0.35 to 1.65 (clamped on screen), driven by bass, mid and treble respectively, and its alpha is a constant 0.001, so how strongly the line shows is uncertain. Shape 0 and shape 3 have possible greyscale or near-black fills. Shape 2's colours and opacity follow all three audio bands. The composite multiplies by cycling corner tints. The red, green and blue cycles run about 14.6 s, 19.6 s and 16.2 s, each ranging from 0.67 to 1.0. Which hue appears where is random per preset load, so I won't name one fixed tint. The composite's response to flat greys is neutral: black stays black, a 0.25 level shows at about 0.34 and 0.5 at about 0.75, and the top is clipped to white. That makes the display response bright-leaning. The calibrated estimate gives mean saturation of about 0.49 (0.22 to 0.76) and warm/cool close to neutral (-0.01, interval -0.35 to +0.34), so I can't say whether it is warm or cool.

Audio. Bass, mid and treble each drive the wave's red, green and blue respectively. They also drive the radii, positions and opacities of the shapes, and the colours and opacities of shape 2, mostly as continuous responses. Several position, opacity and warp-colour routes are thresholded or clamped, and shape 2's position follows smoothed bass. The line's shape follows the left and right waveform. The recipe gives no linear gains except about 0.013 on shape 1's radius, so the strength of each response is unknown. The brightness-cycle ceiling is a nominal 0.08 Hz, but flashing is unverified. The calibrated brightness-jump estimate is high at about 36 per 10 s, and its interval is very wide (11 to 137).

Brightness and overall feel. Mean luminance is estimated at about 0.37 (0.19 to 0.61), a mid brightness with wide uncertainty. The calibrated activity estimate is 0.95 (Intense; the interval keeps Normal possible). Together with the high estimated brightness-jump rate, that suggests an energetic, possibly flickery, psychedelic and geometric look. This is an estimate, not a rendered measurement, and the strict source model assigns no eligible mood.

Random, chaotic or undefined. Ten values (q21 to q29 and q31) are redrawn at random about 3.1 s after load and then every 12.6 s (3.1, 15.7, 28.3, 40.8, 53.4 s ...), so any colours or shapes that read them re-roll at those moments. The composite hue placement is also random per load. The unresolved warp rotation and the unresolved warp and composite palettes mean the exact appearance, the trail length and which layers dominate are uncertain. The recipe says it is only an approximate description, not pixel-exact.
