LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r119 v1, core recipe view; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. After about 5 seconds this should be a dense, busy, fluid-looking field rather than a clean drawing. Four layers of small polygon shapes (shape_0: 16-sided, 5 instances; shape_1: 32-sided, 37 instances with a fixed radius of about 0.02, so many small discs; shape_2: 32-sided, 33 instances; shape_3: 32-sided, 7 instances) are stamped every frame. Their positions, and for shapes 2 and 3 their radius and colour, are driven by the audio and are unresolved, so I cannot say where they sit. They feed a feedback canvas that is smeared by a custom warp shader. That shader advects the picture along its own image gradients (self-advection, a reaction-diffusion or fluid look), takes a 4-tap blur at offset 1, and wobbles slightly. The warp can displace sampling by up to about 0.2 of the screen in each axis (offset range -0.2 to 0.2 in UV). The result should read as soft, flowing, organic smears and blobs. Dots of the small shapes are dragged into streaks and haze. A built-in horizontal waveform line (mode 4, centred at 0.5,0.5, additive) is present, but its alpha is only 0.002, so it is close to invisible by itself. The composite shader adds noise-driven displacement (turbulent shimmer) and a polar, reciprocal-radius sampling that has a tunnel-like candidate. The recipe says a tunnel look is not guaranteed, so I expect at most a mild radial or tunnel feel towards the centre, not a clear tunnel. Mirroring or kaleidoscope structure is not found (symmetry score 0, with only weak symmetry from the stretch controls).

Motion. The only constant global motion is a very slow feedback rotation of 0.003 rad per feedback step, with no constant drift (dx = dy = 0). The overall zoom is audio-driven and unresolved (bass, mid and treble all feed zoom, with clamps or thresholds). The flowing impression comes mostly from the warp shader's self-advection and from the shapes' audio-moved positions. The calibrated estimate gives a mean flow of about 0.32 screen widths per second (10th-90th percentile 0.15 to 0.61) and a mean frame-to-frame difference of 0.093 (0.05 to 0.16). That is lively, continuous swirling, not stillness. The source-side motion speed estimate is about 1.5 viewport lengths per second at the 95th percentile, partly assumed. Trails are short. The warp keeps about 84% of the previous frame per step (gain 0.84 per channel, offset about 0.01), so content halves in about 5 frames, roughly 0.17 s at 30 fps, and flat content settles near 5% of its full level. Features therefore leave short, quickly fading smears, not long persistent trails.

Colour. The colours are not fixed by the recipe. The shapes' palettes are unresolved and depend on audio, because bass, mid and treble all route to the red, green and blue and perimeter colour of shapes 2 and 3. The one fixed colour is the faint built-in wave, yellow-olive (0.6, 0.5, 0.0), which barely contributes. The source steady-state hue family is cyan (canvas about 0.15, 0.17, 0.17 and display about 0.32, 0.35, 0.35), so the background haze leans cool cyan-teal. The approximate-mood estimate leans warm (warm/cool 0.64 source-estimate, but the calibrated warm/cool is -0.20, interval -0.54 to +0.15, slightly cool). I therefore expect a mixed palette. Cool cyan-grey haze is the base, with audio-driven coloured shapes and streaks over it, which could be warm. Saturation is moderate, about 0.36 calibrated (0.09 to 0.63), with a source-side estimate of 0.74 and about 3.5 effective hue bins. Expect several hues at once, not monochrome, but not neon either. The flat colour of the canvas is nearly neutral grey, so the tint comes from the content. The composite does not shift hue over time (time_varying false). No per-load random draw or random texture is listed.

Brightness and contrast. The composite brightens strongly. A flat canvas level of 0.25 is shown at about 0.51, 0.5 at 0.81, and 0.75 at 0.93, all neutral grey. Dim content is therefore lifted a lot, which flattens contrast at the bright end and keeps the picture from looking dark. The calibrated mean luminance is about 0.42 (0.24 to 0.66), so a mid-bright picture with considerable uncertainty. Contrast should come from the dark gaps between shapes and smears and the bright streaks. There is no heavy black border (the outer border covers 0% of the screen), and a mild darken-centre diamond dims the middle by up to 3/32 per frame.

Audio response. This is heavily audio-linked. All three bands (bass, mid, treble), plus attenuated bass, are wired to zoom, shape positions, opacity, outline opacity, radius and colour. Almost all links are marked thresholded or clamped and none has a known gain, so the visible strength is unknown. Expect the shapes to jump, shift and change brightness or colour with the music, and the whole picture to pulse or zoom with the beat. The treble and mid links to shape radius and colour suggest texture and colour changes on hi-hats and vocals, and the bass links suggest position and opacity pulses on kicks.

Flashing and strobing. This is the main risk. The calibrated estimate puts global brightness jumps at about 43 per 10 seconds (interval 14 to 162), meaning several brightness pops per second on a beat-heavy track. The source-side assumed flash figures are about 4 coherent transitions per second at a delta of around 0.3, but those are assumptions, not measurements. The recipe finds no hard audio switch site, so the changes should be gradual flares and pulses from opacity and colour modulation, not hard on/off blackouts. Even so, I would not call it safe for people sensitive to flicker, and it should be treated as flash-capable on strong beats. The calibrated activity percentile is 0.94 (0.63 to 1.0), band Intense.

Uncertainty. The positions, radii, colours and gains of the shapes are all unresolved. The actual palette depends on the audio and the sampled image content. The size of the tunnel effect and of the noise shimmer is unknown. The balance between warm and cool is not settled, since the source estimate and the calibrated estimate disagree. The brightness numbers are estimates fitted to other presets, not measurements of this one. Overall I am confident in the general character (a busy, intense, organic, flowing, audio-reactive picture with short trails and mid-to-bright exposure) and much less sure about the exact colours and the strength of the pulses.
