LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r91 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. This is a feedback-driven scene built from four large four-sided polygons (radius about 0.89 NDC, so each is huge and they overlap heavily), four custom 512-point waveform/point layers (wave_0 to wave_3, positions are closed-form functions of time, not tracked particles), plus the built-in wave drawn as dots (mode 2, a stereo X/Y trace centred at 0.5, 0.5). The polygons sit at the four corners of a 2x2 grid (x about 0.264 and 0.737, y about 0.81 and 0.178) and do not move. Their authored base colour is white with full alpha and zero border alpha, with a texture zoom of 1.08, but the recipe does not confirm which texture is bound or how much of it shows. The result is probably a bright, blocky, point-dotted image: dot clouds and big flat or textured quads smeared by feedback. Which shapes or dots dominate is not established. The composite also adds a left-right mirrored echo (alpha 0.5, zoom 0.9966), so the picture is roughly mirror-symmetric. The recipe calls that symmetry score 1.0 an upper estimate, since drawing and zoom make it inexact.

Colour. The recipe gives only partial colour information. The built-in wave dots are authored orange-brown (0.5, 0.4, 0.3) at alpha 0.001, so they are nearly invisible on their own. wave_2 has authored base colour orange (1.0, 0.6, 0.1) and wave_0 has a green-cyan (0.1, 1.0, 0.7), but their per-point colours are time-dependent sine programs. Their reached hues are unverified. The shapes' perimeter colours are driven by bass, mid and treble attenuated bands, with unknown gain, so their final colours are unknown. The recipe's steady-state palette is grayscale, a lower bound from source. It says the canvas settles toward white/grey, so the overall impression is likely light, whitish or washed with coloured dots and tints rather than a saturated hue field. Do not read this as a confirmed dominant colour. The recipe's approximate palette hint (saturation about 0.4, warm/cool leaning slightly warm, hue cycling 0) is a low-confidence estimate. The calibrated display estimate has mean luminance about 0.19 with a very wide interval (0.004 to 0.43), and saturation about 0.7 (0.43 to 0.97). Those two numbers look inconsistent with each other and with the grayscale hint, so colour and brightness are both quite uncertain.

Feedback and motion. The warp is a plain uniform zoom of 1.01 about the screen centre, with no rotation, translation or warp and zoomexp 1. Content drifts outward from the centre by about 1 percent per frame, and the sampling is inward. Decay is 0.98 per frame, which gives a half-life of about 35 frames (about 1.2 s by the recipe's own figure) for bright content. A faint residue at stored level 24 to 25 of 255 never decays further, so a dim background trail can persist. The stated output radius is 0 to 1.147 (nominal). Expect soft expanding trails and echoes radiating from the centre, not sharp shapes. The polygons' centres are stationary, so apparent movement comes from the feedback zoom, the point-cloud sine motion and the mirrored echo. The calibrated flow estimate is low (about 0.05 screen widths per second, interval 0 to 0.16), and frame-to-frame difference is small (about 0.02). It is overall a fairly calm, smooth, hypnotic zoom-tunnel-like look; the recipe's smoothness score is 94 and intensity about 11 (interval 8 to 100).

Audio. The built-in wave follows the left/right waveform directly and continuously, so its dot trace changes shape with the sound, though at near-zero alpha. Bass, mid and treble (attenuated) all feed the shapes' perimeter RGB through clamped or thresholded responses with unknown linear gain. The perimeter colour can therefore shift with the music, but strength, beat sync and whole-screen pulses are not established. The point-cloud layers read time and beat or bass state variables, with held inputs treated as 1 in the recipe. The calibrated estimate gives about 0.4 brightness jumps per 10 s (interval 0 to 4.1), so strobing is not established but a high end cannot be excluded. The calibrated activity percentile is about 0.08, band Chill, with Normal also possible, at low confidence.

Random, chaotic or undefined. No random-per-load branches, random redraws or random textures are listed. Unresolved items: which texture the polygons use and how much of the screen they cover, the colours the dynamic waves actually reach, the final palette, the real visible trail length and the true brightness. The recipe notes that drawn content, audio and native rounding make real frames differ from this description.
