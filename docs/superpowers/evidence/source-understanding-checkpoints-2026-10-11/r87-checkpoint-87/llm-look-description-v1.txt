LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r87 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

The core picture is a dim, soft, organic feedback field with four swarms of small round dots drawn on top of it, plus a very faint audio waveform. The dots are 14-sided polygons (so they read as small discs), about 3% of the screen in radius, in four groups of 366, 408, 262 and 276 copies. They are tiny repeated blobs, not tracked particles. Their centre positions depend on bass, mid and treble through thresholded or clamped expressions, so the swarms jump or shift with the music, and I cannot say where they sit. Each group's opacity also depends on bass, mid and treble, and it is not known how visible they are. A built-in two-trace waveform (mode 7, two parallel lines, additive, grey 0.65) is drawn at the middle of the screen with source alpha of only 0.001. I expect it to be almost invisible. The warp and composite shaders are fluid-like. The composite shows the image through its local slopes (an embossed or relief look), flows along image gradients and steers its own sampling coordinates, so the picture should read as smeared, flowing and shaded rather than crisp. The warp adds an 8-tap spatial blur, a mirror fold across an axis and a radius-dependent swirl, so a kaleidoscope or symmetry candidate is possible. The recipe's own symmetry score is 0.0, so it is only a weak candidate.

Colour is mostly unresolved. The shapes and wave have white or grey source colours. Everything else comes from the shaders, and the recipe lists feedback colours as unknown. The settled-canvas cue is a yellow hue family, with a display colour near (0.08, 0.09, 0.02), which is a dark olive or yellow-green. The recipe marks that level as a lower bound. The composite palette is time-varying: flat canvas levels map to dim tinted greens and yellows, with a slow per-channel shift. The nominal brightness-cycle rate is 0.08 Hz, about a 12.6 s cycle, and whether it shows as a visible flash is unverified. Several variables (q21 to q29 and q31) are redrawn at random, first at about 3.1 s and then every 12.6 s. Any colour or shape that depends on them is a random draw that changes at those moments, and it differs on every load. I do not name one fixed palette. Bass, mid and treble all feed the red, green and blue of the shaders, so colours can shift with the music. How strongly is unknown. No random bundled textures are used.

Feedback and motion: the warp's own colour transfer is linear with gain 0.571 per frame, with no offset and no wrap. Flat content fades to nothing with a half-life of about 1.2 frames, so trails from drawn dots are short. Persistent structure comes from the blur, relief and advection stages rather than from long fades. There is no translation drift (dx = dy = 0). Motion is a bass-driven zoom and rotation about the screen centre, with a zoom that varies with radius and y. A bitwise-and term in those expressions is unsupported, so the zoom and rotation amounts are unresolved and I cannot give a direction or speed. Shape positions move with the audio. The calibrated display estimate puts mean flow at about 0.12 screen widths per second (10th to 90th percentile 0.03 to 0.28) and frame-to-frame change at about 0.046 (0.014 to 0.094), so I expect gentle to moderate movement.

Brightness and mood: the calibrated estimate gives a mean luminance of about 0.19 (interval 0.003 to 0.43), so the picture is dim to medium and likely dark. Saturation is about 0.43 (0.16 to 0.70). It is slightly warm (0.11, interval -0.22 to 0.46). Global brightness jumps are about 0.65 per 10 s (0 to 5.1). Activity is about 0.38 (Normal, but Chill and Intense are both possible). This is a prediction, not a rendered measurement. Because the composite is relief-like, flat areas show one dim level, while texture and edges stand out brighter, so the real picture may look brighter than the flat-level numbers suggest. Main uncertainties: where the dots sit and how visible they are, the actual colours (random per load and over time), the true zoom and rotation, and whether the 12.6 s cycle looks like a flash. The strict model assigns no eligible mood group.
