Expected look: a relief-shaded, embossed-looking texture in muted colour, with a thick colour-cycling ring at the centre and streaks of feedback pulled hard toward the edges. I expect it to be moderately dim to middling and fairly calm. This is a prediction from source facts, and I am uncertain about the overall hue and the pattern scale.

Drawn element. The only drawn element is a built-in circular waveform centred at (0.5, 0.5) with an effective base radius of about 0.20, drawn thick and alpha-over. The right-channel waveform bends it (radial gain 0.4), and it turns at a nominal 0.2 rad/s. Its raw colour oscillates between -0.5 and 0.5 per channel on periods of about 9.4, 10.2 and 11.1 s. The colour is then clamped and normalised so the brightest channel is full. So the ring is vivid and slowly cycles through hues (blue and green most, then red, pink, orange, plus some grey phases).

Feedback and motion. The warp zoom is 1.03 with an extreme radial exponent (zoomexp 13.7). The effective zoom is about 0.2% per step near the centre and rises steeply toward the edges (up to the order of 100% per step at the extreme corner). So the middle is nearly still while the outer area streams outward, and content leaves the screen quickly. Trails are long where the flow is slow, with a half-life of about 3 s (decay 0.9928). The warp shader also does a blur-difference sharpening, which grows patterns from seeds, plus image-driven self-advection and repeat tiling in both plain and mirrored forms. I expect a patterned, tiled, organic texture filling much of the screen, spreading out from the ring.

Composite. A relief (embossing) stage with two directions shows the image through its local slopes. Flat areas show as one dark, slightly blue-grey level, and edges and texture show as lit relief. A grey texture comes out about 0.84 luminance and nearly colourless. Colour therefore comes from the ring and the hue shader, with a random tint chosen at each load. Calibrated mean luminance is about 0.26 (range 0.08 to 0.48), saturation about 0.32, and the coloured share about 49%. Green and pink lead the hue estimate (about 30% and 22%) with blue and purple behind, but the lead is weak and the hue is random per load. The Dark and Bright facets are both not admitted, so overall brightness is middling.

Audio and flashing. Only the waveform shape reacts. No band-driven brightness route exists. Chill is admitted (0.84), flow is low (about 0.07 screen widths per second) and no_strobe_probability is 0.965 with about 0.05 brightness jumps per 10 s, so I expect steady, non-flashing output.
