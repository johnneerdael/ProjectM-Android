LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r117 v1, core recipe view; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture. About five seconds in, I expect a full-screen, organic, self-feeding pattern rather than a few clean drawn objects. The warp stage is a blur-difference feedback (pattern growth, render.feedback / layers shader_warp) with noise-driven and gradient-driven advection and an unmirrored repeat tiling. Small seeds are sharpened and grown into blobs, cells and splatter that spread over much of the canvas, and the picture drifts like fluid or reaction-diffusion texture. Sampling offsets in the warp reach about +/-0.036 of the texture width (shader_advection), so the flow is gentle and local rather than a huge swirl. The mesh warp adds a position-dependent zoom (varying with x and y, about the screen centre), a spatially varying rotation and an angular-periodic term. That should make the texture bend and rotate unevenly across the screen, not as one uniform spin. The whole canvas is then shown through a gradient-relief composite (two directions), so edges and texture look embossed or lit like relief, and flat areas collapse to one dull level. Seeds come from three places. First, a circular built-in wave is centred at (0.5, 0.5), with an effective base radius of about 0.58 model units, which is larger than the screen's half-height. It rotates nominally at 0.2 rad/s and its outline is deformed by the right-channel audio waveform with radial gain 0.4. Second, a small square (shape_0) of radius about 0.049 NDC, roughly 5% of the screen half-size, moves across the screen. Third, a 512-point custom wave (wave_0). Because the circle is so large and the shape and wave positions range well beyond [0,1], much of the seeding may sit at or past the edges. I would not expect a neat centred ring to dominate.

Colour. The recipe's settled-canvas cue (render.feedback.steady_state) has hue family yellow, with display_local_rgb about (0.117, 0.113, 0.094). That is a dim, slightly warm olive-yellow base level (its stated level is a lower bound). The composite multiplies by projectM's cycling corner shades (composite.hue_shader). Red, green and blue each cycle in the range 0.67 to 1.0 with periods of about 14.6 s, 19.6 s and 16.2 s. Four corners blend across the screen, and which hue sits where is random per preset load. So I expect a slowly shifting, spatially varying tint, not one fixed colour. The composite's flat-canvas colour also moves with time (time_varying true; rgb_spread up to about 0.15 in green). The built-in wave's colour runs through nearly the full hue range: closed-form shares over 120 s are green 29%, yellow 19%, blue 14%, orange 14%, red 12%, pink 7% and purple 4%. The channels have periods of 3.9 s (red) and 1.5 s (green). Max-normalisation makes the line's brightest channel 1, so it is fully saturated whenever it shows, and the hue shifts every second or two. The recipe is clear that this is a raw-source ingredient and not a promise of reached colours. Shape_0 is described as a green/cyan-ish source (centre about (0.5, 1.0, 0.9) at alpha 0.2, edge about (0.83, 0.93, 0.8) at alpha 1), so any visible square should be pale mint-cyan. The custom wave's authored colour is white. I expect the overall impression to be moderately to highly saturated and colour-shifting on top of a dim yellowish ground, with no clear dominant hue. The calibrated warm/cool estimate is about neutral (-0.03, interval -0.37 to +0.32), and the mean saturation estimate is about 0.77 (interval 0.49 to 1.0).

Motion. The feedback is a flat-content decay of 0.953 per frame (halving in about 14.5 frames, which the recipe puts at about 0.6 s), so trails and growth structures persist for around half a second to a second and then dim, with a steady floor of about 0.04 of full level. The calibrated display estimate gives mean flow of about 0.29 screen widths per second (interval 0.13 to 0.56) and a mean frame difference of about 0.042 (interval 0.011 to 0.089). I read that as steady, moderately lively drifting and morphing with no hard cuts. The small square sweeps on independent sinusoids (x period 6.28 s, y period 9.67 s, each spanning -0.5 to 1.5). It is therefore off-screen for a good share of each cycle and crosses the picture at up to about 1.19 viewport units per second, which is a ceiling and not a typical speed. The circular wave's nominal rotation is 0.2 rad/s. Global brightness jumps are estimated at 0 per 10 s (interval 0 to about 2.3), so I do not expect strobing.

Brightness and contrast. The display estimate puts mean luminance at about 0.22, but the interval is wide (0.04 to 0.46), so anything from fairly dark to mid-bright is plausible. The steady-state ground is dim (about 0.1). The relief composite produces bright embossed edges against a flat mid-dark surface. I expect an overall medium-dark picture with local bright, high-contrast edge structure and a hazy feel from the blur stage. The built-in wave has constant alpha 0.001, so, if the recipe is read literally, it adds almost nothing to the image. Its mode-specific boost is not quantified, and I cannot say how bright it really is.

Audio response. The only quantified audio routes are four on the mesh warp, all from attenuated bass (bass_att): zoom, rotation, translation x and translation y. Their gains are null in the recipe and are marked threshold-or-clamped, so I cannot give a strength. Qualitatively, a bass-heavy passage should make the whole feedback field breathe, twist and slide, and the effect is smoothed because it uses the attenuated band. The built-in circle's outline follows the right-channel waveform continuously, so it wobbles with the music (gain 0.4). No audio route to colour or brightness is recorded (bass_peak_rgb_effect is 0). I therefore expect motion-driven reactivity and not flashes.

Uncertainty. The recipe's own confidence for the mood layer is low. The strict source model records no eligible mood group, and the activity estimate is Normal (0.40; interval 0.05 to 0.89, so Chill through Intense are all possible). The provisional approximate tags are Psychedelic and Organic / Liquid. Unknown or unresolved items are the following. The mesh-warp zoom, rotation and translation curves are "unknown", so the motion amount is unresolved. Shape_2's size and position are unknown (its radius is about 0, and its second colour has alpha 0), so it probably contributes nothing visible. Wave_0 has mean alpha 0 in the summary even though its authored alpha is 0.6, so whether it draws visibly is unclear. The feedback colours of the warp and composite shaders are listed as unresolved. The yellow steady state is a lower bound and its hue placement is randomised per load. The true on-screen coverage of the wave and shapes is unknown. The picture could be a busy splatter field or a calm haze, depending on how strongly the blur-difference stage amplifies its seeds, and this recipe cannot settle that.
