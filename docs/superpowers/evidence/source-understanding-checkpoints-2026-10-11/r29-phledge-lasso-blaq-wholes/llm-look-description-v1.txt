LLM look description from the SDK recipe, source parts only (before capture, r29 v1; the recipe's engine-measured facets block was deliberately not used):

Core picture: a dense, busy abstract field rather than a few drawn elements. A built-in stereo XY waveform drawn as dots and a custom line waveform supply small seeds. Four polygon-shape layers add to them: 32-sided (33 instances), 12-sided (92), 20-sided (1024, low alpha about 0.34 fill) and tiny triangles (311, radius about 0.01 NDC). These are repeated, moving polygons and points, not proven tracked particles. Two self-advecting shaders (warp and composite) let the image steer its own sampling, so the result should look smeared, flowing and liquid, with an angularly periodic wobble in the feedback mesh.

Feedback and motion: the whole image expands from the screen centre by about 0.5% per frame, giving a slow zoom-out-of-centre flow. The warp transfer on flat content is strongly channel-selective and non-linear: red, green and blue are each nearly inverted or cleared in one step. Trails are therefore not simple fading echoes. Content is repeatedly re-shaped, so the picture is likely churning and high in motion. The calibrated display estimate puts mean flow around 0.6 screen widths per second (interval 0.32 to 1.09) and frame-to-frame change about 0.25.

Colour: the source palette is unresolved. Shapes and waves carry grayscale ingredients, and the steady-state hue cue is grayscale. Audio routes move RGB on the shapes, so colour shifts with the music. The composite maps flat canvas through a gray ramp that lifts black to about 0.11 and saturates near white, which suggests a washed, bright-leaning grey-based look. The estimate gives moderate saturation (about 0.43, interval 0.15 to 0.70) and neutral warm/cool balance.

Brightness: mid-bright overall, mean luminance about 0.39 (interval 0.20 to 0.63), with a lifted black floor.

Audio reaction: bass, mid and treble (raw and attenuated) drive mesh translation in x and y, so the flow field shifts and drifts with the music. Bass, mid and treble also drive shape radius, opacity and RGB, and bass_att drives shape position. The reaction is continuous, not thresholded, and its strength is unquantified.

Uncertain: actual hues, how much of the screen the shapes cover, whether it reads as sharp geometry or soft liquid, and visible trail behaviour. The estimated brightness-jump rate (about 133 per 10 s, very wide interval) may mean visible pulsing or flicker. Activity is estimated Intense, tagged Psychedelic, Geometric and Organic/Liquid, but these are approximate.
