# Audit: origin/main patch series (0001-0035) vs tools/milk-analyzer

Engine checked: origin/main 806d1282, tools/projectm-patches/0001-0035 (rebased on upstream projectM 4.2 master 6f648074, not 4.1.7). The final patched code was read from build/preset-corpus/source34/production-engine, which matches main (Line.cpp has the 0029 cap, MotionUV.hpp from 0034 and the 0035 cache switch are present).

## Two prediction paths

The analyzer has two separate paths, and they differ in how complete they are:

- **Illustration path (I).** `prediction_illustration.py` and `builtin_wave_geometry.py`. Pure Python, used by `facet_precompute.py` at small rasters (`PROBE_SIZE=(160,90)`), while the declared engine context defaults to 1920x1080 (`source_static_behaviour.py:8`). Most mismatches are here.
- **Forecast path (F).** `forecast.py`, `scene_equations.py`, `spatial.py`, `legacy_composite.py`, `builtin_wave.py`, `custom_wave.py`, `primitives.py`, `composite_mesh.py`, `pipeline_fields.py`, `blur.py`. It compiles the patched engine into native adapters built from the source34 tree (`CMakeLists.txt:33` milk-native-reader, `:136` milk-shader-translate, `:195` milk-wave-inputs, which compiles unchanged `Waveforms/*.cpp`, and native_image.cpp). Its policies are selected by engine identity CORE_2331/2334.

Patches skipped as not output-changing:
- 0010: texture lookup lifetime across pack switches.
- 0016: mesh caching; the patch states the output is identical.
- 0035: program-cache diagnostic switch.
